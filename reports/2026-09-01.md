## The big picture this morning

Three things are shaping the whole market on the first trading day of September:

1. **The U.S.–Iran fighting flared up again.** After more than a month of quiet, violence in the Iran war restarted over the weekend. U.S. forces hit rocket launchers on an Iranian island near the Strait of Hormuz (the narrow sea passage where about a fifth of the world's oil normally ships through), and the fear now is that shipping there gets choked off. Because of this, oil prices jumped about 2% this morning (Brent crude, a global oil price benchmark, rose to about $92 a barrel). That's good news for oil and defense (weapons) companies, and it's making investors nervous about nearly everything else.

2. **Technology stocks are falling and futures are lower.** Nasdaq futures (a bet on where the tech-heavy index opens) were down about 1%, with the S&P 500 and Dow each down roughly 0.6%. Big chip and software names — Nvidia (NVDA), AMD, Micron (MU), and Microsoft (MSFT) — were all lower before the open. Higher oil is pushing up interest rates, and higher rates usually hurt expensive tech stocks the most.

3. **September is historically the worst month for stocks**, and traders are coming back from the Labor Day weekend and adjusting their bets. Two big events are coming this week: cybersecurity company **Palo Alto Networks** reports its quarterly results **today after the market closes**, and the government's monthly **jobs report** comes out **Friday**. Both can move the whole market.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Chevron (CVX) — consider buying
- **Price:** around $200, roughly flat early, but the whole oil group is climbing.
- **Why watch it:** When oil prices jump like they did today, big oil companies usually earn more money and their stocks tend to rise. Chevron is one of the largest and steadiest ways to bet on higher oil. The renewed Iran fighting and the threat to the Strait of Hormuz are exactly the kind of news that keeps oil elevated.
- **What to do:** Consider buying if oil keeps climbing and the Middle East stays tense. **The risk:** if the fighting calms down quickly, oil could drop back and pull these stocks down with it. Other oil names moving up on the same news: Exxon Mobil (XOM), Occidental (OXY), and ConocoPhillips (COP).

### 2. RTX Corporation (RTX) — consider buying
- **Price:** rising; the maker of Raytheon missiles and other weapons has been near its highest prices in 12 months.
- **Why watch it:** When countries fight or fear a wider war, governments buy more weapons. Renewed U.S.–Iran combat puts money and attention back on defense contractors. Lockheed Martin (LMT) and Northrop Grumman (NOC) are in the same group and tend to move together.
- **What to do:** Consider buying while the conflict is in the headlines. **The risk:** defense stocks can fall fast if a ceasefire or peace deal shows up, because the "war premium" comes out of the price quickly.

### 3. Palo Alto Networks (PANW) — watch closely; high-reward but risky
- **Price:** trading ahead of its earnings report, due **today after the close**.
- **Why watch it:** Palo Alto is one of the biggest cybersecurity (computer-security) companies, and it reports its latest quarterly results this afternoon. Wall Street expects sales of about $3.35 billion, up 32% from a year ago. A strong report and upbeat guidance could lift not just Palo Alto but the whole cybersecurity group, including CrowdStrike (CRWD) and Zscaler (ZS, which reports Thursday).
- **What to do:** Some people buy a few days before a report like this hoping for good news. But be careful: earnings reports are a coin-flip. If the numbers or the company's forecast disappoint even a little, the stock can drop sharply the next morning. If you don't want that risk, it's safer to wait and see how it reacts **after** today's report before deciding.

### 4. GoPro (GPRO) — be very careful; this is a wild, risky spike
- **Price:** up about 73% before the open, trading above $1.50.
- **Why watch it:** The camera company jumped after popular YouTube creator Markiplier revealed he had bought an 8.5% stake (about $9.3 million), making him the biggest shareholder. On top of that, a lot of traders had bet against the stock (about 16.5% of the shares were sold short), so when it started rising, those bettors were forced to buy back in, pushing it up even faster. A new camera also hits stores tomorrow.
- **What to do:** This is a fast, emotional, crowd-driven spike, not a story about a healthy business. GoPro's sales fell 31% last quarter and the company itself has warned it may not be able to stay in business. If you chase it, only use money you can afford to lose completely, and expect huge swings. **The risk:** a stock that shoots up like this on hype can crash back down just as fast.

### 5. MicroStrategy / Strategy (MSTR) — consider buying if crypto stays hot
- **Price:** volatile; it trades like a supercharged version of Bitcoin.
- **Why watch it:** This company owns an enormous pile of Bitcoin (more than 800,000 coins), so its stock rises and falls with the price of Bitcoin — usually by a lot more in each direction. Bitcoin has been strong in recent weeks (trading around $70,000), and crypto-linked stocks like Coinbase (COIN) tend to move with it.
- **What to do:** Consider buying if Bitcoin keeps climbing and you want a more aggressive way to ride it. **The risk:** it cuts both ways — if Bitcoin drops, this stock usually falls harder than the coin itself. It has still been down for the year overall despite recent bounces.
