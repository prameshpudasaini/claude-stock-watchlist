## Top 5 Picks for This Week

- **What changed this weekend:** Bitcoin tagged ~$79,500–80,000 then **flash-crashed to ~$76,500 in six minutes Saturday**, wiping ~$108B of crypto market cap and triggering **~$1.8B in liquidations** over 24h (longs hit hardest). - **Why it's in play:** COIN and MSTR are the liquid equity proxies for the coin and moved up 4.5%+ Friday. After a +30%-in-5-days run and a weekend washout, Monday's open is a coin flip — either a "buy-the-dip" bounce if BTC holds $76K, or continued unwind if it loses it.
- **Direction:** **Watch, don't chase the open.** Let BTC prove it holds $76K before going long; MSTR moves ~2x the coin both ways — size small. A clean reclaim of $80K is the long trigger; a break of $74–75K flips the whole complex red.

**2. Nvidia (NVDA) — Watch / setup into earnings (Wed 8/26 after close)**
- **Why it's in play:** The single biggest event of the week and the market's AI-trade referendum. Street expects another blowout — revenue near/above the top of guidance, YoY growth still triple-digit, and a Q3 outlook that could point to Nvidia's first **$100B+ revenue quarter**. Blackwell ramp, data-center demand, and gross-margin commentary drive the reaction more than the headline number.
- **Direction:** **Watch / modest long bias into it, but respect the binary.** Semis just took the brunt of the yield-driven selloff, so NVDA sets the tone for the entire complex. Many traders go flat or hedged (SOXL/SOXS or options) before the print and trade the reaction rather than gamble on the gap.

**3. Tesla (TSLA) — Long momentum**
- **Level:** Closed **~$362.86 Friday (+5.1%)**, on pace for its **best week since May** on ~39% above-average volume.
- **Why it's in play:** A string of robotaxi wins — Nevada/Clark County (Las Vegas) permit for up to 5,000 robotaxis, the upcoming Austin **Cybercab** launch event, and a fleet now running in seven U.S. cities with ~2.5M cumulative paid miles. This is a genuine, multi-headline catalyst, not a one-day pop.
- **Direction:** **Long / buy dips** while the robotaxi narrative runs and volume confirms. Watch the $345 area as near-term support; a China-recall headline is the offsetting risk, so keep a stop.

**4. Semis complex via SOXL / SOXS — Watch, trade the NVDA reaction**
- **Why it's in play:** Semiconductors led the week's downside as long-term yields forced selling in growth. With NVDA reporting Wednesday, the group is coiled for a big directional move.
- **Direction:** **Watch.** These are leveraged (3x) instruments — for the NVDA reaction only, not to hold through the print blind. Long SOXL on a strong guide/reclaim of the broken level; SOXS is the hedge/short-side expression if guidance disappoints or yields spike again.

**5. Walmart (WMT) — Watch a failed bounce as a short**
- **Why it's in play:** Still broken after its worst week since 2022 (~-11%) on soft Q3 comps and EPS guidance. Beaten-down mega-cap retail heading into a risk-heavy macro week.
- **Direction:** **Short bias / watch, not a dip-buy.** A weak bounce that fails back under the earnings-gap level is the setup; don't try to catch the falling knife long.
