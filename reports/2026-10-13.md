## What's going on in the market today (the big picture)

- **It's "big bank" day.** Four of America's largest banks reported their quarterly results before the market opened this morning, and that is setting the mood. The results were mixed (details below), and stock futures (early bets on where the market will open) are flat to slightly lower.
- **Computer-chip stocks are falling.** The big names behind artificial intelligence — Nvidia, Broadcom, and Marvell — slipped again this morning. Two things spooked people: a Chinese memory-chip maker said it is catching up fast, and Broadcom's boss warned that paying for all these giant AI projects could get very hard. One chip executive even compared today's AI spending frenzy to a "casino." When the people running these companies sound cautious, investors get nervous.
- **A big inflation report lands tomorrow.** The government's September consumer price report (which shows how fast everyday prices are rising) comes out **Wednesday, October 14**, with a business-price report on **Thursday**. A hot number could rattle expensive tech stocks, because it would make it less likely the Federal Reserve cuts interest rates.
- **Oil is still high.** Brent crude oil is near $106 a barrel on ongoing Middle East tension. That helps oil and gas companies and hurts fuel-heavy businesses like airlines.
- **Bitcoin is steady.** Bitcoin bounced back above $83,000 this morning even as some stock markets wobbled, which is a small sign of strength for crypto-linked stocks.

**The simple takeaway:** The market is a little jittery today — nervous about chips and waiting on tomorrow's inflation number. That means safer, news-driven trades look better than chasing expensive tech right now.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Goldman Sachs (ticker: GS) — consider buying
- **Move:** Up about 3.8% in pre-market trading this morning.
- **Why:** Goldman was the clear winner among the big banks today. Its results easily beat what Wall Street expected, powered by a very strong quarter in its stock-trading business. When a company beats expectations this cleanly, the good feeling often carries the stock up for several days.
- **What to do:** A reasonable **buy** for a short hold. This is a huge, stable company, so it won't swing wildly, but the strong report gives it a clear reason to drift higher this week. **Risk:** if tomorrow's inflation report is bad, even winners can get dragged down with the whole market.

### 2. Bristol-Myers Squibb (ticker: BMY) — consider buying
- **Move:** Up roughly 6% on strong news.
- **Why:** This big drugmaker announced wins in its final-stage drug tests (the last round of testing before a medicine can be approved), including a lung-scarring treatment and positive results in blood cancer and breast cancer. Good test results like these tend to lift a large, steady drug stock for days.
- **What to do:** A solid, lower-drama **buy** for a short hold. It's a large, calm company, so expect a steadier (and smaller) move than a tiny biotech. **Risk:** big drugmakers don't jump much, so the extra upside from here is modest.

### 3. CCC Intelligent Solutions (ticker: CCC) — consider buying
- **Move:** Up about 9–10% recently and holding the gain.
- **Why:** Two big investment firms, GTCR and Elliott, are reportedly in advanced talks to buy the whole company, with a deal possibly coming this week. (CCC makes software for the car-insurance and auto-repair industry.) When a buyer is circling, a stock usually drifts up toward the rumored purchase price and then jumps if a deal is confirmed.
- **What to do:** A reasonable one to **buy and hold a few days** to see if a deal is announced. **Risk:** these are talks, not a signed deal. If negotiations fall apart, the stock can quickly give back the gains.

### 4. IREN Limited (ticker: IREN) — consider buying, higher risk
- **Move:** Jumping this morning on a fresh report.
- **Why:** IREN runs large data centers, and there's a new report that it is close to a cloud-computing deal with the AI company Anthropic (supplying computing power for AI). Demand for AI computing power has been one of the hottest stories of the year.
- **What to do:** A **buy** for people who want a piece of the AI-and-data-center story, holding a few days to see if the deal is confirmed. **Risk:** this is based on a report, not a signed contract, and AI-related stocks are shaky today. If the deal doesn't land, or chip worries spread, IREN can drop fast — it's a volatile stock.

### 5. Nvidia (ticker: NVDA) — be cautious, or a bet-against idea for the brave
- **Move:** Down about 0.6–0.9% in pre-market trading, after chip stocks fell yesterday too.
- **Why:** The whole chip group is under pressure. A Chinese rival is catching up, Broadcom's CEO warned that paying for huge AI projects is getting harder, and one executive called the AI spending boom a "casino." On top of that, tomorrow's inflation report could hit expensive tech stocks the hardest.
- **What to do:** This is **not a great moment to chase Nvidia or other chip stocks.** If you already own it, think about trimming. For experienced, risk-tolerant traders, a fund that rises when chip stocks fall — SOXS — is one way to bet on more weakness over the next few days. **Risk:** Nvidia is still a world-beating company, and any good AI headline can snap it back up quickly, so betting against it is dangerous. Keep any such bet small.
