**4. SpaceX — SPCX — ~+3% to ~$137**
Why it's in play: Broke a four-week losing streak Friday as the post-lockup selling pressure eased, now following through. Space/defense theme remains hot and the technical reset gives a defined entry.
Direction: **Long / watch.** Momentum turn off a washed-out base; risk is that lockup supply isn't fully absorbed.

**5. GameStop — GME — ~+1.5% pre-market**
Why it's in play: Bloomberg reports GME is weighing abandoning its ~$56B bid for eBay. Dropping a stretched, dilutive-looking deal is being read as a positive, and GME is always a retail-momentum vehicle.
Direction: **Watch (long bias on confirmation).** Headline-driven and volatile — size small; this can round-trip on a single follow-up report.
