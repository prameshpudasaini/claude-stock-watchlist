Mixed, slightly cautious open. Futures are hugging the flatline (S&P and Nasdaq futures within a tenth of a percent either way, Russell 2000 modestly red) as two forces cancel out:

- **AI data-center capex is the dominant bull theme.** The single biggest catalyst on the tape — Riot Platforms' $9.1B Anthropic compute lease — is more evidence that hyperscaler/AI-lab spending on power and compute is still accelerating. It's dragging up anything tied to data-center power and storage.

Brent is above **$86** and extended gains for a fourth straight session after Iran's IRGC said it hit two escorted tankers; U.S. SPR inventories have fallen below **300M barrels, the lowest since 1983**. Higher crude is a tax on consumers and an inflation risk right as **key inflation data hits later this week**.

Add a soft July payrolls print (-23k) that keeps the Fed on hold with cuts still on the table, and you get a choppy, headline-driven tape: strong single-stock catalysts working, but the index going nowhere fast into the inflation data. Bitcoin is ~**$64,200**, down ~1.4% from Monday but firming intraday.
