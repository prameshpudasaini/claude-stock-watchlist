# Daily Trading Brief — Sunday, June 7, 2026 (Weekend Edition / Week-Ahead Setup)

**Heads up:** It's Sunday — U.S. cash markets are closed. Every price below references **Friday's
June 5 close** and may move before Monday's open. This brief sets up the trade for the week of
**June 8–12**, the most catalyst-loaded week of the month.

**Where we stand after Friday's wipeout:** The regime is now firmly **risk-OFF**. May payrolls
printed **+172k vs. ~88k expected** (with upward revisions), unemployment steady at 4.3%. A hot labor
market on top of sticky 3.8% inflation **killed the rate-cut story and put a rate HIKE back in play**
— CME FedWatch now shows ~**57% odds of a hike sometime in 2026** (though the June 16–17 FOMC itself
is priced ~98.7% no-change). The 10-year yield jumped to **4.54%**. The result:

- **Nasdaq −4.18%** (25,709) — worst day since the April 2025 tariff shock.

  **NVDA −6%, AVGO −20% (2-day), AMD −11%, MU −13%, MRVL −17%.**
- **Bitcoin fell to ~$61,500** (now ~51% below its Oct 2025 high of $126k), with ~$3B of leveraged
  liquidations over two days and a rare Strategy/MSTR BTC sale adding pressure.
- **VIX stayed oddly contained near ~15–16** — the pain was concentrated in semis/AI/crypto, not yet a
  broad systemic panic.

**Two things drive everything this week:**
1. **Wednesday's May CPI (June 10, 8:30 ET).** April ran hot (3.8% headline / 2.8% core); nowcasts
   point to **3.7–3.9%** for May. With hike odds already elevated, a **hot CPI = more downside**; a
   **cool CPI = a violent relief rally** in beaten-down tech. Don't predict through it — position around it.
2. **The Iran war / oil.** The conflict is still live, ~20% of world oil flows through the **Strait of
   Hormuz**, and **Brent is above $100**. This is the inflation tax behind the hot CPI nowcasts and the
   clearest *market-direction-agnostic* tailwind on the board (energy + defense).

Other dated catalysts: **Apple WWDC Mon June 8** (Siri 2.0 reveal), **Oracle earnings Wed June 10 AMC**,
**ECB decision + Adobe earnings Thu June 11**, **Lennar June 11/12**, plus **Chewy and GameStop** this week.

## Top 5 Picks for the Week

**1. Energy / Oil — XLE (or a major like XOM/CVX) — Long (the cleanest macro tailwind)**
With the Iran war still hot, the **Strait of Hormuz under threat, and Brent above $100**, energy is the
one trade that works whether the broad tape bounces or breaks. It's also a natural hedge against a hot
CPI Wednesday (higher oil = higher inflation = a fresh leg lower in rate-sensitive tech). **Long the
sector; main risk is a sudden Iran de-escalation headline, which would crack crude fast.** Pair with
defense (see "Also Watching") for the same theme.

**2. Apple — AAPL — Long / Watch (binary catalyst: WWDC Monday, June 8)**
The first dated catalyst of the week, and it lands Monday. Apple is expected to unveil an AI-overhauled
**Siri 2.0** — reportedly a 1.2T-parameter system running on a custom Google Gemini model (~$1B/yr), with
a dedicated Siri app and hand-offs to third-party chatbots. Targets are bullish: **Morgan Stanley $330
(bull $440), BofA $380, Melius $385.** As a defensive mega-cap, AAPL also acts as a relative safe-haven
if money keeps fleeing semis. **Long into a constructive Monday, but size small — expectations are high
and this is a real "sell-the-news" risk if Siri underwhelms or ships only in beta.**

**3. SOXS — Long (tactical hedge into CPI / chip-weakness trade only)**
The 3x-inverse semiconductor ETF is the cleanest way to play **continued chip weakness or a hot CPI
print.** After a −10% SOX day a relief bounce is possible, so this is an **event hedge into Wednesday,
not a hold.** Leveraged decay makes it a **1–2 day instrument — hard stops, and flip off it the moment
chips base.** The mirror trade (SOXL) only makes sense *after* CPI clears cool.

**4. Oracle — ORCL — Watch into earnings (Wed June 10, after the close)**
The week's biggest AI read-through. Street wants ~**$1.58 EPS**, total revenue **+19–21%**, cloud
**+46–50%**, against a jaw-dropping **$553B RPO backlog.** Bull case: the backlog converts. Bear case:
**AI capex is blowing out** ($39.2B spent in 9 months vs. $12.1B a year ago, funded with new debt).
Reports **the same day as CPI** — double event risk. **Watch, don't pre-position; let the print clear,
then trade the reaction.**

**5. Taylor Morrison — TMHC — Long toward the deal (lowest-drama setup)**
Popped **~22%** Friday after agreeing to be acquired by **Berkshire Hathaway for $6.8B all-cash** — an
early move under Buffett successor Greg Abel; expected to close 2H 2026. This is a **direction-agnostic
arb**: shares should grind toward the cash price regardless of CPI or the chip rout. **Long for the
spread; main risk is a regulatory/deal hiccup, which looks low here.**
