# Daily Trading Brief — Wednesday, June 10, 2026

**The setup:** The coiled spring snapped the wrong way overnight. Futures are **risk-OFF** —
Nasdaq 100 **-1.6%**, S&P 500 **-1.1%**, with AI-infrastructure names and miners leading the
declines. Two heavy catalysts landed on top of each other this morning.

**1) The Iran war re-escalated overnight (this is the dominant driver).** After a brief
de-escalation last week, the U.S. launched "self-defense strikes" on Iran following the downing of a
**U.S. Army Apache helicopter over the Strait of Hormuz**. Iran retaliated against **U.S. targets in
Jordan, Bahrain, and Kuwait.** The Strait of Hormuz remains effectively closed, oil is back on the
boil, and the tape is rotating hard out of high-beta tech and into **defense and energy**.

**2) CPI came in hot on the headline, cooler on the core (8:30 ET).** Headline **+0.5% m/m, +4.2%
y/y — the hottest in three years**, driven almost entirely by the energy shock (energy +23.5% y/y,
gasoline +40.5%). The silver lining: **core CPI was +0.2% m/m / 2.9% y/y — BELOW the 0.3% forecast**,
so underlying inflation is tamer than the headline screams. The bull argument is that this is a
supply-side oil shock, not broad demand inflation. The bear argument is that 4.2% headline keeps the
Fed frozen (rate-cut hopes are dead, and hike odds are alive).

**The other live event: Oracle (ORCL) reports after today's close** — the marquee AI-capex read,
landing the same day as CPI and the Iran shock. Options imply a **~12% move**. Coming right after
**Broadcom's disappointing AI guide reset the whole group lower last week**, this is the next big test
for a suddenly-shaky AI trade.

Translation: the clean trades today are **long defense, long energy, and hedged/defensive on tech**.
Keep size tight — a single ceasefire headline can unwind the war trade as fast as it built.

The most direct play on the escalation. RTX makes the **missiles, air-defense (Patriot/NASAMS), and
combat systems** that get consumed and re-ordered in exactly this kind of conflict. The stock is up
~57% over the past year on sustained defense demand and is being bid again this morning as Iran and

sudden ceasefire headline — keep a stop, because defense names give back the geopolitical premium
fast when tensions cool.**

**2. Lockheed Martin — LMT — Long (defense blue-chip, lower beta than RTX)**
The other core large-cap defense name — F-35, missiles, missile defense — up ~40% over the past year.
A slightly steadier vehicle than RTX for riding accelerating global military spending into a live
shooting conflict. **Long; same ceasefire risk applies, but LMT's order book and dividend make it the
sleep-at-night version of the trade.**

**3. Energy — XLE / Exxon (XOM) — Long (oil shock is structural while Hormuz is shut)**
Oil is up nearly **40% since the conflict began**, with the **Strait of Hormuz still closed** (it
normally moves ~20% of global crude). With stockpiles draining, the supply squeeze has real legs.
Integrated majors like **XOM** and the **XLE** sector ETF are the cleanest, most liquid way to play
sustained high crude. **Long, but be aware it's extended and crude is the single most ceasefire-
sensitive asset here — this is the highest-reward, highest-headline-risk leg of the basket.**

**4. Oracle — ORCL — Watch into earnings (after close TODAY, do NOT pre-position)**
At **~$212**, down **~13% on the week** into the print. Options imply a **~12% move**. Bull case: the
record **$553B RPO backlog (+325% y/y)** keeps converting and OCI growth stays elevated. Bear case:
after Broadcom's soft AI guide, any sign capex is outrunning cash flow gets punished hard. **Watch,
don't gamble — this is a binary coin-flip stacked on top of CPI and a war. Let it print tonight and
trade the reaction Thursday.**

**5. SQQQ — Long / hedge (the risk-off vehicle for a war + hot-CPI + shaky-AI day)**
The 3x-inverse Nasdaq-100 ETF is the cleanest way to lean into today's risk-off tape: Nasdaq futures
**-1.6%**, AI names broken post-Broadcom, and Oracle event risk tonight. **Long as a hedge or a
tactical short-the-tech trade. Critical: leveraged decay makes this a 1–2 day instrument ONLY — hard
stop, flip off it fast if tech stabilizes or a ceasefire headline rips futures green.**
