**1. Nasdaq-100 Rebalance — effective pre-open Monday | the cleanest dated catalyst**
The June quarterly reconstitution takes effect before Monday's open, forcing the 200+ funds (~$800B AUM) tracking the index to rebalance in one shot.
- **Adds (index-buy tailwind):** Nebius (**NBIS**), Astera Labs (**ALAB**), CoreWeave (**CRWV**), Rocket Lab (**RKLB**), Teradyne (**TER**). NBIS is the standout AI-infra momentum name; CRWV and ALAB ride the same AI-data-center flow.
- **Deletes (forced index selling):** Charter (**CHTR**), Cognizant (**CTSH**), Insmed (**INSM**), Verisk (**VRSK**), Zscaler (**ZS**).
- **How to play it:** Adds tend to run *into* the effective date, then "buy-the-rumor" fade once the one-day flow clears — watch for a Monday pop-and-fade on NBIS/CRWV rather than chasing the open. Deletes can gap down on forced selling and sometimes bounce once flow is done. **Risk: index flow is a single-day event, not a fundamental trend — don't confuse the two.**

**2. Micron (MU) — earnings Wed June 24 after the close | the week's headline print**
The most direct AI-memory/HBM demand proxy and the week's biggest single-stock catalyst. Consensus is for explosive fiscal-Q3 growth — **EPS ~$20.05, revenue ~$35B (+276% YoY)** — with the stock near all-time highs (~$1,145, up from ~$460 a year ago). **The trap: last quarter MU blew out numbers and *still* sold off — the bar is on the reaction, not the print, and a wobble here whips the whole chip/AI complex. Plan the trade before the bell; don't carry full size into a binary.**

**3. Semiconductors (SOXL / INTC, NVDA, MU) — momentum off a record high**
The SOX closed Thursday at a record on the Intel–Apple headline, and Micron's print sits right in the middle of the week. **Intel (INTC)** is the marquee US-reshoring story. The group is extended after a record print. **Long bias while momentum holds, but size for volatility — chips can give back a record-day move fast. Keep SOXS in mind as the hedge, and respect that a soft MU reaction Wednesday can flip the whole group.**

**4. FedEx (FDX) — earnings Tue June 23 | global-trade bellwether**
First report since the **June 1 spinoff of FedEx Freight**, so comps and guidance will be messy — read carefully. Consensus is ~$5.92 EPS / ~$24B revenue, with some analysts looking for an upside beat (~$6.41). It matters beyond FDX: the volume guide moves industrials and transports broadly. **Watch, don't pre-position into the number.**

**5. Energy complex (XLE, USO, oil majors) — short bias on the Iran peace dividend**
With Hormuz reopened and tankers flowing, crude fell again Friday and sits near 3-month lows. Producers stay pressured; the cleaner expression may be the *beneficiaries* — airlines, transports, consumer — that gain from cheaper fuel. **Fade oil-leveraged bounces; favor the demand-side winners. Risk: this is the highest-headline-risk trade on the sheet — any breakdown in the live Switzerland talks snaps crude back violently.**
