## Top 5 Picks for This Week

**1. Reddit (RDDT) — Long / momentum (index-inclusion play)**
Popped ~10–14% Friday after S&P Dow Jones Indices confirmed RDDT joins the **S&P 500 before the Aug 18 open**. Index funds are forced buyers into the inclusion, which often keeps a bid under the stock for a few sessions. The easy pop already happened Friday, so this is a "buy strength, tight stop" trade — if it fails to hold the inclusion gap early next week, the forced-buying thesis is spent. Best window is the first day or two.

**2. SanDisk (SNDK) — Long (memory/storage momentum leader)**
Up ~7% Friday after a bullish 2026 Investor Day long-term outlook. SNDK is the highest-beta name in the red-hot AI memory complex (NAND/HBM demand from hyperscaler data-center buildouts). Momentum is strong but the group is extended — use it as a swing long with a stop under the Investor Day breakout, not a chase at the highs.

**3. Walmart (WMT) — Watch / event (earnings Thursday Aug 20)**
The single most important consumer read of the week. July retail sales just dropped the most in over a year and consumer sentiment is deteriorating, so WMT's guidance is the tell on whether the U.S. consumer is cracking or just trading down (which helps Walmart). Watch — don't pre-position size ahead of the print; trade the reaction. A strong guide is a broad risk-on signal; a weak one hits all of consumer/retail.

**4. Home Depot (HD) — Watch / lean short into the print (earnings Tuesday Aug 18)**
First big-box out of the gate. Housing turnover is soft and the consumer is pulling back on big-ticket/remodel spend, so the setup skews cautious. Watch for a guidance miss; a soft HD number can drag LOW and the housing-adjacent trade. If you want a directional lean, it's watch-for-short into weakness, but respect that rate-cut hopes can cushion housing names.

**5. Micron (MU) — Long (AI memory super-cycle, cheaper cousin to SNDK)**
The anchor of the memory trade and a way to play the same AI-storage tailwind with more liquidity than SNDK. DRAM/HBM pricing and hyperscaler capex are the drivers. Long on pullbacks toward support; the whole memory group moves together, so size for correlation risk with SNDK.
