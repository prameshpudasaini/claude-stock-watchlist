## The big picture this morning (read this first)

The stock market is nervous and slightly lower before the opening bell. Four big things are pushing prices around today, and almost every pick below ties back to one of them:

1. **The Federal Reserve decides on interest rates tomorrow (Wednesday, Sept. 16), and this time it looks like they will *raise* rates, not cut them.** That is unusual and it makes traders cautious. Higher interest rates make borrowing more expensive and are generally bad for expensive technology and fast-growing companies. Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh will explain the decision in a press conference tomorrow afternoon, so today most big investors are sitting on their hands and waiting.

2. **A loud warning about AI is still shaking things up.** A few days ago, the CEO of the AI company Anthropic (Dario Amodei) published an essay saying companies should *slow down* the race to build ever-more-powerful AI for safety reasons, and OpenAI's Sam Altman agreed. Traders took this as a sign that the giant spending on AI computer chips might cool off. So money has been flowing *out* of chip makers and *into* cybersecurity companies (the firms that keep computer systems safe). People are calling this the "AI worry trade."

3. **Oil is still expensive.** Brent crude oil (the world's main price benchmark) is around $107 a barrel after Saudi Arabia was forced to shut a major pipeline following attacks, and tensions with Iran in the Middle East got worse. Some analysts are warning oil could go much higher. Expensive oil helps oil-company stocks but hurts almost everyone else.

4. **The interest rate on U.S. government bonds jumped to about 5%, the highest in many years.** When you can earn 5% safely from a government bond, expensive tech stocks look less attractive by comparison. This is another reason the tech-heavy Nasdaq is soft.

Bottom line: money is moving *out* of computer-chip and expensive-tech names and *into* oil, cybersecurity, and safer corners of the market. Bitcoin is weak (around $78,600, stuck below $80,000). Expect a choppy, careful day until the Fed speaks tomorrow.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. CrowdStrike (ticker: CRWD) — jumped about 14–15% Monday to roughly $237. Consider buying, but you're paying up.**
CrowdStrike is a cybersecurity company — it protects computer systems and networks from hackers. It is the biggest winner of the "AI worry trade": with everyone suddenly nervous that AI is moving too fast and could be dangerous, money is pouring into companies that keep systems safe. CrowdStrike's own CEO even argued that the answer is to make AI *safer*, not slower — and that plays right into his company's business. If this AI-safety story stays in the headlines through the week, CrowdStrike and its peers can keep climbing. **The catch:** the stock already leaped 15% in one day, so you'd be buying after a big pop, and if the whole market sinks on the Fed news, even winners get pulled down. Keep the size small.

**2. Diamondback Energy (ticker: FANG) — a way to profit from expensive oil. Consider buying while oil stays high.**
Oil is sitting near $107 a barrel because of the Saudi pipeline shutdown and Middle East tensions, and oil producers make more money when oil is expensive. Diamondback is one of the strongest U.S. oil drillers. If you'd rather spread your bet across many energy companies instead of picking one, you can buy the **Energy Select Sector SPDR Fund (ticker: XLE)**, which is a basket of big oil and gas stocks. **The risk:** if the Middle East situation calms down, oil can fall back just as fast, and these stocks would drop with it. This is a "ride it while the news is hot" trade, not a forever hold.

**3. Betting against computer chips (ticker: SOXS) — for people who think the chip slide keeps going.**
This one is only for people who believe chip stocks have further to fall this week. SOXS is a fund built to go *up* when semiconductor (computer-chip) stocks go *down*, and it moves about three times as fast as the chip group, in the opposite direction. Chips have been hit hard by the "slow down AI" fears (Intel fell about 7%, AMD about 4–6%, Broadcom about 5% on Monday), and a possible rate hike tomorrow could keep the pressure on. **Big warning:** these "3x" funds are turbo-charged and lose value quickly if you're wrong or if you hold them too long — and chip stocks were actually ticking slightly *higher* before the bell this morning, which is exactly the kind of bounce that can burn you. This is a short-leash, few-days trade only, not a buy-and-forget.

**4. Palo Alto Networks (ticker: PANW) — up about 13% Monday to roughly $373. Consider buying alongside CrowdStrike.**
Palo Alto is another big cybersecurity company riding the same "AI worry trade" as CrowdStrike. It jumped hard on Monday along with Zscaler, Fortinet, Okta, and Qualys. Buying two cybersecurity names instead of one spreads your bet a little if you believe this theme has more room to run this week. **The risk is the same as CrowdStrike's:** these stocks already moved a lot in one day, and a rough reaction to tomorrow's Fed decision could drag the whole group down for a day or two.

**5. Circle Internet Group (ticker: CRCL) — down about 5% before the bell. This could be one to bet against if the nervous mood continues.**
Circle is a crypto-linked company (it runs a popular digital dollar called a stablecoin). With Bitcoin stuck below $80,000, the safe-bond interest rate near 5%, and big investors pulling money out of risky bets before the Fed decision, crypto-related stocks are under pressure. If the cautious, risk-off mood holds into tomorrow, names like this can keep sliding. **The catch:** crypto stocks are jumpy and can snap back fast on any good Bitcoin headline, so if you bet against it, keep it small and short. A calmer, safer choice is simply to *avoid* crypto-linked names until after the Fed speaks.
