If the deal doesn't land, or if AI stocks wobble on this week's inflation data, IREN could drop fast — it's a volatile stock.

### 5. Salesforce (ticker: CRM) — consider buying

- **Why:** Bank of America raised its rating on the stock (told clients to buy it). Salesforce makes business software and has been pushing AI tools; an upgrade from a big bank often gives a stock a short-term lift.
