# Short-Term Stock Watchlist — September 24, 2026

*A plain-English guide to stocks worth watching today, for a trade you'd hold anywhere from 1 to 14 days. This is research and general information, not personal financial advice. Short-term trading is risky and you can lose money fast — never bet more than you can afford to lose.*

## The mood of the market this morning

Stocks are heading lower again. The big story is the bond market: the interest rate the U.S. government pays to borrow money for 10 years jumped to about 5.1%, its highest since 2007, and the 30-year rate is the highest since 2004. When these rates climb, it makes safe bonds more attractive and makes expensive stocks (especially tech) look less appealing, so money flows out of stocks.

A few things are driving the day:

- **Inflation worries are back.** Because prices and borrowing costs are rising, traders now think there's a 71% chance the Federal Reserve (the U.S. central bank that sets interest rates) raises rates *again* next month — up from about 55% just a day earlier. The Fed already raised rates once this month to a 3.75%–4% range, its first increase since 2023.
- **Oil is spiking.** A barrel of Brent crude (the main global oil price) pushed above $105 because talks between the U.S. and Iran went nowhere. Expensive oil helps energy companies but hurts almost everyone else.
- **A big diplomatic day.** China's leader Xi Jinping is in Washington, with a Trump–Xi meeting expected tomorrow. Any surprise — good or bad — on trade could swing the whole market.
- **Tech and chips are under pressure** from those rising rates, while a few pockets — quantum computing and energy — are going the other way.

In plain terms: this is a nervous, "sell first, ask later" kind of morning. Be picky and keep positions small.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Occidental Petroleum (ticker: OXY) — riding the oil spike
**What's happening:** Oil prices are jumping because U.S.–Iran talks broke down and there are worries about supply. Occidental is a big U.S. oil producer, so higher oil prices flow straight into its profits. On top of that, a well-known Wall Street research firm (Evercore) recently made a rare "double upgrade," lifting the stock all the way from a sell-type rating to a buy-type rating with a $65 target, praising the company's cleaned-up finances and stronger cash flow.
**Why watch it today:** When oil rises on conflict fears, oil stocks usually rise with it, and this one has fresh analyst support behind it.
**What to consider:** Consider buying if oil keeps climbing and the Iran standoff stays tense. The risk: if the U.S. and Iran suddenly reach a deal, oil could drop fast and take these stocks down with it. Warren Buffett's Berkshire Hathaway is a big Occidental shareholder, which some see as a vote of confidence.

### 2. IonQ (ticker: IONQ) — the quantum computing story still running hot
**What's happening:** IonQ (a leader in quantum computing, a new kind of ultra-powerful computer) had two pieces of good news this week: it announced a technical breakthrough in fixing computing errors, and it said one of its machines will be installed and connected directly to Nvidia's chips at a new Nvidia research center. The stock climbed to about $45.
**Why watch it today:** Exciting news like this often keeps a stock moving for several days, and the whole quantum group (Rigetti, D-Wave) is rising along with it.
**What to consider:** Consider buying if it keeps going up, but keep it small. These companies make very little money today, so the stock is running on hope and can drop just as sharply as it rises — especially on a jittery day like this one.

### 3. Worthington Enterprises (ticker: WOR) — a quiet winner from the AI build-out
**What's happening:** Worthington makes metal products, and one of its lines — special tanks used to cool the giant computer warehouses (data centers) that power artificial intelligence — is booming. It reported quarterly results that beat expectations, revenue up about 13%, and cash flow that nearly doubled. Management said this cooling business could eventually be 10 times bigger than its older business. The stock jumped roughly 15%.
**Why watch it today:** This is a way to play the AI boom that isn't a pricey, well-known tech name, so it's less exposed to the rising-rates pain hitting big tech. Strong earnings moves often carry on for a few days.
**What to consider:** Consider buying if it holds its gains or keeps rising after the earnings pop. The risk: the stock already made a big one-day jump, so it could cool off (no pun intended) if the overall market keeps sliding.

### 4. Coinbase (ticker: COIN) — be careful here today
**What's happening:** Coinbase is the largest U.S. crypto trading exchange, and its stock closely follows Bitcoin. Bitcoin had a great run recently (up near $85,000) but slipped about 3% today as nervous investors pulled back from riskier bets. Coinbase trades around $192.
**Why watch it today:** Coinbase tends to move in the same direction as Bitcoin, only more dramatically — up more on good days, down more on bad ones.
**What to consider:** On a risk-off day with rising interest rates, this is one to be cautious with. If Bitcoin keeps sliding, Coinbase could fall harder, so this is a candidate to *bet against* (or simply avoid) rather than buy — unless Bitcoin turns back up, in which case it can bounce quickly. Watch Bitcoin's price as your guide.

### 5. GRAIL (ticker: GRAL) — the big vote already happened; now it's a waiting game
**What's happening:** Yesterday, a panel of outside advisers to the FDA (the agency that approves medical products) voted in GRAIL's favor on its "Galleri" blood test that screens for many cancers at once — 10-0 that it's safe, 6-4 that it works, and 7-2 that the benefits outweigh the risks. The stock had already jumped hard this week ahead of the vote.
**Why watch it today:** The news is good, but the actual FDA yes-or-no decision isn't expected until early 2027, so the near-term excitement may have mostly played out.
**What to consider:** Because the big event has passed and the real decision is far away, chasing it here is risky — the stock could drift back down as traders who bet on the vote take their profits. Watch how it trades today; a calm hold above this week's jump would be a healthier sign than a sharp fade.
