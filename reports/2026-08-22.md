## Top 5 Picks for This Week

**1. Nvidia (NVDA) — Watch / setup into earnings (Wed 8/26 after close)**
- **Why it's in play:** The single biggest event of the week and the whole market's AI-trade referendum. Street expects another blowout — revenue slightly above the top end of guidance (~$93–95B), **YoY growth north of 100%**, and a Q3 outlook that could herald Nvidia's first-ever **$100B+ revenue quarter**. Blackwell ramp and data-center demand are the numbers to watch; guidance and gross-margin commentary drive the reaction more than the print.
- **Direction:** **Watch / long bias into it, but respect the binary.** Build or hold a position before Wednesday only if you can stomach a gap either way; many traders go flat or hedged (via SOXL/SOXS or options) before the print and trade the reaction. The move sets the tone for the entire semi/AI complex.

**2. Crypto complex — Coinbase (COIN) / Strategy (MSTR) — Long momentum**
- **Level:** Bitcoin ripped to **~$78,300 Friday (+7.3% on the day, +22% on the week)** — its strongest weekly run since 2024 — and tagged ~$80K overnight before easing. ETH ~$2,515, XRP ~$1.45. Spot BTC ETFs pulled in $606M Thursday (BlackRock took 83%).
- **Why it's in play:** A weaker dollar, friendlier Washington regulatory tone, and institutional ETF inflows have BTC breaking out of its bear market. COIN is the cleanest liquid proxy; MSTR is the highest-beta treasury play.
- **Direction:** **Long momentum, tied to the coin.** If BTC stalls under $80K or the dollar bounces on a hawkish Warsh, tighten stops — MSTR moves ~2x the coin both ways, so size small.

**3. Moderna (MRNA) / mRNA-oncology theme — Long momentum, manage risk hard**
- **Level:** MRNA closed ~$174 (+10% Friday), after an epic **+177% Wednesday** on the Moderna/Merck cancer-vaccine Phase 3 win.
- **Why it's in play:** Their mRNA vaccine (intismeran autogene) met primary and key secondary endpoints in the INTerpath-001 melanoma trial — the **first-ever positive Phase 3 for an mRNA cancer therapy.** The read-through lifted the whole group: BioNTech +22%, Arcturus +25%, Novavax +11%, Merck to a record high.
- **Direction:** **Long / watch for follow-through,** but this is a parabolic move — a stock up ~200% in three days can give back a chunk fast. Trade it, don't marry it; size small and use stops.

**4. Palo Alto Networks (PANW) — Watch / earnings (Mon 8/24)**
- **Why it's in play:** Kicks off the week's mega-cap software/cyber earnings before CrowdStrike (Wed). A clean read on enterprise security and AI-security spend heading into the NVDA-week tape.
- **Direction:** **Watch.** Guidance and next-gen-security ARR are the tells; a hold-and-go above the earnings gap is the setup — don't chase the first print.

**5. Merck (MRK) — Long / lower-beta way to play the cancer-vaccine win**
- **Why it's in play:** Hit a **record high Friday** (+~13% on the week) as the co-owner of the Moderna cancer-vaccine data plus a Keytruda tailwind — and it led the Dow's healthcare-driven rally. Far less volatile than MRNA for the same catalyst.
- **Direction:** **Long / buy dips.** The cleaner, calmer expression of the mRNA-oncology theme with a real defensive bid behind it.
