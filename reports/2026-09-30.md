# Short-Term Stock Watchlist — Wednesday, September 30, 2026

*A plain-English guide to today's best short-term trading ideas (hold roughly 1 to 14 days). This is research and opinion, not financial advice. Short-term trading is risky — never bet money you can't afford to lose.*

## What's driving the whole market today

Good news this morning, and it's a big relief after the scare of the last few days. The government's main inflation report (called the PCE price index — it measures how fast prices are rising) came in **cooler than expected**. In plain words: prices are climbing a little slower than people feared. That matters a lot right now because just yesterday traders were terrified the Federal Reserve (the U.S. central bank that sets interest rates) might *raise* interest rates in October — which usually hurts stocks. Today's soft inflation number takes some of that fear off the table.

The result: the interest rate on 10-year U.S. government bonds, which had spiked to a 19-year high, pulled back a bit today, and the main stock indexes are pointing slightly higher before the opening bell (up about 0.2%). It's the last trading day of September, so expect some end-of-month position juggling too.

Two other things are helping the mood:
1. **Computer-chip stocks are hot.** Micron, a major memory-chip maker, reported blowout results last night (see pick #1). That's giving the whole chip and AI world a lift this morning.
2. **The fear trade is calming.** With rate-hike worries easing, money is flowing back toward stocks — though the flip side is that safe-haven and crypto bets are cooling off (Bitcoin and crypto-linked stocks are down today).

The simple takeaway: yesterday's storm cloud (fear of a rate hike) has thinned out for now. That's a friendlier backdrop for stocks, especially chips and companies with their own good news today. Just remember one soft inflation report doesn't end the story — Friday still brings the big monthly jobs report, which can shake things up again.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Micron Technology (MU) — consider buying
This is the headline of the day. Micron makes memory chips (the parts that store data), which are in massive demand for artificial intelligence. Last night the company crushed its quarterly results: sales of about **$41.5 billion** (way above the roughly $35.7 billion expected) and profit far above forecasts. Even better, management said its most advanced AI memory chips are **fully sold out through 2027**, with demand reaching into 2028, and it has already locked in $22 billion of customer agreements including $18 billion in cash deposits. The stock jumped about **15% after the results came out**. **Consider buying** — this is a real, powerful signal that the AI chip boom is still going strong, and it tends to lift other chip names too. **The risk:** the stock already leapt a lot on the news, so it could give back some of that jump in the first day or two; don't chase it if it spikes hard at the open, and remember expensive tech stocks fall fastest if the market mood sours again.

### 2. Capricor Therapeutics (CAPR) — consider buying (higher risk)
Capricor is a small drug company working on a treatment (called deramiocel) for Duchenne muscular dystrophy, a serious muscle-wasting disease in children. It just presented strong late-stage trial data showing the drug meaningfully slowed the loss of arm and hand function versus a dummy treatment, and that data is now part of its application to the FDA. Wall Street reacted fast: two firms upgraded the stock to "Buy," with one lifting its price target all the way from $5 to $21 and another from $2 to $25. The shares jumped roughly **15% to 30%**. **Consider buying** for those comfortable with risk — there's a clear catalyst coming: the FDA is due to decide on the drug by **November 22**, and excitement usually builds into a date like that. **The risk:** this is a make-or-break FDA decision — if the answer in November is "no," the stock could drop sharply, so treat this as a speculative bet and keep it small.

### 3. Boeing (BA) — consider buying
Boeing just won a huge government contract: about **$20 billion** to build the U.S. Navy's next-generation fighter jet (the F/A-XX). A contract this size means years of guaranteed work and is a strong vote of confidence in the company. The stock rose about **2% to 3%** this morning. **Consider buying** — big, multi-year defense contracts give a stock a steady tailwind, and Boeing has been a company investors wanted to see win something like this. **The risk:** the move so far is modest, and Boeing has a long history of production delays and cost overruns, so this is a slower, steadier idea rather than a quick pop.

### 4. Moderna (MRNA) — a good one to bet against
Moderna, the vaccine maker, had a strong run recently that pushed its stock to a new 52-week high near $209. This morning Citi (a big bank) turned negative on it, cutting its rating all the way to **"Sell"** and warning that the price had simply climbed too far, too fast. The stock fell about **6% before the open**. **This could be a good one to bet against (bet that it keeps falling)** as investors react to the downgrade and the "it got too expensive" argument. **The risk:** it has already dropped on the news, and beaten-down stocks can bounce back for a day or two, so don't assume it falls in a straight line — and any surprise good news on Moderna's drugs could flip the story quickly.

### 5. Robinhood (HOOD) — consider buying
Robinhood, the popular stock-trading app, announced a batch of new features: an AI helper built into the app, round-the-clock trading for certain stocks, and a new type of product for more active traders. The stock rose about **2%** this morning. **Consider buying** — new features that keep users trading more can grow the company's revenue, and this is exactly the kind of fast-moving name that runs higher when the market mood improves, like it is today. **The risk:** Robinhood is a "risk-on" stock, meaning it swings up hard on good days but falls hard when investors get scared — so a bad jobs report Friday could hit it quickly.

It jumped about 13%. A clear beat like this can carry a stock for several days. Risk: it's already up a lot, and cruise stocks are sensitive to worries about the economy.
2. **Bloom Energy (BE) — consider buying, but it's jumpy.** This clean-energy (fuel cell) company rose about 11% on higher price targets from analysts tied to its new Fremont factory, and it's up roughly 38% over the past month. Momentum is clearly on its side. Risk: a stock that's run up this fast can pull back hard on any disappointment, so keep positions small.
3. **Lululemon (LULU) — a good one to avoid or bet against.** The athletic-wear maker just cut its sales forecast for the *third* time this year and sank to an 8-year low, with sales actually shrinking. When a company keeps lowering its own outlook, the stock often keeps sliding. Risk: it's already fallen a long way, so a short-term bounce is possible even if the long-term picture is weak.
4. **Nvidia (NVDA) and other chip stocks — consider buying if the group stays strong.** Micron's blowout results (pick #1) are lifting the whole chip and AI group this morning. Nvidia is the biggest name that benefits from the "AI demand is still booming" message. Risk: expensive tech stocks are the first to drop if interest-rate fears come roaring back.
5. **Coinbase (COIN) and Strategy/MicroStrategy (MSTR) — watch for weakness, don't rush in.** These move with Bitcoin, which is pulling back today as money shifts away from risky bets. ### Quick reminder on risk
Today's mood is calmer and friendlier than yesterday's, thanks to a softer inflation report that cooled fears of a rate hike — and Micron's huge earnings gave the chip world a boost. But this is still a jumpy market: one good report doesn't erase the bigger worry about interest rates, and Friday's monthly jobs report can move everything fast. Stick to stocks with their own clear news (like Micron, Boeing, and Capricor today), keep positions small, decide your exit price before you buy, and be careful with expensive tech and risky crypto bets that can reverse in a hurry.
