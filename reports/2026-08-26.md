## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. Nvidia (NVDA) — around $213 — WAIT and watch, don't gamble on tonight's report**
- **Why it matters today:** Nvidia is the most important company in the artificial-intelligence boom, and tonight it tells everyone how much money it made and — more importantly — what it expects going forward. Wall Street expects about **$92 billion in sales** (nearly double a year ago) and profit of about **$2.09 per share**, with a hopeful whisper of $2.16. The stock has fallen seven days in a row heading into this.
- **The honest truth:** This is a coin flip. The options market (traders betting on how much it moves) expects the stock to swing about **6% up or down** the next morning — that's roughly $280 billion in value changing hands in one move. If the outlook is strong, it flies; if it's merely "good," it can still drop because expectations are sky-high.
- **What to do:** **Don't bet your money before the report — that's gambling, not investing.** The smarter play is to wait until tomorrow morning, see the reaction, and then decide. If the results are strong and the stock is climbing, that's your signal it could be worth buying for a short run. If the outlook disappoints and it's falling, stay away.

**2. Semiconductor funds SOXL (bet up) and SOXS (bet down) — a way to play the Nvidia reaction**
- **What these are:** These are special funds that move about **three times** as much as the group of chip stocks each day. SOXL goes up when chips go up (and down when they fall); SOXS does the opposite. A 1% Nvidia move turns into roughly a 3% move in these.
- **Why watch today:** All chip stocks are coiled up waiting for Nvidia. After the report, the whole group will make a big, fast move in one direction.
- **What to do:** These are **fast and dangerous — for a very short bet only, not to hold blindly.** If Nvidia's report is strong and chips are rising tomorrow, SOXL is the aggressive way to ride it up. If Nvidia disappoints and chips are falling, SOXS is how you bet against the group. Because they multiply moves by three, only risk a small amount and don't hold them for long.

**3. Coinbase (COIN) and Strategy (MSTR) — consider buying carefully while Bitcoin recovers**
- **Where things stand:** Bitcoin crashed hard over the weekend, then climbed back near $80,000 on Tuesday before easing to about **$78,700** today. These two stocks are the easiest way to bet on the price of Bitcoin through the regular stock market. Both have already jumped roughly 25–35% over the past week. Wall Street just raised its targets: Goldman lifted Coinbase to **$196**, and another firm lifted Strategy to **$175**.
- **What to do:** **Consider buying if Bitcoin keeps holding above roughly $76,000–78,000** — the recovery from the crash is real and analysts are getting more positive. But know that Strategy moves about twice as much as Bitcoin itself in both directions, so it's the riskier of the two. If Bitcoin falls back below the mid-$70,000s, step aside; these stocks would fall fast with it.

**4. Abercrombie & Fitch (ANF) — around $95, up about 12% today — a genuine winner, but don't chase too hard**
- **Why it's moving:** The clothing company reported much better results than expected this morning — profit of **$4.17 per share** versus about $1.99 expected — and raised its forecast for the rest of the year. It just posted its 15th straight quarter of growth.
- **What to do:** **This is a real, news-driven jump, so it's worth considering for a short hold.** The risk with buying a stock that already popped 12% is that some traders sell into the good news and it drifts back down during the day. A calmer approach: buy a smaller amount, and add more only if it stays strong over the next day or two rather than fading.

**5. Semtech (SMTC) — up about 5–9% — consider buying on the AI data-center story**
- **Why it's moving:** This chip company reported record results, and the standout was its data-center business — sales there jumped **91%** from a year ago to $100 million as AI computer demand keeps growing. It also gave a strong forecast that beat expectations by a wide margin.
- **What to do:** **Consider buying if it keeps going up.** It's riding the same AI wave as Nvidia, and its own results already prove the demand is real. Be aware that if Nvidia disappoints tonight, Semtech could get dragged down with the rest of the chip group tomorrow regardless of its own good news — so keep the position small until after Nvidia reports.
