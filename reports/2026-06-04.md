# Daily Trading Brief — Thursday, June 4, 2026

**Backdrop:** Risk-off, tech-led. Nasdaq futures are down ~1.4% and S&P futures ~0.5%
after two marquee earnings reports soured AI sentiment overnight: **Broadcom (AVGO −15%)**
guided AI chip sales *below* the Street and refused to raise its 2026 AI forecast, and

## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. Broadcom — AVGO — Watch for Short / fade bounces**
Down **15% premarket**. The miss wasn't the numbers (AI revenue +143% YoY to $10.8B, EPS beat)
— it was the **guidance**: Q3 AI chip sales guided to ~$16B vs. ~$17.2B expected, and CEO Hock
Tan declined to lift the full-year AI forecast. After a huge run, "great-but-not-better" broke
the momentum. Don't catch the knife on the open; let it find a level. Bias is to fade weak
rallies until it stabilizes, not to buy the gap.

**2. CrowdStrike — CRWD — Watch for Short**
Down **~11% premarket**, on track for its worst day in 22 months. It actually beat (EPS $1.10
vs. $1.07, rev $1.39B) and announced a **4-for-1 split**, but the net-new ARR beat was smaller
than usual and the stock had ripped **97% since April 10**. Classic "priced for perfection"
unwind. Same playbook as AVGO — respect the broken momentum, don't bottom-fish the first hour.

**3. Nvidia — NVDA — Watch (long only once the dip holds)**
~$224 and getting dragged lower in sympathy with AVGO this morning. But the read-through is
nuanced: AVGO's softness was *custom-ASIC* guidance, not a demand collapse, and NVDA's Blackwell
ramp and active upgrade cycle are intact. This is the highest-quality name to *buy on
stabilization* — wait for the AI complex to find a floor (ideally post-jobs-report), then go long.
Don't be early.

**4. SQQQ — Long (tactical hedge only)**
With Nasdaq futures −1.4% on AI earnings + Iran risk + a jobs print tomorrow, the 3x-inverse
Nasdaq ETF is today's clean macro hedge. Pure event trade — leveraged decay makes this a
**1–2 day instrument, not a hold**. Take the hedge off if/when chips stabilize.

**5. Micron — MU — Watch**
~$1,070 and freshly in the $1T club, but vulnerable to a sympathy flush as the whole AI-chip
group de-risks off AVGO. Fundamentals are still strong (CFO: "demand far exceeds supply" beyond
2026) and **Q3 earnings on June 24** is the next real catalyst. Watch for support to hold; a
washout into a defended level is a better entry than chasing here.
