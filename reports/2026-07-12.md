The chip tape is the market's fault line and **ASML (Wed) + TSMC (Thu)** are the referees on whether the "AI peak-demand" call sticks. Play: **SOXL** for a capitulation bounce, **SOXS** if ASML bookings disappoint — but wait for the prints, don't pre-load a 3x fund. Risk: leveraged decay; day-to-few-day trades only.

**4. Coinbase — COIN — Watch (lean short/defensive)**
Crypto has flipped risk-off: **Bitcoin slid below ~$66,000** on ETF outflows and profit-taking, and the **Coinbase premium has been negative for 50 straight days** — a sign of soft U.S. demand. COIN and MSTR are the high-beta expressions of that. Play: watch for a failed bounce to fade, or wait for BTC to reclaim support before touching longs. Risk: crypto turns on a headline; a sudden BTC reclaim squeezes shorts hard.

**5. Taiwan Semiconductor — TSM — Watch / long into Thursday**
The single cleanest read on "does the AI buildout have a ceiling." Analysts are broadly bullish into the **Thursday July 16** print (multiple "buy before earnings" calls), and hyperscaler capex is still running hot. Play: watch for a pre-earnings drift/base, or use the report as the fresh catalyst. Risk: after a $1T group selloff, a merely-in-line guide could still sell off; this is the whole AI trade's referee, so the move will be large either way.
