**1. Energy complex — XOM / COP / VLO (or XLE) — LONG (macro momentum)**
Oil is the cleanest trend in the market right now: a sixth day of U.S.–Iran fighting, Iran striking a Kuwaiti desalination plant, and renewed **Strait of Hormuz** disruption fears pushed WTI to a one-month high (**$82.47, +4.46%**) and Brent to **$86**. Risk: a sudden de-escalation headline (there was a June truce that cratered oil) reverses this fast — keep a stop and don't chase a gap.**

**2. Defense — LMT / RTX / NOC — LONG / WATCH (geopolitics + earnings)**

**3. Alphabet — GOOGL — WATCH (the AI trade's referee, reports Wed Jul 22)**
GOOGL (~+10% YTD, more resilient than the chip names) is the single most important report of the week. The Street wants **~23–24% EPS/revenue growth**, continued **Google Cloud** strength (last quarter grew 62.7% y/y), and above all **higher 2027 capex guidance**. In a week where Kimi K3 has investors questioning AI spend, GOOGL's capex line is the tell for whether hyperscaler demand — and the chip names — stabilize. **Watch into Wednesday; this print sets the tone for the whole complex. Defined-risk only through the number.**

**4. Tesla — TSLA — WATCH (binary earnings, reports Wed Jul 22)**
TSLA is **down ~15% YTD** into the print. Estimates have crept higher (EPS +22% y/y, revenue +12%), but as always **automotive gross margin** dictates the reaction, layered on robotaxi/FSD progress. Expectations are middling, which cuts both ways. **Watch the event; don't hold size naked through the number — Tesla routinely gaps 8–12% on earnings.**

**5. Semiconductors — SOXX / NVDA / AMD — WATCH (short-bias, or fade the bounce)**

With $3.3T erased since June 22 and the Kimi K3 overhang sitting directly in front of Alphabet's capex guide, the trend favors the bears near-term. **For aggressive traders, SOXS/SQQQ is the momentum vehicle (leveraged/inverse — strictly 1–3 day, hard stop, decay eats you).
