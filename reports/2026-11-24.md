# Stock Watchlist — Tuesday, November 24, 2026

**The big picture today:** Yesterday (Monday) the stock market had one of its best days of the year. The Nasdaq (the index full of tech and chip companies) jumped about 6.5% and the S&P 500 rose about 4.7% — the strongest single day since spring 2025. The reason was Nvidia: its board brought back Jensen Huang as chief executive after he had stepped down earlier this month, and that calmed everyone's fears about the company that sits at the center of the whole artificial-intelligence (AI) boom.

Today the mood has flipped a little. Stock futures (early bets on where the market will open) are pointing *down* this morning — tech futures are off more than 1.5%. In plain words: after a huge one-day jump, a lot of traders are cashing in their quick wins and catching their breath. On top of that, the interest rate on 10-year U.S. government bonds is sitting high at around 4.95%, which makes risky stocks less attractive, and the Federal Reserve (the people who set interest rates) may still raise rates at its December meeting.

**What this means for you:** Be careful chasing anything that already shot up yesterday — a lot of the easy money was made Monday, and today is a "give some back" kind of day. The better, cleaner stories today are the companies moving on their *own* news: a big drug-company buyout and a batch of store-chain earnings reports (quarterly results).

---

## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. Insmed (INSM) — up about 14% this morning (trading near $170)**
This is the cleanest story of the day. Sanofi, a giant French drug maker, agreed to buy Insmed for $175 per share in cash — a deal worth about $41 billion. That's the biggest drug-company takeover in years. Because it's an all-cash deal, the price is basically locked: Insmed shares should drift up toward $175 and mostly stop there. **What to do:** The big one-day jump already happened. If you buy now near $170, you're really betting the deal closes and you collect the last few dollars up to $175 — a small, fairly safe gain, but small. This is a low-risk, low-reward "park it and wait" idea, not a home run. The main risk is if government regulators block the deal, which would send the stock back down.

**2. Nvidia (NVDA) — down about 4% this morning (near $158 after closing around $165)**
Nvidia is the most important stock in the market right now. It soared about 31% yesterday on the news its boss is back. Today it's pulling back as traders take profits. There are still real worries hanging over it: some analysts say the leadership situation is still messy, and U.S. securities regulators are looking into the company's accounting. **What to do:** This is the key stock to watch all day — as Nvidia goes, so goes the whole market. After a 31% pop in one day, a pullback is normal and healthy. If it keeps sliding, this could be a good one to bet against for a short trade (betting the price keeps falling). Only buy it if it steadies and starts climbing again — and even then, expect big daily swings. Do not put in money you can't watch closely.

**3. Dollar Tree (DLTR) — up about 8% to 9% this morning**
This discount store chain (everything cheap) reported strong quarterly results: it earned $1.16 per share when Wall Street only expected about $0.92, and sales at stores open more than a year rose a healthy 4.6%. **What to do:** On a day when the overall market is shaky, a cheap-goods retailer doing well is exactly the kind of steadier stock money moves into. Consider buying if it keeps going up, especially since shoppers hunting for bargains tend to help this company when people are worried about money. Lower drama than the tech names. The risk is that it already jumped at the open, so try not to chase the very top.

**4. Abercrombie & Fitch (ANF) — up about 5% this morning**
The clothing retailer beat expectations again and raised its outlook for the rest of the year — that's now 16 quarters in a row (four straight years) of growing sales. **What to do:** This is a genuine winner heading into the holiday shopping season, which starts in earnest with Black Friday this week. Consider buying if it keeps climbing. The catch: retail stocks can be moody, and if holiday shopping numbers disappoint later, these names can drop fast — so this is a shorter-term idea tied to holiday momentum.

**5. Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE) — down about 10% this morning**
This big business-technology company actually beat expectations on its results, but it warned that profit margins in its cloud and AI business will be thinner than hoped. Investors hate that, so they're selling. **What to do:** This is the opposite of a buy today. When a company warns about weaker profits, the stock often keeps sliding for several days as more investors head for the exits. This could be a good one to bet against if it keeps falling. Do not try to "grab a bargain" here just because it dropped 10% — there's a real reason it fell, and falling stocks like this can keep going down.

## Big Movers (up or down about 40% or more today)

**Lion Group Holding (LGHL) — up about 85% to 90% this morning**
This is a tiny, very cheap stock (trading around $1–$2) that rocketed higher with no clear news behind it. **Risk warning:** This is as dangerous as it gets. Tiny stocks like this, with very few shares actually changing hands, can double one morning and give it all back the same afternoon. There is no real news to support the move, which usually means it's just fast traders pushing it around. Do not chase this — it can crash just as fast as it spiked, and people who buy near the top often get badly burned.

**Research Solutions (RSSS) — up about 45% to 50% this morning**
This small research-software company agreed to be bought out at $3.25 per share, about a 52% premium over where it was trading. **Risk warning:** Like the Insmed deal above, the big jump is a one-time event — once a buyout is announced, the stock settles near the agreed price and stops moving. There's very little left to gain here now, and small-company deals occasionally fall apart, which would send it back down. Not worth chasing after the pop.

## Also Watching (5 more)

**Best Buy (BBY) — up around 6% to 7%.** The electronics store chain reported solid quarterly results right before the biggest shopping week of the year. Worth watching as a bet on strong holiday electronics sales, but remember it already jumped this morning.

**Kohl's (KSS) — up around 7%.** The department store chain beat on sales. It's been a beaten-down, troubled retailer, so this is a bounce on better-than-feared news — fine for a short trade if it keeps climbing, but this is not a strong, healthy company, so keep it small.

**Agilent Technologies (A) — up about 7%.** This maker of lab and testing equipment beat expectations on sales. A steadier, less flashy winner today. Consider watching if it keeps rising; much calmer than the tech and chip names.

**Semtech (SMTC) — down about 7%.** This chip company actually beat expectations, but the stock fell because it had already shot up nearly 19% yesterday — classic case of people taking profits after a big run. Watch to see if the selling stops; not an obvious buy while it's still sliding.

**Strategy (MSTR) — moving with Bitcoin.** This company is basically a giant pile of Bitcoin wrapped in a stock. Bitcoin crashed last week to around $55,000 and has been clawing back toward the low $60,000s. If Bitcoin keeps recovering, this stock jumps with it; if Bitcoin rolls over again, it falls faster than Bitcoin does. Only for a small, high-risk bet on a crypto rebound over the next week or two. (Coinbase (COIN) and the miner MARA move the same way.)

*This is research and general information, not personal financial advice. These are short-term trading ideas (holding 1 to 14 days). Short-term trading is risky.
