## Set-Up for Monday, July 6 (Next Session)

**What's on deck next week (light, macro-gated calendar):**
- **Mon Jul 6 — ISM Services PMI.** First read of the week on whether the services side of the economy is still expanding after recent softer labor data. Can set the risk-on/risk-off tone for the open.
- **Tue Jul 7 — US Trade Balance.** A window on import demand, tariff effects, and the dollar-growth story.
- **Wed Jul 8 — June FOMC minutes.** The headline event: first meeting minutes under new Chair **Kevin Warsh**, whose hawkish reputation has kept rate-path fear alive. This is the week's binary — it can whip **SPY / QQQ** and the rate-sensitive complex (semis, fintech, small-caps).
- **Wed Jul 8 (other):** Consumer Credit, Wholesale Inventories, EIA Crude Oil Inventories, MBA Mortgage Applications.
- **Thu Jul 9:** Initial & Continuing Jobless Claims, Existing Home Sales, EIA Natural Gas Inventories.

**Themes to re-check at Monday's open (prices will have reset over the 3+ day break):**

- **Semiconductors / AI — SOXL / SOXS / NVDA / AMD / AVGO / MU.** Chips led the Nasdaq lower into the break as investors reassessed AI-capex valuations (Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Intel, Applied Materials, Lam, KLA all under pressure). This is the group most exposed to the Fed-minutes reaction: a hawkish read extends the selloff, a dovish read sparks a relief bounce. Let direction confirm before pressing — leveraged ETFs decay, so treat SOXL/SOXS as 1–3 day momentum only.
- **Macro fulcrum — SPY / QQQ.** Dow at record highs masks a two-tier tape (value/industrials firm, tech soft). Everything keys off the rate path; the July 8 minutes are the swing factor.
- **Crypto-adjacent — COIN / MSTR / miners.** Bitcoin is limping into the weekend near **~$60–62K** after a fresh 21-month low, with June marking the **worst-ever month for spot BTC ETFs (~$4.5B of outflows)**. A modest weekend bounce doesn't erase the downtrend. These names can gap either way Monday on weekend BTC action — check where bitcoin printed over the break before trusting any setup; the tape is fragile, not a clean long.
- **Oil & energy — USO / XLE / oil majors / cruise lines & airlines (RCL / CCL / UAL).** Crude has been soft on Middle East de-escalation, a margin tailwind for fuel-sensitive travel names. **Key risk: any weekend geopolitical headline (Middle East, Strait of Hormuz) snaps oil back up in a single Monday gap** — verify the tape before trusting this.

**Long-weekend gap risk:** three-plus market-closed days mean any geopolitical, oil, tariff, or Fed-commentary headline gets priced into a single gap at Monday's open. Don't assume Friday's-would-be levels — wait for the Monday tape to print before committing.
