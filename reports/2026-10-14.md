## What's going on in the market today (the big picture)

- **Inflation came in hot this morning, and that is the big story.** The September consumer price report (how fast prices are rising for everyday shoppers) showed prices up 0.6% in just one month and up 3.7% from a year ago — faster than experts expected. The "core" number (which strips out food and gas to show the underlying trend) also rose more than hoped.
- **Why that matters:** The Federal Reserve (the group that sets interest rates) already raised rates in September — its first increase in more than three years. A hot inflation number like today's makes it more likely they raise rates again this year. Higher rates are bad news for expensive, fast-growing tech stocks and for risky "story" stocks, and they are especially bad for Bitcoin and crypto.
- **The market reaction:** Stock futures turned lower after the report, the interest rate on government bonds jumped (the 10-year hit about 5.29%), the dollar got stronger, and **Bitcoin fell sharply.** In plain terms: money is moving out of the riskiest bets and into safer, income-paying things.
- **Big banks are reporting their quarterly results this week, and it's a mixed bag.** BlackRock (the world's largest money manager) beat expectations and rose. Bank of America fell after a large one-time government penalty hurt its quarter. State Street dropped hard after its sales came in light.
- **Oil is still high,** which keeps helping oil-and-gas companies but adds to the inflation worry.

**The one-sentence takeaway:** Today favors steady, profitable companies and punishes anything expensive, speculative, or tied to crypto. Be picky.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Liquidia (ticker: LQDA) — consider buying, but higher risk
- **Move:** Jumped roughly **40–47%** in pre-market trading this morning.
- **Why:** The company settled a long and messy courtroom fight with a rival drugmaker, United Therapeutics. The settlement ends all six lawsuits at once and lets Liquidia keep selling its lung-and-heart drug, Yutrepia, for its main approved use. The cloud of legal uncertainty that had been crushing the stock is now mostly gone.
- **What to do:** This could be a good short bounce-back **buy.** Just last week the stock crashed about 57% on a bad court ruling, so today's jump is partly relief that the fight is finally over. **Risk:** the company also agreed to pay the rival a lot of money (about $175 million), and it had to give up one of the drug's uses. After a wild swing like this, the stock can be jumpy — keep the position small and don't chase it if it runs much higher.

### 2. Coinbase (ticker: COIN) — consider betting against
- **Move:** Likely under pressure today as Bitcoin fell sharply after the hot inflation report.
- **Why:** Coinbase is the biggest U.S. crypto trading exchange, and its stock tends to fall when Bitcoin falls and when people get nervous about crypto. Today's hot inflation number pushes interest rates higher, and higher rates pull money away from risky things like crypto.
- **What to do:** This is one to **bet against** (profit if it keeps dropping) for a short trade while the inflation scare lasts. **Risk:** crypto is famously unpredictable and can snap back up fast on any good headline, so this is a quick trade, not a long hold — set a limit on how much you're willing to lose.

### 3. BlackRock (ticker: BLK) — consider buying
- **Move:** Up about **4%** after its quarterly report beat expectations.
- **Why:** BlackRock manages more money than any other firm in the world, and it reported stronger profit and sales than analysts expected. With the stock market near record highs, more money has been flowing into its funds, which directly boosts its earnings.
- **What to do:** A solid, lower-drama **buy** for a short hold — a big, stable company riding good news. **Risk:** large, steady stocks like this don't jump much, so the gain here is likely modest. If the whole market sells off on the inflation fear, even good news can get dragged down.

### 4. Accenture (ticker: ACN) — consider buying
- **Move:** Up about **4%** in pre-market trading.
- **Why:** The big consulting company is holding its investor day (a meeting where it lays out its plans for the future), and investors liked what they heard about how it will use and sell artificial-intelligence services. AI remains one of the market's favorite themes.
- **What to do:** A reasonable **buy** for a few days on positive momentum from the investor-day excitement. **Risk:** if this week's inflation worry sparks a sell-off in anything AI-related, this could give back the gain quickly.

### 5. Bank of America (ticker: BAC) — watch, possible buy on the dip
- **Move:** Down roughly **4–6%** after a large one-time government penalty (about $1.2 billion) ate into its quarterly profit.
- **Why:** The drop is mostly because of a single penalty, not because the bank's everyday business is broken. When a stock falls on a one-time hit like this, it sometimes bounces back once investors realize the problem won't repeat next quarter.
- **What to do:** **Watch it today**, and consider **buying** if it steadies and stops falling — a one-time penalty is the kind of bad news the market often forgives. **Risk:** if higher interest rates slow down lending and the economy, that's a bigger, longer-lasting worry for all banks — so don't treat this as a sure thing.
