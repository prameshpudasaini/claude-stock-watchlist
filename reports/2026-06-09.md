parking spot; don't expect a runner — the big move already happened on the print.**

**3. Micron — MU — Long / Watch (chip-rebound leader, day two)**

The cleanest large-cap way to ride the chip recovery momentum. **Long for continuation, but it's a

**4. Oracle — ORCL — Watch into earnings (Wed, after close)**

the **$553B backlog** keeps converting and OCI growth stays elevated (84% last quarter). Bear case:

print clear Wednesday night and trade the reaction.**

**5. Nvidia — NVDA — Long / Watch (oversold-bounce leader + upgrade cycle)**
Riding the rebound with the group, helped by an **ongoing analyst-upgrade wave** on expanded H2
Blackwell shipments. Highest-quality chip to own when the group is basing higher. **Long for the
bounce, but size it small — like the rest of semis, it lives or dies on tomorrow's CPI. Buy strength,
not the knife.**
