# 2026-08-28 — Friday Trading Watchlist

> **Quick picture of the day:** This is the biggest event day of the week, and it all comes down to one speech. At **10:00am New York time, new Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh gives his first big speech at the Jackson Hole conference** (a yearly gathering of central bankers). The Fed sets interest rates, and rates drive almost everything in the stock market. The setup is tense: on Wednesday the government's favorite inflation measure (called PCE) came in **hotter than hoped at 3.7% for the year**, which makes it harder for the Fed to cut rates. If Warsh sounds worried about inflation and hints rates will stay high, expect stocks, gold, and Bitcoin to drop fast. If he sounds relaxed and open to cutting rates, expect a relief rally. Most of the move will happen in the two hours right after he finishes, and the jumpiness will likely last into the close.
>
> The good news underneath: **Nvidia gave a strong sales forecast Wednesday night**, which lit a fire under computer-chip stocks and is helping the whole market lean positive going into the speech. Bitcoin is sitting near **$80,000** after a huge August run, keeping crypto-related stocks hot. Futures (early bets on where the market opens) are mixed and quiet — everyone is holding their breath for 10am.
>
> *Prices below are pre-market or last-known levels. Always confirm the live price before you buy or sell anything. And remember: today is unusually risky because one speech can flip the whole market in minutes.*

## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. Nvidia (NVDA) — Consider buying while chip stocks are hot, but keep it small**
- **Price/setup:** Rallying after its earnings report, leading a broad computer-chip rally into today.
- **Why it matters today:** Nvidia reported its quarterly results Wednesday night and gave a **strong sales forecast**, which is exactly what nervous investors wanted to hear. That has pulled up the entire chip group and is the main reason the market is leaning positive this morning. Nvidia is the single most important stock in the market right now, so when it goes up, it tends to lift everything.
- **What to do:** **Consider buying** to ride the chip strength, but understand the catch: the Warsh speech at 10am can slam the whole market in either direction no matter how good Nvidia's news is. Keep the position small so a hawkish surprise (the Fed sounding tough on inflation) won't hurt much.

**2. Affirm (AFRM) — Consider buying on strong results**
- **Price/setup:** Up about **13% in pre-market** after its earnings report.
- **Why it matters today:** Affirm (the "buy now, pay later" company that lets shoppers split purchases into payments) reported **$1.17 billion in sales**, beating the roughly $1.11 billion Wall Street expected, and its forecast for the next few months was also better than expected. That combination — beating on both the past quarter and the outlook — is what makes a stock pop and often keeps rising for a few days as more analysts raise their targets.
- **What to do:** **Consider buying** on the strong report. Just keep in mind it's a fast-moving stock, and today's Fed speech affects it too (it does better when rates are expected to fall). Use a small position and a clear exit point.

**3. Gap (GAP) — Consider buying the momentum, but don't chase too hard**
- **Price/setup:** Up roughly **15% to 20% in pre-market** after its earnings report.
- **Why it matters today:** The clothing retailer (owner of Old Navy, Banana Republic, and the Gap brand) topped profit expectations with adjusted earnings of **52 cents per share versus the 48 cents** Wall Street wanted, and it named a **new CEO for Old Navy**, its biggest brand. Investors like both the beat and the fresh leadership.
- **What to do:** **Consider buying**, but be a little careful because the stock has already jumped a lot before the market even opens — a big early pop can fade during the day. If you buy, wait to see it hold its gains rather than chasing the very top, and set a clear exit point.

**4. PayPal (PYPL) — Lean against it / avoid buying the dip today**
- **Price/setup:** Down nearly **16% in pre-market**.
- **Why it matters today:** A report from Bloomberg said buyout firm Advent and payments company Stripe **walked away from a possible deal to buy PayPal** — which would have been one of the largest buyouts ever. The stock had been floating higher on takeover hopes, and now that those hopes are gone, that support is gone with it.
- **What to do:** **Be cautious — this is one to bet against or simply avoid today.** When a takeover story dies, the stock usually keeps drifting lower for a while as the traders who bought on the rumor give up. Don't rush to "buy the dip" (buy just because it's cheaper) until the selling settles down.

**5. Coinbase (COIN) & Strategy/MicroStrategy (MSTR) — Consider buying while Bitcoin runs, but watch the 10am speech**
- **Price/setup:** Both riding Bitcoin, which is near **$80,000** after a big August climb.
- **Why it matters today:** These two stocks move up and down with Bitcoin — Coinbase is the biggest U.S. crypto exchange, and Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) is basically a giant pile of Bitcoin in stock form. Bitcoin jumped more than 25% over the past week, and these names tend to climb even faster than Bitcoin itself when it's rising.
- **What to do:** **Consider buying while Bitcoin holds up**, but stay nimble today. Here's the danger: if Warsh sounds tough on inflation at 10am, Bitcoin and these stocks can drop hard and fast, because high interest rates are bad for risky bets like crypto. Small position, clear exit, and don't be a hero right before the speech.
