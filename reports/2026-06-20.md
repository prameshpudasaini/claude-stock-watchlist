## Top 5 for Monday (June 22) — Build Your Watchlist

**1. Nasdaq-100 Rebalance — effective pre-open Monday | the cleanest known catalyst**
The June quarterly reconstitution takes effect before Monday's open, forcing the 200+ funds (~$800B AUM) tracking the index to rebalance.
- **Adds (index-fund buying tailwind):** Nebius (**NBIS**), Astera Labs (**ALAB**), CoreWeave (**CRWV**), Rocket Lab (**RKLB**), Teradyne (**TER**). NBIS is the standout AI-infrastructure momentum name; CRWV and ALAB ride the same AI-data-center flow.
- **Deletes (forced index selling):** Charter (**CHTR**), Cognizant (**CTSH**), Insmed (**INSM**), Verisk (**VRSK**), Zscaler (**ZS**).
- **How to play it:** Adds often run *into* the effective date, then "buy-the-rumor" fade once the one-day flow clears — watch for a Monday pop-and-fade on NBIS/CRWV rather than chasing green. Deletes can gap down on forced selling and sometimes bounce once the flow is done. **Risk: index flow is a single-day event, not a fundamental trend — don't confuse the two.**

**2. Semiconductors (SOXL / SOX names: INTC, MU, NVDA) — momentum off a record high**
The SOX closed Thursday at a record on the Intel–Apple headline. **Intel (INTC)** is the marquee US-reshoring story. The group is extended after a record print, and **Micron reports Wednesday** sits right in the middle of it. **Long bias while momentum holds, but size for volatility — chips can give back a record-day move fast. Watch SOXS as the hedge.**

**3. Micron (MU) — earnings Wed June 24 | the week's headline print**
The most direct AI-memory/HBM demand proxy and likely the week's biggest single-stock catalyst. Bulls point to memory pricing and AI demand. **The trap: last quarter MU blew out the numbers and still sold off — the bar is on the *reaction*, not the print. Plan the trade before the bell; don't carry full size into a binary.**

**4. FedEx (FDX) — earnings Tue June 23 | global-trade bellwether**
First report since the **June 1 spinoff of FedEx Freight** into a standalone company, so the comps and guide will be messy — read carefully. Matters beyond FDX: the volume guide moves industrials and transports broadly. **Watch, don't pre-position into the number.**

**5. Energy complex (XLE, USO, oil majors) — short bias on the Iran peace dividend**
WTI sits at a 3-month low (~$74) after the US–Iran memorandum stripped the war premium. Producers stay pressured; the offset is fuel-cost *beneficiaries* — airlines, transports, consumer. **Fade oil-leveraged bounces; favor the demand-side winners. Risk: any crack in the fragile peace deal snaps crude back violently — this is the highest-headline-risk trade on the sheet.**
