# Short-Term Stock Watchlist — Monday, October 5, 2026

*A plain-English guide to today's best short-term trading ideas (hold roughly 1 to 14 days). This is research and opinion, not financial advice. Short-term trading is risky — never bet money you can't afford to lose.*

## What's driving the whole market today

The mood coming into this Monday is cautiously upbeat, but the market is taking a small breather. Before the opening bell, stock futures (bets on where the market opens) were pointing slightly **lower**, down about **0.2%** across the Dow, the S&P 500, and the tech-heavy Nasdaq. That's a tiny pullback after a strong Friday, not a sell-off.

Here's the backdrop in plain words:

1. **Friday's jobs report was weak — and the market loved it.** The government said the economy added only **29,000 jobs** in September, far fewer than the roughly 79,000 expected, and the unemployment rate ticked up to **4.2%**. Normally weak jobs sound bad, but lately investors had been worried the Federal Reserve (the U.S. central bank that sets interest rates) might *raise* rates to fight inflation. A soft jobs number makes a rate hike much less likely — the odds of an October hike dropped from about 70% down to roughly 23%. Lower-rate hopes pushed stocks up Friday, and the Nasdaq touched a record high during the day.

2. **Bitcoin just hit an all-time record.** Bitcoin climbed above **$125,000** this weekend — a brand-new high — and is up roughly 30% so far this month. That's lighting a fire under every stock tied to crypto.

3. **A big takeover deal is in the news.** A European industrial giant, Schneider Electric, agreed to buy U.S. software company PTC for about **$22.6 billion in cash**. Takeovers like this often put a little extra energy into the whole software and industrial-technology group.

4. **Brazil had an election, and investors cheered the result.** Brazil held the first round of its presidential vote on Sunday, and the early outcome is being read as pointing toward a more business-friendly government. Brazilian company stocks that trade here in the U.S. jumped sharply this morning.

The simple takeaway: the market is in a good mood because a feared interest-rate hike now looks less likely, and there's plenty of fresh, stock-specific news to trade. But stocks are near record highs and just paused this morning, so don't chase anything blindly — stick to names with their own clear, fresh reason to move, and keep every bet small.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Coinbase (COIN) — consider buying
Coinbase runs the biggest U.S. marketplace where people buy and sell Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies, so its stock tends to rise and fall with the crypto market. With Bitcoin hitting a **record high above $125,000** and crypto up about 30% this month, Coinbase is a direct way to ride that wave. **Consider buying** — when crypto prices keep climbing, Coinbase usually makes more money (more people trading means more fees), and the momentum right now is strong. **The risk:** this stock is a turbo-charged version of Bitcoin — it goes up faster when crypto rises, but it also falls harder and faster if Bitcoin suddenly drops, which it can do in a single day. Decide your sell price before you buy.

### 2. Alector (ALEC) — consider buying (but it already jumped a lot)
Alector is a brain-disease drug company, and today it signed a major partnership with **Genentech** (part of the big drugmaker Roche). Genentech is paying Alector **$100 million up front**, with the chance of up to **$1.17 billion more** down the road, for the rights to an experimental Parkinson's disease treatment. The deal also gives Alector enough cash to keep running into 2029. The stock soared about **67%** on the news. **Consider buying** — a cash-rich deal with a giant partner is exactly the kind of real, lasting good news that can keep a small drug stock elevated. **The risk:** the stock already made most of its big jump this morning, so a lot of the easy gain is gone — don't chase it if it spikes hard at the open, and small drug stocks can give back part of a pop within days.

### 3. Intel (INTC) — consider buying (bumpy, but strong story)
Intel is the famous American chipmaker that's trying to reinvent itself as a company that *manufactures* chips for others. Over the weekend, Tesla's Elon Musk said Tesla will use **Intel's newest manufacturing technology (called 14A)** to build chips at a huge new factory in Austin, Texas. That's a big vote of confidence — and it comes on top of the U.S. government recently taking a 10% ownership stake in Intel. The stock has been choppy (one report showed it down about 3% early as traders sorted out the details), but the underlying news is a clear win. **Consider buying** — landing a marquee customer like Tesla is exactly the proof Intel's turnaround needs. **The risk:** turnarounds take years, Intel still has to actually deliver these chips, and the stock can swing on every headline — so treat it as a bet on a slow comeback, not a quick sure thing.

### 4. DraftKings (DKNG) — consider buying
DraftKings is the big online sports-betting and gambling company. This morning a major bank, Bank of America, **upgraded the stock to "buy"** from a neutral rating, and the shares rose about **5%**. **Consider buying** — a fresh upgrade from a big bank often brings in new buyers over the following days, and this is a clean, simple catalyst (reason to move). **The risk:** a single analyst upgrade is a smaller push than a takeover or earnings surprise, so the pop may be modest; also, betting stocks can swing on gambling-regulation headlines.

### 5. Nu Holdings (NU) — consider buying (higher risk, watch the runoff)
Nu Holdings is a fast-growing digital bank serving tens of millions of customers in Brazil and across Latin America. It jumped about **10%** this morning as part of a broad rally in Brazilian stocks after Sunday's first-round election result was seen as favorable to business. **Consider buying** for those comfortable with risk — Nu is a real, profitable, fast-growing company, and a friendlier political backdrop could lift Brazilian stocks for a while. **The risk:** Brazil's election isn't over — there's likely a second-round runoff on October 25, and if that race gets messy or the mood flips, Brazilian stocks can drop just as fast as they rose. This is a news-driven move, so keep it small and watch the headlines.

Treat it as a gamble, not an investment.
- **Apimeds Pharmaceuticals US (APUS) — up about 95%.** A micro-sized drug company that spiked on news of progress in a clinical trial. **Risk warning:** tiny biotech stocks like this routinely double and then crash back within a day or two on little real substance — extremely risky, and easy to get trapped in.

*(General note: the tiny stocks above spiking 70%–95% almost always have a shaky or incomplete reason behind them — an unfinished merger, a vague trial update, or just hype. (PTC) — low-risk, but limited upside left.** This software company agreed to be bought by Schneider Electric for **$205 a share in cash**, and the stock jumped about **36%** to trade close to that price. Once a buyout is announced, the stock usually settles near the agreed cash price and stops rising, so the big move is mostly over. It's very safe but there's little quick profit left — mainly useful to understand today's deal news.
2. **Strategy / MicroStrategy (MSTR) — consider buying (higher risk).** This company owns an enormous pile of Bitcoin, so its stock acts like a supercharged bet on Bitcoin's price. With Bitcoin at a record high, MSTR is riding the same wave as Coinbase. Risk: it falls even harder than Bitcoin when crypto pulls back, so keep it small.
3. **MARA Holdings (MARA) — consider buying (higher risk).** MARA is a Bitcoin "miner" (it runs computers that earn Bitcoin), so it also rises and falls with the crypto market and is strong today. Risk: same as above — very bouncy, and tied completely to Bitcoin's direction.
4. **Vaxcyte (PCVX) — speculative buy (small bet only).** This vaccine company is expected to release important late-stage trial results for its pneumonia vaccine sometime this quarter, and anticipation can build into a date like that. Risk: this is a make-or-break data event — a disappointing result could sink the stock fast, so keep any bet tiny.
5. **Petrobras (PBR) — consider buying (higher risk, political).** The big Brazilian oil company's U.S.-listed shares rose about 10% in the same election-driven Brazilian rally as Nu Holdings. Risk: it's tied to both oil prices and Brazil's unfinished election, so it can reverse quickly if either turns — a short-term, news-driven idea only.

### Quick reminder on risk
Today's good mood comes from two things: a weak jobs report that makes an interest-rate hike less likely, and Bitcoin hitting a record high. Both can reverse — a single strong economic number could revive rate-hike fears, and crypto can drop sharply in a day. Stick to stocks with their own clear, fresh news (Alector's Genentech deal, Intel's Tesla win, DraftKings' upgrade, the Brazil election rally), stay far away from the tiny stocks spiking 70%–95% on hype, keep every position small, and decide the price at which you'll sell *before* you buy.
