## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. Tempus AI (TEM) — Long**
Jumped ~24% Wednesday to ~$61 and holding. The Moderna/Merck Phase 3 melanoma-vaccine win validates the exact MRD (minimal residual disease) sequencing thesis behind Tempus's $1.5B Personalis (PSNL) acquisition. PSNL clinical revenue is running +442% YoY. This is the cleanest "AI-meets-oncology" read-through and the momentum is fresh, not exhausted. Long on strength, trail stops under the breakout.

**2. Coinbase (COIN) — Long**
Up ~7–9% pre-market (~$159) as Bitcoin blasts past $71K and Ether ripped ~20% in 24h, triggering ~$3.3B in short liquidations (biggest of 2026). COIN is the highest-liquidity, cleanest way to ride the crypto surge without single-treasury balance-sheet risk. Momentum long; direction stays with the coin — if BTC stalls under $71K, tighten up.

**3. Merck (MRK) — Long**
Surged ~13% Wednesday to a record high (~$153) on the same cancer-vaccine data. Unlike Moderna's parabolic 177% spike, Merck is a profitable mega-cap where the vaccine de-risks the Keytruda patent-cliff story — a much cleaner risk/reward for a 1–2 week hold. Buy pullbacks toward the breakout shelf.

**4. Walmart (WMT) — Watch (lean short / fade bounces)**
Down ~6% pre-market despite a revenue and EPS beat and a raised full-year outlook. The problem: US comps grew just 2.6% vs. 3.5% expected — the slowest pace in six years — and guidance came in light on the top line. Defensive-retail leadership is cracking. Watch for a failed bounce as a short; don't chase the initial gap.

**5. Nvidia (NVDA) — Watch (long bias into earnings)**
No move of its own today, but earnings hit Wednesday 8/26 after the close and the whole semi complex (ACMR, MU, AMAT) has been leading. This is the market's single biggest near-term catalyst. Build/watch a long into the print or trade the semis around it (SOXL for leverage) — just be flat or hedged before the number if you don't want binary risk.
