## Top 5 Picks for the Week

**1. Replimune — REPL — closed ~+127% Friday; FDA decision due TODAY (Aug 2)**
The **PDUFA date for RP1 + nivolumab in advanced melanoma is Aug 2** — the FDA's own goal date is this weekend, so the approval/rejection headline can land before or at Monday's open. The advisory panel already backed the efficacy data **10–3**, which lifts approval odds, and IGNYTE showed a 34% ORR with ~24.8-month median duration. **Direction: watch / small long into the decision** — but this is a pure binary. A favorable panel is not a guarantee, and an unexpected complete-response letter reverses Friday's spike hard. Do not oversize.

**2. Palantir — PLTR — earnings Monday Aug 3, after close**
Street expects revenue **~$1.81B (+81% YoY)** and EPS **$0.34** (vs $0.16). Oppenheimer sees ~85% growth and a raised full-year guide. Options are pricing a **~12% move** either way. Palantir has beaten-and-raised for several straight quarters, but at **~71x sales / ~113x forward earnings** it needs a flawless print. **Direction: long/watch as an event trade** — ride a beat-and-raise for a momentum swing, but respect the "priced-for-perfection" fade risk. This report also sets the tone for AI/software names into AMD.

**3. AMD — earnings Tuesday Aug 4, after close**
Consensus EPS **$1.61** (from $0.48) on revenue **~$11.3B (+47% YoY)** — the key AI-semiconductor read-through after Microsoft's blowout capex guide. **Direction: long/watch.** A clean beat likely extends the semi bid (express via SOXL); a "good-but-priced-in" reaction fades it (SOXS). Size for a gap and don't chase into the print.

**4. Eli Lilly — LLY — earnings Wednesday Aug 5**
The cleanest large-cap in the bunch. Lilly holds ~60% of the U.S. obesity/diabetes market and 2026 revenue guidance sits at **$82–85B**. The story is **orforglipron**, its oral GLP-1, which cleared multiple Phase 3 trials and is heading into global filings. **Direction: long** — lower binary risk than the biotech/earnings gamblers here; watch for guidance and any orforglipron launch/timeline color as the swing driver.

**5. SpaceX — SPCX — first-ever public earnings Tuesday Aug 4, 4:30pm ET (on X)**
Maiden report as a public company (period ending Jun 30), audio-only webcast on X. Street focus: Starlink net adds and ARPU. Stock ~**$108**. **Direction: watch** — huge upside if the Starlink/AI story lands, but a **911.5M-share lock-up expires Thursday Aug 6** (~$116B), a real overhang. This is an event trade, not a hold-through-blind setup.
