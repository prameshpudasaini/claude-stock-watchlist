**2. Capricor (CAPR) — Speculative long / binary event, size small**
- **Level:** Ripped **~+87% Friday** (to the low $20s area) on the news below.
- **Why it's in play:** *The Lancet* published Phase 3 HOPE-3 data showing Deramiocel slowed upper-limb decline **54%** vs. placebo (p=0.03) in Duchenne. Cantor Fitzgerald upgraded to Overweight and jacked its target to **$28 from $3.50.** **PDUFA decision is 8/22 (this Friday).**

The FDA AdCom voted **9–3 AGAINST** on 7/29 (questioned the stats), so an approval is far from certain even after the Lancet validation. Approval = another leg up; CRL/rejection = brutal gap down. If you play it, size tiny and know you're holding into a coin-flip Friday.

**3. Intel (INTC) — Long / watch — foundry-conviction momentum**

**4. Home Depot (HD) — Watch / earnings event Tuesday**

**5. Energy / Oil & Gas (XLE) — Long / rotation leadership**
