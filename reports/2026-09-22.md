## The big picture this morning (read this first)

Yesterday was a huge, happy day for stocks, and today is the morning after. Here is what happened and what it means for you:

1. **Monday was the best day for stocks since early August.** The Nasdaq (the index full of technology companies) jumped 2.3% to a brand-new record high of 27,122. The S&P 500 (the 500 biggest U.S. companies) rose about 1.5% to 7,765, and the Dow rose 0.7% to 52,049. In plain words, money flooded into stocks — especially technology.

2. **Computer-chip companies led the party.** The rally was set off by early signs that Meta's new "AI agent" (a smart computer helper that can do tasks on its own) is working well. That makes investors think companies will need to buy even more chips to power this kind of software. Chip stocks soared: AMD briefly became worth more than $1 trillion for the first time, Intel jumped about 12%, and Arm Holdings jumped about 17%.

3. **This morning the market is taking a breather.** Before the opening bell today, stock futures (early bets on where the market will open) are pointing slightly *lower* — the S&P 500 down about 0.3% and the Dow down about 0.3%. This is normal after a giant up day: some people take profits and the market pauses. It is a rest, not a crash. Do not assume it means the good mood is over.

4. **Oil got cheaper and that helped everything.** Oil prices fell back below $100 a barrel because the U.S. and Iran may restart peace talks. Cheaper oil means less worry about inflation (prices rising across the economy), which also pushed down interest rates on government bonds. Lower oil and lower rates are good for most stocks.

5. **Bitcoin is hot again, and that is lifting crypto stocks.** Bitcoin climbed to about $87,000 — its highest level since January. The big reason: the government's market regulator (the SEC) just created a new five-year rule that lets certain platforms trade "tokenized stocks" (blockchain versions of normal company shares). That is seen as a win for crypto companies, and their stocks jumped.

6. **Keep in mind: the Fed raised interest rates last week** — its first increase in three years — to fight inflation. Higher rates are usually bad for expensive tech stocks. But right now, falling oil and falling bond yields are outweighing that worry. If oil suddenly spikes again, the mood could flip fast.

Bottom line: the market is strong and just hit records, but it is catching its breath this morning. The two hottest stories are computer chips (AI) and crypto. Both have run up a lot, so there is real money to be made — but chasing a stock after a big jump is riskier than usual.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. Strategy (ticker: MSTR) — the biggest way to bet on Bitcoin. Consider buying while crypto is hot, but keep it small.**
Strategy (the company formerly called MicroStrategy) owns a giant pile of Bitcoin — about 846,000 coins, worth a fortune. Because of that, its stock tends to move in the same direction as Bitcoin, only much harder — roughly five times as much on a given day. With Bitcoin at an 8-month high and the SEC's new crypto-friendly rule adding fuel, this stock has clear near-term reasons to keep climbing. **The catch:** that same "five times" effect works in reverse. If Bitcoin drops even a little, this stock can fall a lot in one day. This is a fast, jumpy ride, not a calm hold.

**2. Coinbase (ticker: COIN) — a direct winner from the new crypto rule. Consider buying on the momentum.**
Coinbase runs the biggest U.S. exchange where people buy and sell crypto. The SEC's new rule that allows "tokenized stock" trading is exactly the kind of news that helps Coinbase's business, and the stock jumped about 10% on it. On top of that, when Bitcoin is rising and people are excited about crypto, Coinbase usually does more business and its stock does well. **The risk:** Coinbase lives and dies with the crypto mood. If Bitcoin cools off or the excitement about the new rule fades, this stock can drop quickly.

**3. Ciena (ticker: CIEN) — a fresh vote of confidence from Wall Street. Consider buying on the good news.**
Ciena makes the equipment that sends data through fiber-optic cables using light — the kind of gear that AI data centers need more and more of. Yesterday a respected research firm (Evercore) upgraded the stock and raised its price target all the way to $550, saying Ciena is one of the best ways to ride the AI build-out. A big upgrade like that often keeps a stock moving up for several days. **The catch:** the stock only rose about 4% on the news, so the pop is modest so far — and if the whole market pulls back this week, even a good story can get dragged down.

**4. AutoZone (ticker: AZO) — a "beat" that was not as good as it looks. One to be cautious about or bet against.**
AutoZone, the car-parts retailer, reported its results before the bell this morning. On the surface it "beat" (earned more per share than Wall Street expected), but the details are weak: its actual sales came in *lower* than hoped, and the profit beat leaned heavily on a one-time tariff refund (a tax money-back), not on strong everyday business. When a beat is built on a one-time item and sales miss, the stock often struggles. If it starts sliding today, this could be one to bet against, or simply to avoid. **The catch:** management said sales are picking up again, so if investors focus on that hopeful message, the stock could hold up — don't oversize this bet.

**5. KB Home (ticker: KBH) — reports tonight, and the timing is bad. One to avoid or bet against into the report.**
KB Home builds houses, and it reports its latest results after the market closes today. The setup is rough: the Fed just raised interest rates, which pushes up mortgage costs and makes it harder for people to buy homes. Wall Street already expects KB Home's profit to fall sharply from a year ago. If you think tonight's report and outlook will disappoint, this is one to bet against or steer clear of before the numbers come out. **The catch:** betting before an earnings report is always a gamble — if the results are less bad than feared, a beaten-down stock like this can bounce hard. The safest move for a beginner is simply to watch and stay out until after the report.
