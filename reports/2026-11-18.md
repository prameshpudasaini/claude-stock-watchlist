## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Nvidia (NVDA) — up about 2% to 3% early (around $160) after strong results
This is the most important stock in the market right now. Last night's report (the company's quarterly sales and profit update) beat expectations by a wide margin, and management sounded confident that demand for AI chips is still huge. Several Wall Street firms raised their price targets this morning (JPMorgan to $230, Wedbush to $293).

**What to do:** Leaning positive — this is a reasonable one to consider buying if it keeps climbing after the open, because the business results were genuinely excellent and they ease the biggest worry hanging over the market. The catch: the company still has no permanent CEO, and a few analysts actually lowered their targets over that uncertainty, so the stock can still swing hard on any leadership headline. Keep your position modest and don't chase it if it has already jumped a lot by the time you look.

### 2. Coherent (COHR) — up about 9% early
Coherent makes the fiber-optic parts that move data around inside AI data centers. A fresh news report says the U.S. government is considering blocking some Chinese-made data-center equipment from being imported. If that happens, American suppliers like Coherent would pick up a lot of new business, so the stock jumped this morning. Two similar companies, Lumentum (LITE) and Credo (CRDO), are up too.

**What to do:** Consider buying if it keeps going up, since this is a clear piece of news with real money behind it (Nvidia has already committed billions of dollars to buy from Coherent). The warning: this is only a *possible* rule, not a final one — a similar story earlier this year faded when experts said the rule would be hard to actually enforce. If the news cools off, today's pop could shrink fast.

### 3. Take-Two Interactive (TTWO) — around $237, big event today
Take-Two owns Rockstar Games, the maker of Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) — one of the most anticipated video games ever. The professional reviews of the game are expected to come out *today*, one day before the game goes on sale tomorrow (November 19). The last two Rockstar games scored a near-perfect 97 out of 100, and the stock has climbed for weeks on the excitement.

**What to do:** This is a true event-driven bet. If the reviews are excellent (roughly 90 or higher), the stock could keep rising. If they disappoint, it could drop sharply — and because so much good news is already baked into the price, there is a real chance of a "sell the news" dip even on good reviews (the stock already slipped a few percent when the game's price was announced). Only for people comfortable with a sharp move in either direction today; consider waiting until the review scores are actually out before committing.

### 4. Palo Alto Networks (PANW) — reports earnings after the close today
Palo Alto is one of the biggest cybersecurity companies (it protects businesses from hackers). It releases its latest quarterly results *after* the market closes this afternoon, which usually causes a big move in the stock the next morning.

**What to do:** This one is about the earnings report. If you already own it and believe cybersecurity spending is strong, you can hold through today, but understand the stock often swings 5% to 10% the morning after it reports. If you don't own it, the safer move is to wait and see the numbers tonight, then decide tomorrow rather than guessing beforehand.

### 5. Newmont (NEM) — down about 3% early, and could keep sliding
Newmont is the world's largest gold-mining company. Gold just fell to its lowest price in a year, and when the price of gold drops, gold miners usually fall even harder because their profits shrink.

**What to do:** This could be a good one to bet against (betting it keeps falling) if gold stays weak, since the whole mining group is under pressure this morning. The warning: gold can bounce back quickly on any scary world news or a weaker U.S. dollar, and if it does, miners snap back fast — so this is a short-leash trade, not a long hold.
