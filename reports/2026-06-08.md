quality name to own when chips find a floor. **Long for the bounce, but this is a CPI hostage — don't

**4. Apple — AAPL — Watch (binary event: WWDC is live)**
Trading flat (**~$308**, near all-time highs) as **WWDC opens today** and runs through Friday. The

so expectations are sky-high. **Watch, don't chase — this is a classic "sell-the-news" risk if Siri
underwhelms or ships only in beta. Let the keynote clear, then trade the reaction.**

**5. Oracle — ORCL — Watch into earnings (Wed June 10, after the close)**
The week's biggest AI read-through, landing **the same day as CPI** — double event risk. Street wants
~**$1.96 EPS / ~$19.1B rev** with cloud guided **+46–50%** and a **~$553B RPO backlog**. Bull case:
backlog converts. Bear case: **AI capex blows out** (Oracle is raising heavy debt to build capacity).
**Watch, don't pre-position; let the print clear and trade the move.**
