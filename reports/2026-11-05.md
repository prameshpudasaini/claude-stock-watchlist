So today's action is really two things at once: an overall market that's digesting the election bounce, and a flood of company-by-company earnings reactions. The best opportunities today are in those earnings reactions.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Unity Software (U) — consider buying
Pre-market price around **$52, up about 22%**. Unity, which makes the software that powers a huge number of video games, reported quarterly results that blew past what Wall Street expected and gave a rosy outlook for what's coming next. When a company this size beats expectations *and* raises its forecast, the stock can keep climbing for several days as more investors pile in. **This could be a good one to buy if it keeps going up.** Just know you're buying after a big one-day pop, so don't chase it too hard — if it opens sharply higher and then starts fading, that's your warning sign.

### 2. AppLovin (APP) — consider buying, but be careful
Up roughly **16% to 21%** after a fantastic quarter and strong guidance. AppLovin runs technology that helps app makers show ads and make money. The results were a big beat, and the stock had actually been down about 60% earlier this year, so there's a lot of room for it to recover. **Worth buying if the momentum holds.** The caution: it's already jumped a lot overnight, so you're paying up. If you get in, keep a tight leash on it.

### 3. Qualcomm (QCOM) — one to avoid or bet against
Down about **9%** to roughly $157. Qualcomm makes chips for smartphones. Even though its results technically beat, the company gave a weak forecast for the months ahead and warned that a shortage of memory chips is hurting phone sales. When a big company guides lower like this, the stock often keeps drifting down for a while. **This is one to stay away from, or to bet against if it keeps falling.** Don't try to "catch the bargain" here until there's a reason to think the bad news is over.

### 4. Zillow (ZG) — one to avoid or bet against
Down about **16% to 17%** to roughly $29. Zillow, the online real estate company, reported worse-than-expected results and, more importantly, cut its own sales forecast for the coming year. A company lowering its own outlook is a strong negative signal. **Avoid this one, or consider betting against it if it keeps sliding.** The housing market being soft is a real headwind that won't fix itself in a few days.

### 5. Sezzle (SEZL) — consider buying (higher risk)
Sezzle, a "buy now, pay later" company, jumped after reporting revenue up about 43% from a year ago and nearly doubling its profit, then raised its outlook for both this year and next. That's exactly the kind of strong, growing story short-term traders like. **Worth buying if it keeps climbing** — but this is a smaller, more jumpy stock than the others on this list, so use smaller amounts of money and expect bigger swings in both directions.
