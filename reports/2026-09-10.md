# Short-Term Stock Watchlist — September 10, 2026

*For someone who wants to buy today and hold anywhere from 1 to 14 days. Written in plain English. This is research and education, not financial advice. Short-term trading is risky, and small, thinly-traded stocks are the riskiest of all — they can fall as fast as they rose.*

## The mood this morning (read this first)

After three losing days in a row, the market is trying to steady itself this morning. The Dow and the S&P 500 (two of the main U.S. market scorecards) are trying to stop the bleeding, while the tech-heavy Nasdaq is still a little soft. A few big things are steering the day:

1. **Oil is still expensive.** U.S. crude oil is sitting right around $100 a barrel, kept high by the ongoing tension between the U.S. and Iran. Costly oil helps energy and defense companies but raises costs everywhere else and keeps inflation worry alive.

2. **A big inflation report lands tomorrow.** The Consumer Price Index (CPI) — the main monthly measure of how fast prices are rising — comes out tomorrow, Friday, September 11. It's the last major piece of data before the Federal Reserve (the U.S. central bank) meets next week (September 15–16) to decide on interest rates. A calm number could relieve the market; a hot one could bring back the fear that the Fed leaves rates high, or even raises them, which hurts expensive tech and crypto the most. Because everyone is bracing for that report, today can be jumpy and cautious.

3. **Two huge earnings reports come out after the bell today.** Oracle and Adobe both report their quarterly results this afternoon, after the market closes. These are big software companies, and their numbers can move not just their own stocks but the whole software and AI group.

4. **Metals are pulling back.** Gold (around $4,427) and silver (around $66) both slipped this morning after big runs, so money is not rushing into safe-haven metals today.

The takeaway: it's a wait-and-see day ahead of tomorrow's inflation number and tonight's tech earnings. Keep your bets small and don't chase.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Oracle (ORCL) — Cloud & AI software — *Watch closely; risky to buy just before tonight's report*
Oracle reports its quarterly results this afternoon, after the market closes, and this is the single biggest scheduled event of the day. The stock has been climbing into the report (around $159–$160, up about 3%). Traders are betting on how fast Oracle's cloud-computing business — the part that rents out computing power for artificial intelligence — is still growing, and whether its giant backlog of future orders (reportedly over $600 billion) is actually turning into real sales.
- **Why it matters today:** Options trading suggests the stock could swing roughly 11% up or down after the numbers come out — a very big move.
- **The catch (important):** Buying right before an earnings report is a coin flip. If the growth numbers disappoint even a little, an expensive stock like this can drop hard and fast. The lower-risk approach is to watch how it reacts after the report tonight and decide tomorrow, rather than guess ahead of it.

### 2. Meta Platforms (META) — Social media & AI — *Consider buying*
Meta (which owns Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp) rose about 1.4% before the opening bell after the bank JPMorgan raised its rating and said it sees "meaningful upside" as Meta rolls out new artificial-intelligence products. On a nervous day, a big-name stock going up on a fresh, positive analyst call stands out.
- **Why it matters today:** A brand-new upgrade from a major bank often brings in new buyers for several days.
- **The catch:** Meta is a large, expensive tech stock, and if tomorrow's inflation number comes in hot, big tech is exactly what tends to get sold. Keep the position small until that report is out of the way.

### 3. Lithium Americas (LAC) — Lithium mining (batteries/EVs) — *Consider buying, but treat as speculative*
This lithium miner jumped about 4% after JPMorgan upgraded it to its "overweight" (positive) rating and set a $6 price target — which the bank noted was roughly double where the stock was trading. Lithium is the key ingredient in electric-vehicle and phone batteries.
- **Why it matters today:** A fresh, aggressive upgrade with a much higher price target is a clear reason for buyers to step in.
- **The catch:** This is a smaller, very jumpy stock tied to the volatile price of lithium. A single upgrade doesn't guarantee the move sticks, and a rough day for the broader market can drag it down regardless. Only risk what you can afford to lose.

### 4. Adobe (ADBE) — Design & creative software — *Watch closely; don't guess ahead of tonight's report*
Adobe (maker of Photoshop, Acrobat, and other creative tools) also reports its quarterly results this afternoon after the close. Analysts expect a large swing afterward — reportedly billions of dollars of value could move on the numbers. The key question is whether Adobe is winning or losing as artificial-intelligence tools change how people make images and documents.
- **Why it matters today:** A genuine, scheduled event that can move the stock sharply within hours.
- **The catch:** Same as Oracle — buying right before the report is a gamble. Watching the reaction tonight and acting tomorrow is usually the safer play.

### 5. Novartis (NVS) — Pharmaceuticals — *Consider buying / watch*
This big Swiss drugmaker rose nearly 2% after a report that a major shareholder is pushing to overhaul the company's board of directors to improve how it's run. When an activist investor pushes for change at a big, steady company, the stock can drift higher for a while as investors bet the shake-up unlocks value. A large drugmaker is also a calmer place to be on a jittery, rising-inflation day than expensive tech.
- **Why it matters today:** A fresh, specific catalyst (the board fight) plus a defensive, lower-drama business.
- **The catch:** These campaigns can take a long time to play out, so don't expect fireworks. And drug stocks carry their own headline risk from trial results and regulators.

## Big Movers

*Honest note: in this morning's scan I did not find a large, well-known U.S. stock clearly up or down about 40% or more today on clean, verified news. The clearest very-large move was in a tiny company, listed below. Among the big names, the moves were smaller — earnings winners and losers in the single-digit to low-double-digit percent range.*

- **Tenon Medical (TNON), up roughly 40%+ (around $3.62):** This tiny medical-device company (it makes an implant for a lower-back joint) spiked after it said it paid off its convertible debt before that debt came due on September 11, removing an immediate money worry that had been hanging over the stock. **Risk (very important): this is a micro-cap stock trading under $4, and it is thinly traded, meaning small amounts of buying or selling can swing it wildly. It is still burning cash and has warrants outstanding that can dilute shareholders. Moves like this often reverse just as fast as they happened. Treat this as a gamble, not an investment, and keep it tiny.**
- **Macy's (M), down about 1.5%:** The department-store chain actually beat expectations and raised its full-year forecast, but the stock slipped anyway — a sign the good news was already expected. Risk: a solid report the market shrugs off can keep drifting; don't assume an automatic snap-back.

*If any tiny stock rockets 40% or more on a single headline later today, treat it with extreme caution: those moves often reverse fast, and thin trading means you can get stuck unable to sell at a good price.*
