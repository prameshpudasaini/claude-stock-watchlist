confirm follow-through and don't chase a gap open the day before the Fed.**

**3. AST SpaceMobile — ASTS — Long-watch (hard catalyst tomorrow)**
The space complex is **rebounding** from last week's SpaceX-IPO siphon, and ASTS has a real near-term catalyst:
its **BlueBird 8/9/10 satellites launch tomorrow, June 17.** **Long-watch into the launch.** **Risk: "buy the
rumor, sell the launch" is a classic trap — a successful launch can still sell off, and a scrub/failure hits hard.

window, don't marry it.**

**4. CrowdStrike — CRWD — Long-watch (quality that survives the Fed)**

hawkish.** **Risk: it's already run with the analyst tailwind and software is rate-sensitive on a genuine hawkish
surprise; confirm it holds above recent highs rather than chasing the upgrade pop.**

**5. Dave & Buster's — PLAY — Watch for short / fade**
**Down ~11%+** after a Q1 **double miss** — EPS well below consensus, revenue light, operating margin compressed
to 8.4% from 11.1%, and the CEO blaming gas prices and weak consumer sentiment. **Watch for short / fade bounces.**
**Risk: a stock down double-digits on bad earnings can squeeze on any "it's washed out" bid, and broad risk-on +
