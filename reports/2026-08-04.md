The advisory panel voted **unanimously (9–0)** that benefits outweigh risks — a strong tell for approval, and it would be the first mRNA flu vaccine licensed in the U.S. **Direction: long into the print** as a positioning trade, but approval is partly priced in after the unanimous vote — the real swing may come on any commercial/guidance color. Cleaner risk/reward than most binary biotech.

**5. Ameresco — AMRC — +27% pre-market**
