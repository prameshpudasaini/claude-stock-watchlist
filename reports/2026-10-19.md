# Stock Watchlist — Monday, October 19, 2026

*A short-term trading guide in plain English. These are ideas for trades you might hold for 1 to 14 days, not long-term investments. Nothing here is a guarantee — prices move fast and you can lose money. Always do your own checking before you buy.*

## What's going on in the market today

Last week was rough for technology stocks. On Friday the whole market dropped — the S&P 500 (a basket of 500 big U.S. companies) fell about 1.1%, and the Nasdaq (which is heavy on tech) fell about 2.1%. Computer chip companies led the fall.

This morning, things are bouncing back. Before the market opened, the futures (bets on where the market will open) pointed higher — Nasdaq futures up about 1.1% and the S&P up about 0.5%. So today looks like a tech rebound day after a scary week.

Two other things to keep in mind:
- **This is a huge earnings week.** Some of the biggest names in the market report their results in the next few days: **Netflix on Tuesday (Oct 20) after the market closes**, and **Tesla, Meta, and IBM all on Wednesday (Oct 21) after the close**. Earnings reports (a company's update on how much money it made) can make a stock jump or crash overnight, so be careful holding these right into the report unless you're ready for a big swing.
- **No help from the Fed right now.** Some economists even think the Federal Reserve could *raise* interest rates this month because inflation is still sticky, not cut them. Higher rates are generally bad for expensive tech stocks.
- **Oil is still pricey** because of attacks on tankers in the Gulf and a Ukrainian strike on a Russian oil terminal. That tends to help oil and gas companies.
- **Bitcoin is around $82,000**, up a bit today, which is lifting crypto-related stocks.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Disc Medicine (IRON) — a biotech that just hit the jackpot
**Move:** Up anywhere from about 35% to 70% today (reports differ because it was measured at different times).
**Why:** This small drug company announced that its experimental pill, bitopertin, passed its final big clinical trial for a rare disorder where sunlight causes severe pain (called EPP). The drug hit every goal it was supposed to. The company plans to re-file for FDA approval by the end of this year, with a decision expected by the middle of 2027. It also has plenty of cash ($718 million) to last for years.
**What to consider:** This is the clearest winner of the day. If you believe the drug will get approved, this could keep climbing. **Consider buying**, but understand it has already jumped a lot, so it could pull back in the short term. Don't chase it blindly — a lot of good news is already baked into today's price.

### 2. Cleveland-Cliffs (CLF) — a steelmaker that crushed its results
**Move:** Up about 11% to 12%, to roughly $13.65.
**Why:** This big American steel company reported it made far more money last quarter than expected. It also told investors it expects to earn even more next year (profit above $4 billion in 2027). Simple story: the business is doing better than people thought.
**What to consider:** **Consider buying** if it keeps climbing today. This is a more down-to-earth pick than a biotech gamble — a real company with real profits beating expectations. Watch out if steel prices or the overall market turn weak.

### 3. Intel (INTC) — a chip giant getting a vote of confidence
**Move:** Up about 4%.
**Why:** A major Wall Street bank, JPMorgan, upgraded its opinion on Intel (moved it from "avoid" to "neutral"). When a big bank says nicer things about a stock, buyers often follow. Intel is also riding today's rebound in chip stocks.
**What to consider:** **Consider buying** on the rebound, but keep expectations modest — "neutral" is lukewarm praise, not a ringing endorsement. Also note Intel reports its own earnings on October 29, so this is a shorter window before that event.

### 4. Netflix (NFLX) — big earnings coming Tuesday
**Move:** No huge move yet, but it reports results **tomorrow (Tuesday, Oct 20) after the close.**
**Why:** Netflix just announced it's cutting about 1,000 jobs (6% of staff), which usually means it's trying to boost profits. Investors will react hard to tomorrow's numbers.
**What to consider:** This is an event-driven pick. If you want to bet it rises, you'd have to buy before tomorrow afternoon and accept the risk of a big overnight swing either way. A safer approach is to **wait and watch** the reaction after the report, then trade the direction once it's clear. Don't hold through the report unless you can stomach a sharp drop.

### 5. Tesla (TSLA) — big earnings Wednesday
**Move:** Bouncing with the rest of tech today; reports **Wednesday (Oct 21) after the close.**
**Why:** Tesla already told us how many cars it delivered, so the focus Wednesday will be on profits and how much it's spending on robotaxis, its robot project, and AI. These reports routinely swing the stock 5–10% overnight.
**What to consider:** Same warning as Netflix — this is a coin-flip event. **Consider waiting** until after Wednesday's report and trading the clear direction, rather than guessing beforehand. If you do buy ahead of it, keep the position small.
