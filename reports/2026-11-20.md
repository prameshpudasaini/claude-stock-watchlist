# Stock Watchlist — Friday, November 20, 2026

*Short-term trading ideas for holding 1 to 14 days. Written in plain English.*

**The big picture this morning:** The stock market is having a rough, scary week, and today is shaping up to be another down day. Yesterday was ugly — the Dow dropped more than 1,100 points and the Nasdaq (the tech-heavy market index) fell about 2.25%. This morning, tech stocks are falling again before the market even opens. The main reason is that people are nervous that the big artificial-intelligence (AI) companies have gotten too expensive. The brand-new Anthropic stock (the AI company behind the Claude chatbot) began trading Thursday and then quickly dropped, which spooked everyone and made them doubt the whole AI boom. Anthropic is sliding again this morning.

**Two more things weighing on stocks:** First, the Federal Reserve (the U.S. central bank that sets interest rates) now looks unlikely to cut interest rates at its December meeting — the odds of a cut have fallen from about 94% a month ago to roughly a third today, and one big Wall Street firm (Goldman Sachs) now even expects a rate *increase* early next year. Higher interest rates are generally bad for expensive tech stocks. Second, there's geopolitical worry in the air, with reports that the president is weighing a decision on Iran. Put simply: this is a "be careful" day. When the whole market is falling, even good stocks can get dragged down, so keep any new bets small.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Palo Alto Networks (PANW) — up about 9% before the open
This is today's standout winner going against the gloomy mood. Palo Alto, one of the biggest cybersecurity (computer-protection) companies, reported excellent quarterly results, raised its forecast for the year, and announced it is buying an AI startup. It was already the single best-performing stock in the S&P 500 yesterday, and it's climbing again this morning while most tech stocks fall.

**What to do:** Consider buying if it holds its gains — a company beating expectations and raising its outlook is exactly the kind of stock that can keep going up for several days as Wall Street analysts raise their price targets. Warning: it has jumped a lot very fast, so it could cool off, and on a bad market day even winners can get pulled lower. Keep the position modest.

### 2. Nvidia (NVDA) — falling again, at its lowest price in about a year
Nvidia is the most important computer-chip company in the world and the beating heart of this whole sell-off. Even though it reported strong results last week, the stock has now fallen six days in a row and just hit its lowest price in roughly a year. On top of the general AI nervousness, California's top lawyer has issued a legal demand (a subpoena) for information from the company, which is adding to the pressure.

**What to do:** This is the single most important stock to watch today, but handle it with care. If it keeps falling, this is not the moment to jump in — a stock dropping this hard can keep dropping (traders call this "catching a falling knife," meaning you can get hurt grabbing something on its way down). The braver bet is that the business is genuinely strong and the fear is overdone, so a bounce could come — but only consider buying once it actually stops falling and starts going back up, not while it's still sinking. Mostly one to watch closely today.

### 3. ProShares UltraPro Short Semiconductor (SOXS) — a fund that goes UP when chip stocks go DOWN
This is a special fund designed to rise when computer-chip stocks fall — and chip stocks are falling hard this week. If you think today's AI and chip sell-off has further to go, this is a simple way to bet on that without betting against any single company.

**What to do:** Consider buying only if you believe the chip sell-off continues today, and plan to get out quickly. Big, important warning: this is a "leveraged" fund, meaning it's built to move about three times as much as chip stocks do each day, so it swings violently in both directions. These funds also slowly lose value over time because of how they reset every day, so they are strictly for very short holds (a day or a few days), not for keeping for two weeks. Use only a small amount of money you can afford to lose, and if chip stocks bounce back up, this will drop fast.

### 4. Gap (GAP) — up about 4% before the open
Here's a calmer, non-tech idea for a jittery day. Gap, the clothing retailer, reported quarterly profits that beat expectations and named a new boss for its Athleta brand. Because it has nothing to do with AI or chips, it's rising this morning while tech stocks fall — which makes it a nice way to step away from the tech drama.

**What to do:** Consider buying if it holds its gains. Retail stocks that beat expectations often drift higher for a few days, and this one isn't caught up in the AI panic. Warning: we're heading into the holiday shopping season, so any worry about shoppers spending less could hurt it, and a weak overall market can still weigh on it. Keep it modest.

### 5. Intuit (INTU) — down about 10% before the open
Intuit, the company behind TurboTax and QuickBooks, is falling hard this morning. It agreed to sell its Mailchimp email-marketing business for about $3.68 billion, and — more importantly — it lowered its sales forecast for the year, which investors did not like. The quarter itself was fine, but the weaker outlook is what's driving the stock down.

**What to do:** This could be a good one to bet against if it keeps falling, because a company cutting its own forecast often sees its stock stay weak for several days while analysts lower their expectations. The other view is that a 10% drop in one day is a big punishment and the stock could bounce back a bit — so if you'd rather buy, wait until it clearly stops falling first. Don't rush in while it's still dropping.
