# 2026-08-25 — Tuesday Trading Watchlist

> **Quick picture of the day:** The market is trying to bounce back this morning after a shaky Monday. Futures (bets on where the market opens) point up — S&P 500 about +0.4%, the tech-heavy Nasdaq about +0.9%, Dow about +0.5%. Chip stocks are leading the way after a big thumbs-up for AMD from Wall Street. There are still two nagging worries in the background: the U.S. added new sanctions on Iran, and trade tensions with Canada are simmering. But mostly this is a "hold your breath" day before the two giant events of the week: **Nvidia reports its quarterly results tomorrow (Wednesday) after the market closes**, and the **Jackson Hole economic conference** wraps up Friday with **new Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's first big speech** plus the **July PCE inflation report** (the Fed's favorite measure of how fast prices are rising). Today also brings the **Consumer Confidence report at 10am** (a survey of how good people feel about the economy) and **durable goods orders** (how much businesses and people are buying big-ticket items). Bitcoin has come roaring back — up more than 20% this month to around $79,000–$80,000 — which is lighting a fire under the crypto-related stocks.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) — Consider buying while the chip group is hot**
- **Price/setup:** Around **$467 in pre-market**, bouncing around near flat-to-up after a big analyst call.
- **Why it matters today:** The research firm Raymond James just gave AMD its highest rating ("strong buy") and set a **$641 price target** — that's roughly **40% higher** than where the stock closed Monday. They think AMD will pass Intel in the market for computer brains (CPUs). This lifted the whole chip group: Intel is up about 3%, Nvidia up about 1%, and the big chip fund (SMH) up about 1%.
- **What to do:** **Consider buying** while the chip stocks have wind at their backs heading into Nvidia's report. The one catch: Nvidia's results tomorrow night can swing every chip stock hard in either direction, so keep the position sized so a bad surprise won't hurt too much.

**2. Dick's Sporting Goods (DKS) — Lean against it / avoid buying the dip today**
- **Price:** Down about **15% in pre-market** after a disappointing report.
- **Why it matters today:** Dick's reported weaker quarterly results this morning — it earned **$3.53 per share on $5.59 billion in sales**, short of the **$3.76 and $5.65 billion** Wall Street wanted. Its own stores actually did fine (sales up 4.9%), but the **Foot Locker business it now owns is struggling** (sales down 3.6%), and the company cut its outlook, blaming a "challenging" market for sneakers and athletic clothes.
- **What to do:** **Be cautious — this is one to bet against or simply avoid today.** When a retailer misses and cuts its forecast at the same time, the stock often keeps sliding for a few days as more analysts lower their targets. Don't rush to "buy the dip" until the selling calms down.

**3. Navitas Semiconductor (NVTS) — Consider buying on the AI-power deal**
- **Price/setup:** Up about **5% in pre-market**, trading in the mid-teens (around $14–$15).
- **Why it matters today:** Navitas announced it is **buying a company called Claros for up to $232.8 million** (a mix of cash and stock) to strengthen its technology for powering AI data centers. Navitas makes energy-efficient chips that help feed electricity to the giant computers behind AI — a red-hot corner of the market right now.
- **What to do:** **Consider buying** on this fresh news, which adds to an already-improving story (the company recently guided for stronger sales). Keep in mind it's a small, jumpy stock, so use a small position and a clear exit point if the excitement fades.

**4. Coinbase (COIN) & Strategy/MicroStrategy (MSTR) — Consider buying while Bitcoin runs**
- **Price/setup:** COIN around **$179–$187 (up ~8%)**, MSTR around **$119 (up ~6%)**.
- **Why it matters today:** Bitcoin has staged a big comeback — up more than **20% in August** to around **$79,000–$80,000** — and these two stocks move up and down with it. When Bitcoin is climbing like this, they often climb even faster (MSTR especially tends to swing more than twice as hard as Bitcoin).
- **What to do:** **Consider buying while Bitcoin keeps rising**, but stay nimble. These are fast movers — if Bitcoin stalls or slips back under about $76,000, expect them to give back gains quickly. Small position, set a clear exit.

**5. Nvidia (NVDA) — Watch and get ready; don't gamble on the report yet**
- **Price/setup:** Up about **1% in pre-market**, riding the chip-group strength.
- **Why it matters today:** Nvidia is the single most important stock in the market right now, and it reports its results **tomorrow (Wednesday) after the close**. Wall Street expects around **$28.7 billion in sales**, more than 50% higher than a year ago, mostly from AI data-center chips. Everyone wants to hear about AI demand, its new chips, and its China business. This one report can move the entire market.
- **What to do:** **Watch and prepare, but be careful.** In the short term the report is basically a coin flip — a great result can send it flying, a cautious outlook can drag down the whole chip group. Many careful traders don't bet big right before the number; they wait and trade the reaction Wednesday night/Thursday. If you buy ahead of it, keep it small.

## Big Movers (about 40% or more today)

- **Xiao-I (AIXI) — up ~150% (some quotes as high as ~220%).** This small Chinese artificial-intelligence company is spiking on very heavy trading, but there is **no clear piece of news** behind the jump (it recently changed the ratio of its U.S.-listed shares, effective Aug. 23, which can distort the quote). **Risk warning:** A tiny stock more than doubling with no obvious reason is almost always a short-lived frenzy that can crash just as fast as it spiked. Treat it as a lottery ticket, not an investment.
- **T3 Defense (DFNS) — up ~70% in pre-market.** A tiny defense/drone company (it recently bought most of an Israeli maker of drones and anti-drone systems) that has been wildly volatile — it recently did a 1-for-125 reverse stock split and swung between about $10.65 and $20.30 in a single day. **Risk warning:** Enormous daily swings in both directions; extremely dangerous to chase, and it can drop 40%+ just as quickly as it rose.

*Note: No large, well-known stock is down 40%+ on news this morning. The most notable decliner is Dick's Sporting Goods (see Top 5 above), down about 15% on earnings.*

## Also Watching (5 more)

- **Bloom Energy (BE) — Consider buying.** A maker of fuel cells (devices that generate electricity on-site) that has become a favorite way to bet on powering AI data centers. It recently landed a big deal with cloud company Nebius, posted booming sales, and one firm (Evercore) has a **$350 price target** on it. Trading around **$240**.
- **Kura Oncology (KURA) — Watch, leaning positive.** This cancer-drug company jumped about **11%** after its CEO personally bought **100,000 shares (about $1.1 million)** — a sign an insider thinks the stock is cheap. Insider buying can be an encouraging signal, but biotech stocks are bumpy, so keep any position small.
- **Gorilla Technology (GRRR) — Watch, be cautious.** This security/AI software company **fell about 10%** after its quarterly report: it lost more money per share than expected, even though sales came in much higher than forecast ($78.4 million). Mixed results like this can keep a small stock choppy for a few days.
- **Intel (INTC) — Watch.** Up about **3%** this morning, pulled along by the positive AMD news and the strength across chip stocks. It's a good quick read on whether the chip rally has staying power into Nvidia's report tomorrow.
- **Leveraged chip funds SOXL / SOXS — Watch for the Nvidia reaction.** These funds amplify the daily move of chip stocks (SOXL goes up 3x when chips rise, SOXS goes up 3x when they fall). They're a way to trade tomorrow's Nvidia result, but they lose value fast if you're wrong — for a quick move only, not for holding blindly through the report.
