## The big picture this morning (read this first)

1. **The big story is Anthropic.** Yesterday (Monday, Oct. 26), the artificial-intelligence company Anthropic — the maker of the Claude chatbot — filed the official paperwork to sell its shares to the public (an "IPO," which is when a private company lists on the stock market for the first time). It plans to trade on the Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT," possibly starting in mid-November. This reminded everyone how much excitement there still is around AI, and it lit a fire under computer-chip stocks: Nvidia rose about 2.4% and the big basket of chip stocks (the ticker SMH) jumped about 3% yesterday. Asian chip makers soared overnight too. That AI excitement is carrying into this morning.

2. **The Federal Reserve starts a two-day meeting today, and tells us its rate decision tomorrow (Wednesday, Oct. 28) at 2:00 p.m. New York time.** The Fed is the group that sets interest rates for the whole country. Back in September it raised rates for the first time in years (to a range of 3.75%–4%). This time, most investors expect it to *leave rates alone* — the odds of another increase have dropped to around 1-in-5. The key word for you: the market tends to go quiet and jumpy right before a Fed decision, because nobody wants to make a big bet until they hear the news. Expect a calm-ish Tuesday that could turn sharply tomorrow afternoon.

3. **This is one of the busiest earnings weeks of the year.** "Earnings" are the quarterly report cards companies give on how much money they made. This week the giants report: Microsoft and Alphabet (Google's owner) tomorrow after the close, and Amazon and Intel on Thursday after the close. Today you get PayPal, S&P Global, Hilton, Royal Caribbean, and the copper miner Freeport-McMoRan before the opening bell, plus Robinhood and Coinbase after the close. These reports can swing a stock 10% or more in a day.

4. **Stock futures (early bets on where the market opens) are roughly flat to slightly higher this morning,** with technology leading the small bounce and oil prices easing back. In plain terms: a quiet, slightly-up open, with everyone waiting on earnings and the Fed.

5. **Bitcoin is weak.** Bitcoin has fallen below $72,000 and is down roughly half from its October record near $126,000. On top of that, there's talk that Michael Saylor's company Strategy made its first sale of Bitcoin in nearly four years, which spooked crypto traders. Anything tied to Bitcoin is under pressure right now.

Bottom line: AI and chips have the wind at their back this morning, individual earnings winners and losers are the main action, and the whole market is holding its breath for the Fed tomorrow afternoon.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. Celestica (ticker: CLS) — jumped about 16% this morning on a great report. Consider buying the momentum, but know you're chasing.**
Celestica builds the hardware that goes inside AI data centers (the giant computer warehouses that run AI). This morning it reported stronger results than Wall Street expected *and* raised its forecast for the rest of the year — a one-two punch investors love — and the stock shot up about 16%. It's riding the same AI wave that the Anthropic news just re-energized, so there's a real, fresh reason for it to keep moving. **The catch:** it has already climbed a huge amount in a short time, so you'd be buying after a big pop. If the AI mood cools or the Fed rattles the market tomorrow, fast-rising stocks like this fall fast too. Keep it small.

**2. The chip-stock basket (ticker: SMH) — the simple way to ride the AI excitement. Consider buying while the enthusiasm is hot.**
Instead of guessing which single chip company wins, SMH is a fund that holds all the big ones (Nvidia, Broadcom, and others) in one basket. The Anthropic IPO news just reminded everyone how much money is still pouring into AI, and chip stocks jumped about 3% on it yesterday. Owning the basket spreads your bet so one company's bad news doesn't sink you. **The risk:** chip stocks are expensive and have had a monster year, so they can drop sharply on any disappointment — and the Fed decision tomorrow afternoon is exactly the kind of event that can turn the mood. This is a "ride it while it's hot" idea, not a forever hold.

**3. SoFi (ticker: SOFI) — up about 11% on a strong report. Consider buying on the momentum.**
SoFi is a popular online bank and money app. This morning it beat Wall Street's expectations and gave an upbeat picture of its future growth, and the stock jumped about 11%. When a fast-growing company both beats *and* sounds confident about what's ahead, that enthusiasm often carries the stock for several days. **The catch:** same as the others — you're buying after the pop, and if the broader market gets nervous before the Fed, even good news can get sold off. Don't oversize it.

**4. Coinbase (ticker: COIN) — reports after the close today while Bitcoin is sliding. This is one to bet against or simply avoid.**
Coinbase is the big U.S. place to buy and sell cryptocurrency, so its fortunes rise and fall with Bitcoin — and Bitcoin is down about 50% from its October high and still dropping. Worse, Wall Street actually expects Coinbase to report a *loss* for the quarter after the market closes today, versus a solid profit a year ago. Falling Bitcoin plus a possibly ugly report is a bad combination. If you think it keeps sliding, this is one to bet against; the simpler move for a beginner is just to stay away from crypto-linked stocks until Bitcoin steadies. **The catch:** betting against a stock right before its earnings report is still a gamble — if the report somehow surprises to the upside, it could bounce hard. Many beginners should just watch, not touch.

**5. Robinhood (ticker: HOOD) — reports after the close today, and it has a strange habit. Watch closely; lean cautious.**
Robinhood is the trading app. Here's the oddity worth knowing: even when Robinhood has reported *good* numbers, its stock has usually *fallen* afterward — it dropped after each of its last six reports, including five times when the results actually beat expectations. That tells you the stock is priced for near-perfection, so "good enough" tends to disappoint. Because of that pattern, buying right before today's report is risky, and some traders will bet against it expecting the usual post-report dip. **The honest truth:** this is a coin-flip event either way. The safest play for a beginner is to watch what happens after the report and decide tomorrow, rather than gamble on the outcome tonight.
