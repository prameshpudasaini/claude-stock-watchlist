## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Take-Two Interactive (TTWO) — up about 5% before the open
This is today's clearest story. Take-Two is the company behind *Grand Theft Auto 6*, the most anticipated video game in years, and it goes on sale to the public **today**. Pre-orders have reportedly been enormous (early reports suggest tens of millions of copies and billions of dollars already, though the company hasn't confirmed exact numbers). The stock has been climbing for weeks in anticipation.

**What to do:** Consider buying if it keeps going up on launch-day excitement and strong early sales headlines. The big warning: when a long-awaited event finally arrives, some traders "sell the good news" — meaning the stock can actually fall right after the thing everyone waited for happens, because the excitement is already baked into the price. Keep your position modest and watch how it trades in the first day or two.

### 2. Palo Alto Networks (PANW) — up about 9% before the open
Palo Alto, one of the biggest cybersecurity (computer-protection) companies, reported quarterly results that beat expectations across the board. Sales grew 39% from a year ago, and demand tied to artificial intelligence is strong. Good earnings like this often keep a stock lifted for several days as analysts raise their price targets.

**What to do:** Consider buying on the strong results if it holds its morning gains. Warning: the stock jumped a lot at once, so it could cool off after the initial pop, and the company is spending heavily on new acquisitions, which can squeeze profits. A smaller position is wiser after such a big one-day jump.

### 3. Nvidia (NVDA) — up about 2% before the open, near its lowest price in a year
Nvidia reported excellent results last night: profit and sales both beat expectations, and it predicted even more sales ahead. But the stock fell yesterday because investors are nervous about the company having no permanent boss after Jensen Huang left. It's now trading at one of its cheapest levels in years relative to profits, and today's Anthropic excitement is giving it a lift.

**What to do:** This is a reasonable one to consider buying if you believe the worst fears are overdone — the actual business numbers are genuinely very strong, and one major Wall Street firm called the recent drop a buying opportunity. Warning: the leadership uncertainty is real, and the stock can swing hard on any new headline, so keep it modest and expect a bumpy ride.

### 4. Anthropic (ANT) — brand-new stock, starts trading today
Anthropic, the AI company behind the Claude chatbot, begins trading today after selling shares at $83 each. Brand-new stocks on their first day can be wildly exciting but also very unpredictable — the price can open far above or far below the $83 starting price depending on how many people want in.

**What to do:** Mostly one to **watch**, not chase. First-day trading in a hyped company is often a rollercoaster, and the opening price can be much higher than $83, which leaves you paying a steep price. If you really want in, wait for the first wave of buying frenzy to calm down (often a few days) rather than jumping in at the open. Do not use money you can't afford to lose.

### 5. Bloom Energy (BE) — up about 8% before the open
Bloom Energy makes fuel cells — devices that generate electricity on-site — which are increasingly used to power the massive data centers behind artificial intelligence. A Wall Street firm (BMO) just upgraded the stock to a positive rating this morning, and the whole "powering AI" theme is hot right now.

**What to do:** Consider buying if it keeps climbing on the upgrade, since it rides the same AI-power demand story that's lifting the market today. Warning: these energy-equipment stocks can be volatile and are sensitive to the overall AI mood, so if AI enthusiasm fades, this one falls with it. Keep it small.
