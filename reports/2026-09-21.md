## The big picture this morning (read this first)

It's Monday, and the market is trying to shake off a rough week. Here is what is going on and what it means for you:

1. **Stocks are pointing up before the opening bell.** Early bets on where the market will open (called futures) have the Dow up about 0.8% (roughly 400 points), the S&P 500 up about 0.7%, and the tech-heavy Nasdaq up about 1.1%. In plain words: after a nervous week, buyers are stepping back in this morning.

2. **The main reason for the good mood is that oil got cheaper.** Oil dropped more than 3% — U.S. crude fell to about $97 a barrel and the global price slipped near $101 — on hopes that the U.S. and Iran might restart talks. Cheaper oil is good for almost everyone because it lowers costs and eases inflation worries. It is bad for oil-company stocks, though.

3. **All eyes are on a Trump–Xi meeting this Thursday.** President Trump and China's leader Xi Jinping are expected to meet, and the topics include tariffs (import taxes), "critical minerals" (rare metals used in chips, magnets, and defense gear), and artificial intelligence. A friendly meeting could lift the whole market; a tense one could sink it fast. This is the big wildcard hanging over the next few days.

4. **Remember the backdrop: the Fed just raised interest rates.** Last week the Federal Reserve raised its main interest rate for the first time in three years to fight stubborn inflation, and it hinted more increases could come. Higher rates make borrowing pricier and generally weigh on expensive, fast-growing tech companies. So even on an up morning like this, stay careful — the mood can flip quickly.

5. **Bitcoin came roaring back.** Bitcoin jumped above $85,000, up about 5% in a day, and it dragged crypto-related stocks up with it. This is a big change from a week ago, when Bitcoin was stuck and weak.

Bottom line: the market is bouncing this morning on cheaper oil and a Bitcoin surge, but a rate-hiking Fed and a make-or-break Trump–Xi meeting on Thursday mean today could stay jumpy. Keep positions small.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. Strategy (ticker: MSTR) — the biggest way to ride the Bitcoin comeback. Consider buying if Bitcoin keeps climbing, but know it swings hard.**
Strategy (the company formerly called MicroStrategy) owns more Bitcoin than any other public company, so its stock basically moves like Bitcoin on steroids. With Bitcoin jumping back above $85,000, Strategy was up about 7% before the bell. If the crypto rally keeps going this week, this stock tends to go up more than Bitcoin itself. **The catch:** it also falls harder when Bitcoin drops. This is a fast, risky ride — only for money you can afford to lose, and keep the position small.

**2. Warner Bros. Discovery (ticker: WBD) — a merger logjam may finally be breaking. Consider buying, but understand this is a bet on a deal going through.**
Warner Bros. Discovery (the company behind HBO, CNN, and the Warner movie studio) jumped more than 7% before the bell. The news: Paramount Skydance is reportedly making real progress in settlement talks with state attorneys general who had sued to block Paramount's roughly $81 billion takeover of Warner Bros. If those legal roadblocks clear, the deal moves forward and WBD shareholders get paid. **The catch:** this is a "will-the-deal-happen-or-not" bet. Some states (like New York) are still resisting, and if talks fall apart the stock could give back today's gains quickly. Paramount Skydance (ticker: PSKY), up about 6%, moves on the same story.

**3. Merck (ticker: MRK) — a government decision on one of its drugs is due today. Worth watching for a pop, but the reaction may be small.**
Merck, a big, steady drug company, is expecting a decision today from the FDA (the U.S. agency that approves medicines) on an expanded use for its lung-disease drug Winrevair, based on a large study called Hyperion. Winrevair treats a serious high-blood-pressure condition in the lungs. A "yes" would widen the group of patients who can get the drug and is a mild positive; a surprise "no" would be a small negative. **Why it's here:** Merck is a large, lower-risk company, so this is a calmer pick than the others — the stock won't swing wildly, but a clear approval could give it a nudge up over the next few days.

**4. Nvidia (ticker: NVDA) — the AI-chip leader is catching a bid, and there's fresh news. Consider buying on the up morning, but you're paying a high price.**
Nvidia makes the computer chips that power artificial intelligence, and chip stocks are rising this morning as the market bounces. Today the company announced a partnership with Einride to help run self-driving trucks on Nvidia's technology, and Nvidia's CEO keeps saying demand for its chips is so strong that supply can't keep up. Analysts remain very positive, with an average price target around $305. **The catch:** the stock is expensive, and a rate-hiking Fed is a headwind for pricey tech. If the Trump–Xi meeting sours on chip-related tariffs, chip stocks could drop. Chase it lightly.

**5. USA Rare Earth (ticker: USAR) — a small player in a hot theme right before the Trump–Xi meeting. Higher-risk buy for the brave.**
USA Rare Earth mines and processes rare metals used in magnets, chips, and defense equipment — exactly the "critical minerals" that are on the agenda for Thursday's Trump–Xi meeting. The company recently announced a partnership using quantum computing and AI to improve how it separates these metals (a process China currently dominates). With critical minerals in the headlines this week, this stock could get extra attention. **The catch:** it's a small, jumpy company, and a lot of the excitement is about the future, not today's profits. If the summit disappoints, the theme could cool off fast. Small position only.
