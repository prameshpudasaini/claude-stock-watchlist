## What's going on in the market today

Two things matter most this morning.

**First, a big inflation report lands at 8:30 a.m. New York time — today.** This is the government's October report on how fast prices are rising (called the CPI). It's the last one before the Federal Reserve's meeting next month, so it's a big deal. Here's the twist this time: because of a government shutdown last year, there's no normal year-over-year number, so everyone is watching the one-month change. The expectation is that prices rose about 0.3% in the month, cooler than September. Right now betting markets think there's roughly an 80% to 85% chance the Fed *raises* interest rates in December. If today's number comes in hotter than expected, that makes a rate increase even more likely, which usually spooks the stock market (especially expensive technology stocks). If it comes in cooler, stocks tend to breathe a sigh of relief. Bottom line: the whole market could get jumpy right after 8:30 this morning, so if you're buying today, keep that in mind.

**Second, the Nvidia cloud is still hanging around.** Nvidia — the biggest name in AI computer chips — has been under pressure for days over a scandal involving its CEO, Jensen Huang, who is being called to testify in front of Congress next Monday (November 18). Because Nvidia is so huge and so widely owned, its mood tends to pull the whole chip and AI group around with it. Worth a glance before you buy anything chip-related.

One more helpful backdrop: oil prices dropped sharply (down around 5%) after President Trump said new strikes on Iran are "highly unlikely" for now. Lower oil is generally good for the broad market and for smaller companies, and smaller-company stocks were holding up better than the big famous names this morning.

The clearest fresh opportunities today are a couple of earnings winners, a video-game story with a real date attached, and some one-off news moves below.

If you were ever tempted to bet against it, the weak outlook could keep it under pressure short-term, but betting against a stock is a riskier game and any good headline could snap it back up.

### 5. CoreWeave (CRWV) — wait and watch; don't gamble before tonight
