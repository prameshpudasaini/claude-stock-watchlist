## On Deck for Monday (June 22) — Build Your Watchlist

**1. Nasdaq-100 Rebalance — effective pre-open Monday | the cleanest known catalyst**
The June quarterly reconstitution takes effect before Monday's open, forcing index funds to rebalance.
- **Adds (index-fund buying tailwind):** Nebius (**NBIS**), Astera Labs (**ALAB**), CoreWeave (**CRWV**), Rocket Lab (**RKLB**), Teradyne (**TER**). NBIS is the standout — up ~165% YTD and the top AI-infrastructure momentum name in the group.
- **Deletes (forced index selling):** Charter (**CHTR**), Cognizant (**CTSH**), Insmed (**INSM**), Verisk (**VRSK**), Zscaler (**ZS**).
- **How to play it:** Adds often run into the effective date then "buy-the-rumor" fade once flows clear — watch for a Monday pop-and-fade on NBIS/CRWV rather than chasing. Deletes can see a forced-selling dip that sometimes bounces once the flow is done. **Risk: the index flow is a one-day event; don't confuse it with a fundamental trend.**

**2. Semiconductors (SOXL / SOX names: INTC, MU, NVDA) — momentum into a record high**
The SOX closed Thursday at a record on the Intel–Apple headline. **Intel (INTC)** is the marquee reshoring story (closed up ~10%). **Micron (MU)** reports **Wednesday, June 24** — a direct AI-memory demand proxy and likely the week's biggest single-stock catalyst. **Risk: MU blew out results last quarter and still sold off — expectations are high. Chips are extended after a record; size for volatility.**

**3. Micron (MU) — earnings Wed June 24 | the week's headline print**
Stifel and others see upside into the report; memory/AI demand is the bull case. But the setup is a coin-flip on the *reaction*, not the numbers — last quarter's blowout was sold. **Plan the trade before the print: don't hold full size into the binary.**

**4. FedEx (FDX) — earnings Tue June 23 | global-trade read-through**
A bellwether for shipping volumes and global trade health. Matters beyond FDX itself — a strong/weak guide moves industrials and transports. **Watch, don't pre-position into the number.**

**5. Energy complex (XLE, USO, oil majors) — short bias on the Iran peace dividend**
WTI sits at a 3-month low (~$74) after the US–Iran memorandum stripped out the war-risk premium. Producers stay pressured; fuel-cost beneficiaries (airlines, transports, consumer) are the offset. **Fade oil-leveraged bounces; favor the demand-side winners. Risk: any breakdown in the fragile peace deal snaps oil back violently.**
