# Daily Trading Brief — Friday, June 5, 2026

**Backdrop:** Jobs day, and the tape is split. May payrolls came in at **+172k** with
unemployment steady at **4.3%** — cool enough to keep the Fed in the conversation, but not
weak enough to spark a rate-cut rush (Street cut-bets keep sliding to the right; FOMC is
**June 17**). The bigger story is *what's moving under the surface*: the AI/semiconductor
complex is still bleeding from yesterday's **Broadcom (AVGO)** guidance shock, with Nasdaq-100
futures down ~1.0% and chips off another 1.5–3.8%. But money isn't leaving the market — it's
**rotating**. The **Russell 2000 (IWM) is up ~1.5%+ while the Nasdaq-100 (QQQ) is down ~0.5%**.
That's the actionable trade today: chips frothy and getting sold, small/mid-caps catching the
flows. Add a **Lululemon (LULU) −13%** guidance blowup and **Bitcoin stuck under $70k** dragging
crypto names, and you have a classic risk-rotation tape, not a risk-off crash.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. iShares Russell 2000 ETF — IWM — Long (the rotation trade)**
Up **~1.5%+** while the Nasdaq is red. With the AI/chip leaders looking expensive and
technically stretched, traders are pulling money out of mega-cap tech and pushing it into
beaten-down small-caps. This is the cleanest way to play the move without picking a single
name. Long while the divergence holds; the trade is over if chips stabilize and money rotates
back into QQQ.

**2. Lululemon — LULU — Watch for Short / fade bounces**
Down **~13% premarket**, now down ~40%+ on the year. The Q1 numbers were fine (EPS $1.69,
slight rev beat), but the **guidance gutted it**: full-year EPS cut to $10.95–$11.15 (from
$12.10–$12.30) and a brutal Q2 EPS guide of $1.76–$1.81 vs. ~$2.68 expected. Weak North
American demand, and management blaming "media commentary" rather than owning the miss is a
red flag. **BTIG cut it to Neutral.** Broken story on a down day — bias is to fade weak rallies,
not bottom-fish the open.

**3. Nvidia — NVDA — Watch (long only once the dip holds)**
~$214 and getting dragged lower in sympathy with the AVGO de-rating. The read-through is
nuanced — AVGO's weakness was custom-ASIC guidance, not a demand collapse, and NVDA's Blackwell
ramp is intact (banks keep nudging targets higher on expanded H2 shipments). Highest-quality
name to **buy on stabilization**, not to catch mid-flush. Let the chip group find a floor first.

**4. Strategy (MicroStrategy) — MSTR — Watch for Short**
~$128 and still wobbly. Bitcoin is stuck **under $70k**, and the overhang from Strategy's
first disclosed BTC sale in years won't go away. **Mizuho cut its target to $265** (from $320).
Risk-off crypto tape + a broken leveraged-Bitcoin proxy = short-side bias until BTC reclaims
$70k. (Note: TD Cowen still has a $400 Buy — it's a two-sided fight, so respect stops.)

**5. Snowflake — SNOW — Long / Watch (software leadership vs. chips)**
The standout software momentum name after its ~$6B, five-year AWS AI deal sparked a ~36% pop
last week. On a day when capital is fleeing semis but staying in the market, high-quality
software with a fresh catalyst is where rotation money can land. Long on a constructive
open; don't chase straight up — wait for a pullback to a level if it gaps.
