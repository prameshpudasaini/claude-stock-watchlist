## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Modine Manufacturing (MOD) — up about 28% after yesterday's close
This is the cleanest story on the list. Bloomberg reported that Samsung, the giant Korean electronics company, is in talks to buy Modine, which makes cooling systems that keep AI data centers from overheating. When a big company offers to buy a smaller one, the smaller company's shares usually jump toward the price of the offer. Modine is worth about $5.6 billion and already has a roughly $4 billion cooling supply deal tied to Google.

**What to do:** This is a reasonable one to consider buying if you believe a deal gets done, because buyout news tends to keep a stock lifted. The big warning: nothing is signed, both companies refused to comment, and if the talks fall apart the stock could quickly give back today's jump. There's also a chance a U.S. national-security review slows any deal. Keep the position small and don't chase it if it has already spiked a lot by the time you look.

### 2. Nvidia (NVDA) — down about 2% today, down roughly 30% from its October high
The uncertainty that hammered the stock has cleared a little now that Huang has actually resigned and a temporary boss is in place. Some professional investors call this a relief because at least the mystery is over; others say that with no permanent CEO chosen yet, it's too risky to touch. Huang has also been asked to testify before Congress, so headlines could keep coming.

**What to do:** This is still too much of a coin-flip to buy with confidence — wait for calmer trading or news that a strong permanent CEO is coming. If you already believe the worst is priced in and AI spending stays huge, a small bet that it slowly recovers is defensible, but understand the stock can swing hard on any new headline. Safest move for most people: watch and wait.

### 3. Cerebras Systems (CBRS) — up about 11%
Cerebras, a company that builds large chips and computers for AI (a competitor to Nvidia), just reported strong quarterly results, and investors liked them. This is one of the few AI-chip names going up while Nvidia struggles, which shows real demand for its product.

**What to do:** Consider buying if it keeps climbing on the strong results — the business numbers are genuinely good. Big warning: this is a newly public, fast-moving stock that can drop just as fast as it rises, and it's still tied to the overall mood on AI chips, so keep your position modest.

### 4. IonQ (IONQ) — riding a strong quantum-computing wave
IonQ is one of the leading quantum-computing companies (a new kind of super-powerful computer). It recently reported better results and raised its sales forecast, and the whole group of quantum stocks has been climbing for the past week or two as excitement builds.

**What to do:** Consider buying if the upward momentum continues, since this group is one of the hottest corners of the market right now. Warning: these stocks are built on big future promises, not big profits today, so they can tumble sharply when the mood turns. Only risk money you can afford to lose.

### 5. Vir Biotechnology (VIR) — jumped about 15% on strong trial news
Vir reported that its treatment for hepatitis delta (a serious liver infection) hit its main goal in a late-stage patient study. Good trial results like this often keep lifting a biotech stock for days as analysts raise their opinions.

**What to do:** Consider buying on the positive trial news if it holds its gains. Warning: analysts caution the study was small, and biotech stocks can reverse quickly if doubts creep in or the next update disappoints. Keep it small.
