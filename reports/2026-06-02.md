# Daily Trading Watchlist — Tuesday, June 2, 2026

> Short-term swing horizon: 1–14 days. Every name below has a clear near-term catalyst.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Marvell Technology (MRVL) — ~+20–24% premarket
The day's biggest story. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang called Marvell the "next trillion-dollar company" onstage at Computex in Taipei, citing its networking/interconnect chips as essential to AI data centers. Nvidia already holds a $2B stake. Cap is ~$192B, so the "$1T" framing is aspirational hype — but the tape doesn't care today.
**Direction: Long, but don't chase the open gap.** Let it digest the first 30–60 min; buy a pullback/VWAP reclaim. Multi-day momentum trade, not a fade.

### 2. Broadcom (AVGO) — earnings tomorrow (June 3, after close)
The marquee catalyst of the week. Consensus EPS $2.40 on $22.11B revenue, but the real bar is AI revenue — buy-side wants ~$5.0B vs. $4.1B last quarter. EPS estimates climbed 11% in 90 days, signaling confidence.
**Direction: Long into the print (pre-earnings drift) or watch — size for volatility.** Today's Marvell/Huang AI-infra narrative is a direct tailwind into the number. Decide before tomorrow's close.

### 3. Micron (MU) — ~$1,030, -0.4% premarket (profit-taking)
HBM capacity **sold out through 2026**, HBM4 36GB module launched at Computex with 2.6x inference gains, up ~68% YTD. The slight dip is healthy profit-taking after a 6.6% session. Cleanest fundamental story in memory — the "memory crunch" trade.
**Direction: Long on dips.** Add on any flush toward support; the supply/demand setup is structural.

### 4. Coinbase (COIN) — ~$179.50, -5%
Hit by the MicroStrategy headline (first BTC sale since 2022) that triggered a crypto-wide selloff. Q1 was already soft — GAAP loss of $1.49/share, leaning on a 14% headcount cut.
**Direction: Watch for short continuation OR an oversold bounce trade.** Sell-off is sentiment-driven (Saylor's sale was tiny, at cost), so it can snap back fast — trade the reaction, don't marry a side. Annual meeting June 16.

### 5. AMD (AMD) — ~$502.75, -1.45% premarket
Under pressure as Nvidia's new N1X "superchip" (with Microsoft) overshadows it, plus ARK is rotating out of AMD into NVDA. BUT fresh analyst love: Mizuho to $615 (from $515), Barclays to $665 (from $500), both bullish.
**Direction: Watch.** A tension trade — competitive fear vs. strong PT hikes. Wait for it to pick a direction off the open; a reclaim of the premarket high is the long trigger.
