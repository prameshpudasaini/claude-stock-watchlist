Leadership is a split screen: **AI-infrastructure earnings** keep carrying pockets of the tape (SanDisk Investor Day, Micron), while **consumer and event-driven M&A names** (Wendy's, Birkenstock, Ardagh) are today's cleanest single-stock stories.
