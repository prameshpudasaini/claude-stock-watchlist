## The big picture this morning

It's a rough morning for technology stocks, and that colors everything today.

- **Big tech is falling even though the companies did well.** Tesla, Meta, IBM, and Lam Research all reported quarterly results (their regular updates on sales and profit) that actually beat what Wall Street expected. But their stocks are dropping anyway, because investors are nervous about how much money these companies are pouring into artificial intelligence. When a company spends tens of billions on AI and the payoff isn't obvious yet, traders get spooked. Stock market futures (early bets on where the market opens) pointed sharply lower: the broad S&P 500 down about 1%, and the tech-heavy Nasdaq down close to 1.7%.
- **Interest rates are high and that hurts.** The interest rate on the 10-year U.S. government bond is sitting near 5.3% — the highest since 2002. When safe government bonds pay that much, risky and fast-growing stocks (especially small companies and biotech) look less attractive, so money flows out of them.
- **Oil is expensive.** Crude oil is trading above $111 a barrel. That's good for oil companies but bad for airlines and anyone who burns a lot of fuel.
- **Bitcoin slipped too.** Bitcoin is around $82,000, down roughly 1.6% today, pulled lower by the same nervousness hitting tech stocks. Crypto-linked stocks tend to fall with it.

**Bottom line:** today favors caution. Safer corners like defense companies and gold are holding up, while high-flying tech and crypto names are under pressure.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Lockheed Martin (LMT) — *consider buying*
**Up about 3.8% this morning.** The big defense contractor just reported strong quarterly results, and its stock is rising while most of the market falls. Defense stocks are a classic hiding spot when the overall market is jittery — governments keep spending on weapons no matter what the economy does. With the market scared of tech today, money is rotating into steady names like this one. If it keeps climbing after the open, it could be a good one to buy and hold for a short move higher.

### 2. Spotify (SPOT) — *consider buying*
**Higher this morning.** The music-streaming company gave an upbeat forecast for its sales in the months ahead, and that beat what analysts expected. Good forward guidance (a company's own prediction about future sales) is often what makes a stock pop, more than the past quarter's numbers. The stock had been weak earlier this year, so a strong outlook could spark a bounce. Consider buying if it holds its gains in the first hour of trading.

### 3. Tesla (TSLA) — *be careful; could be one to bet against*
**Down roughly 4% to 5% in early trading, near $367.** Tesla beat expectations on both sales and profit, but investors zeroed in on one thing: the company cut its spending plans for this year to $20 billion from the $25 billion it promised three months ago. That worries people about future growth. When a popular stock falls on "good" news, it often keeps sliding for a few days as disappointed buyers give up. This is a risky one to bet against (betting it keeps falling), and only for those comfortable with Tesla's wild swings. Safer move for most people: simply stay away until it settles.

### 4. Newmont (NEM) — *watch closely, possible buy around tonight's report*
**Reports its quarterly results after the market closes today.** Newmont is the world's largest gold miner, and gold has been one of the best-performing assets this year as investors look for safety. With the market nervous and interest rates high, gold stays in demand. A strong report tonight could push the stock up tomorrow. This is a bet on the report, so it carries extra risk — if the numbers disappoint, it can drop fast. Consider a small position before the report only if you're comfortable with that gamble, or wait and buy after the results if they look good.

### 5. Lam Research (LRCX) — *wait and watch; risky bounce candidate*
**Down about 8.5% this morning.** This company makes the machines that build computer chips, and it fell hard even after beating expectations, caught in today's tech sell-off. A drop this big in a good company sometimes sets up a quick rebound once the panic fades. But catching a falling stock is dangerous — don't rush in. Watch it through the day; if it stops falling and starts ticking back up, it could be worth buying for a short bounce. If it keeps sliding, leave it alone.
