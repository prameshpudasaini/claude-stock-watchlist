Friday was a "bad news is good news" session. July payrolls came in at **-23,000 jobs** (a loss, vs. +83k expected), unemployment steady at 4.1%. Traders read the weak labor print as the Fed staying on hold with rate cuts back on the table, and bought risk hard:

- **S&P 500 +0.62% to a record 7,757.64**
- **Nasdaq +1.3% to 26,690.62**
- **Dow +0.28% to 54,036.93**
- **Bitcoin above $65,000**, gold at records — everything risk/rate-sensitive caught a bid.

Leadership was in AI/software (post-earnings) and industrials/materials. Only communication services and energy fell. The setup into Monday is momentum-friendly, but a lot of good news (soft-landing + cuts) is now priced in, so chase carefully.
