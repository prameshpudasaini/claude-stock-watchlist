## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. Nvidia (NVDA) — around $224.69, up about 7% before the open — consider buying if it keeps climbing after the open**
- **Why it matters today:** This is the clearest, strongest piece of good news in the market right now. Nvidia didn't just beat expectations — it raised the bar for the next quarter and started buying back its own shares, which is a loud signal of confidence. The worry going in was that its huge growth was slowing down; these results say the opposite.
- **What to do:** **This is a real, news-driven jump worth considering for a short hold.** The honest risk: the stock had already fallen for several days before the report, so some of this pop is just relief. When a stock gaps up 7% overnight, some traders sell into the good news and it can drift back during the day. A calmer approach is to buy a smaller amount and add more only if it holds its gains over the next day or two, rather than chasing the very first spike.

**2. Semiconductor funds SOXL (bet up) and SOXS (bet down) — the fast way to play the chip rally**
- **What these are:** These are special funds that move about **three times** as much as the whole group of computer-chip stocks each day. SOXL goes up when chips go up; SOXS goes up when chips go down. A 1% move in chip stocks becomes roughly a 3% move in these.
- **Why watch today:** Nvidia's blowout is lifting the entire chip group — companies like AMD, Broadcom, and Micron all move with it. SOXL is the aggressive way to ride that wave up today.
- **What to do:** **Only for a very short, small bet — these are fast and dangerous.** If chips keep climbing through the day, SOXL amplifies the gain. But because it multiplies moves by three, a sudden reversal (for example, if tomorrow's Fed speech spooks the market) hurts three times as much. Keep any position small and don't hold it blindly for days.

**3. CrowdStrike (CRWD) — up about 9% before the open — consider buying on strong results**
- **Why it's moving:** This cybersecurity company reported sales of **$1.47 billion**, up 26% from a year ago, and its measure of new signed-up business hit a record — up 51% from last year. It also raised its forecast for the rest of the year. In plain terms: more companies are paying CrowdStrike to protect them, and faster than expected.
- **What to do:** **This is a genuine winner worth considering for a short hold.** Same caution as Nvidia — it already jumped 9% overnight, so don't chase the very first burst. Watch whether it holds up after the open; if it stays strong, that's your signal it can keep running for a few days.

**4. Salesforce (CRM) — jumped sharply after hours on earnings and the Anthropic/Claude deal**
- **Why it's moving:** Salesforce reported profit of **$5.90 per share** — nearly double a year ago and far above the ~$3.27 that was expected — and raised its forecast. On top of that, it announced "Claudeforce," a new tie-up letting people use Salesforce's business data directly inside Anthropic's Claude AI assistant. That deal is a big vote of confidence that Salesforce is winning in AI, not losing to it.
- **What to do:** **Worth considering for a short hold.** One thing to watch: some investors expected a strong report and may have already bought ahead of it, so the stock's move after the market opens may be calmer than the after-hours pop suggested. If it opens strong and stays there, it's a solid short-term buy candidate riding real AI momentum.

**5. Rocket Lab (RKLB) — Wall Street just piled on with upgrades — consider buying if it keeps going up**
- **Why it's moving:** Several big firms just raised their opinions and price targets on this space and rocket-launch company on the same day: Morgan Stanley moved it to one of its top ratings with a **$105** target, Bank of America went to **$120**, and TD Cowen to **$100**. The story is that Rocket Lab is winning more national-security and space work. A wave of upgrades like this in one morning often draws in new buyers.
- **What to do:** **Consider buying if it keeps going up today.** This isn't tied to Nvidia, so it gives you something different from the crowded chip trade. Be aware that space stocks are jumpy and can swing hard on a single headline, so keep the position modest.
