## What's going on in the market today

Two big things are happening at once.

First, there was a rough day for technology stocks on Thursday. The Nasdaq (the stock index packed with tech and AI companies) had a sharp drop — one of its worst days in a while — as investors suddenly got nervous that AI-related stocks had climbed too far, too fast. A huge amount of market value was wiped out in a single day, and Nvidia had its biggest one-day fall since early 2025. This morning (Friday), things are calming down a bit: tech futures are pointing up, and the beaten-down chip stocks (Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Intel) are bouncing back by a few percent each in pre-market trading. Whether that bounce holds is the big question for the day.

Second, we're deep in earnings season (the time when companies report their quarterly results), so a lot of individual stocks are making giant moves this morning based on what they reported — some way up, some way down.

On top of all that, the government's October jobs report comes out at 8:30 a.m. Eastern time. This is the big monthly snapshot of how many jobs the U.S. economy added. Economists expect a small number (somewhere around 45,000 to 65,000 new jobs) with unemployment around 4.2%. Here's why it matters for your trades: if the jobs number comes in strong, it makes it *less* likely the Federal Reserve will cut interest rates soon, which can spook the stock market; if it comes in weak, it can actually help stocks because it makes a rate cut more likely. Either way, expect the whole market to jump around right after 8:30, so be careful buying anything in the first half hour.

The best opportunities today are in the earnings reactions. Those are below.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Cloudflare (NET) — consider buying
Pre-market price around **$295, up roughly 15% to 21%**. Cloudflare runs a big chunk of the internet's "plumbing" — it keeps websites fast and safe from attacks. It just reported quarterly results that beat Wall Street's expectations, with sales growing about 39% from a year ago, and it raised its forecast for the rest of the year. A big beat *plus* a raised forecast is the strongest kind of earnings news, and stocks like this can keep climbing for several days as more buyers pile in. **This is a good one to consider buying if it keeps going up.** The one caution: it's already jumped a lot overnight and the overall tech market is jittery right now, so don't chase it too hard — if it opens sharply higher and then starts sliding back, that's your signal to step aside.

### 2. DraftKings (DKNG) — one to avoid or bet against
Down about **21% to roughly $18.40**. DraftKings runs online sports betting. It reported a much bigger loss than expected (it lost about 94 cents per share when Wall Street was braced for a loss of only 39 cents), its sales came in short, and — most importantly — it *cut* its own revenue forecast for the year. When a company lowers its own outlook like this, the stock often keeps drifting lower for a while as disappointed investors sell. **This is one to stay away from, or to bet against if it keeps falling.** Don't try to grab it as a "bargain" just because it dropped — there's no sign yet that the bad news is over.

### 3. Airbnb (ABNB) — consider buying
Up about **12% to roughly $191**. Airbnb, the home-rental booking company, reported strong quarterly results that topped expectations and raised its outlook for the full year. Like Cloudflare, this is the good kind of earnings surprise — beat the numbers and lifted the forecast. **Worth considering buying if the strength holds.** Same warning as always: you're buying after a big overnight pop, so keep your position modest and have a price in mind where you'll sell if you turn out to be wrong.

### 4. Atlassian (TEAM) — one to avoid or bet against
Down about **16% to 17%**. Atlassian makes workplace software that teams use to manage projects (tools like Jira and Trello). Its results came with a weaker-than-expected forecast for growth ahead, and investors are punishing it hard for that. A soft outlook tends to weigh on a stock for more than just one day. **Lean toward avoiding this, or betting against it if it keeps sliding.** Wait for clear signs the selling has stopped before thinking about it as a buy.

### 5. Lam Research (LRCX) — consider buying (part of the chip bounce)
Up about **11%** in pre-market trading. Lam Research makes the machines that factories use to build computer chips. Two things are helping it today: it announced a giant **$25 billion plan to buy back its own shares** (which usually supports the stock price), and the whole chip group is bouncing back this morning after Thursday's beating. **Consider buying if the chip rebound holds up during the day.** The risk here is that this is really a bet on the shaky chip sector recovering — if that morning bounce fades and chips roll back over, this one will fall with the group, so watch how the other chip names (Nvidia, AMD) are trading before you commit.
