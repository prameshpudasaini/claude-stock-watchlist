## The one thing driving everything: cool PPI + a fintech mega-deal reawaken risk appetite

Two straight soft inflation reads (negative CPI Tuesday, -0.3% PPI today) take the "inflation reaccelerates" scare off the table, even if the Fed is still expected to hold July 28-29. Into that friendlier backdrop drops the day's blockbuster: **Stripe and Advent International offered $60.50/share (~$53B) for PayPal**, a ~28% premium with ~$50B of committed financing. That does two things — it hands PYPL holders an instant re-rate, and it signals that strategic/PE buyers see deep value in beaten-down large-cap fintech, which puts a floor bid under the group. Layer on **ASML lifting guidance** (AI-driven equipment demand) and **BlackRock crushing Q2**, and the megacap-quality names lead. The offset is idiosyncratic earnings pain: **Pentair cut guidance and lost its CFO**, and **Elevance flagged elevated government medical costs** — so avoid the managed-care and water-equipment corners while leaning into fintech, semis, and the quality financials.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. PayPal — PYPL — Long (event/merger-arb)**
PYPL is up **~18-20% premarket** after a report that **Stripe + Advent International bid $60.50/share (~$53B)**, a ~28% premium to Tuesday's close, with ~$50B committed financing. The buyers want a 50/50 joint ownership and say they won't dismantle the company. Play: this is a live M&A situation — long for the deal premium and the possibility a competing/higher bid emerges, or that PayPal engages. Risk: PayPal hasn't responded and could reject; if talks stall, the stock gives back a chunk of the pop. Merger-arb, not a clean fundamental long — size accordingly and know your downside to the pre-deal price (~$47).

**2. ASML — ASML — Long**
ASML rose **~3.8%** after raising its **2026 financial forecasts**, citing strong AI-driven demand for its lithography tools. As the chokepoint supplier to every leading-edge fab, an ASML guide-up is the cleanest read that the AI capex cycle is still accelerating — it lifts the whole semi-equipment and memory complex (AMAT, LRCX, KLAC, MU). Play: long ASML on the guidance beat, or use SOXL/SMH for broader semi exposure into the AI-trade re-acceleration. Risk: semis have been a two-way wrecking ball all month; leveraged ETFs are 1-3 day trades, not holds.

**3. Pentair — PNR — Watch for short / avoid**
PNR is down **~20.7%** after **cutting Q2 and full-year 2026 guidance** and disclosing that **CFO Nicholas Brazis resigned** for a private-company role. A guidance cut plus a CFO departure on the same morning is a two-headache setup that tends to have a multi-day tail. Read-through already hitting peer **Hayward (HAYW) -6.3%**. Play: momentum-short / read-through trade, not a dip-buy — let it prove it can't reclaim the gap. Risk: 20% gap-downs can see violent oversold bounces; don't chase the short late, and beware a relief pop if management reassures on the call.

**4. BlackRock — BLK — Long**
BLK climbed **~3.8%** on a strong Q2: **EPS $13.91 vs $12.67 expected**, revenue **+31% y/y to $7.08B**. The world's largest asset manager beating on both lines confirms fee/AUM momentum and rides the same "quality financials work" theme that powered Tuesday's bank tape. Play: long the best-in-class beat; add on dips that hold the gap. Risk: BLK has already run into the print, so profit-taking after the initial pop is possible even on great numbers.

**5. BridgeBio Pharma — BBIO — Long (momentum/catalyst)**
BBIO has been trading up **~17%** after its **Phase 3 PROPEL 3 trial of oral infigratinib in pediatric achondroplasia hit primary and key secondary endpoints** with a clean safety profile — with an FDA NDA planned for Q3 2026. This is a genuine de-risking event with a near-term regulatory catalyst on deck. Play: momentum long on orderly consolidation; the data plus a filing timeline is a multi-week story. Risk: biotech that's already run can chop 10%+ intraday — keep size disciplined and don't chase vertical.
