# Stock Watchlist — Monday, November 23, 2026

**The big picture today:** The stock market is bouncing back hard this morning after a brutal week. Last week was the worst week for the Nasdaq (the index full of tech and chip companies) since the COVID crash in March 2020 — it dropped about 10%. This morning, money is rushing back into the beaten-down tech and chip stocks. Two things are driving the mood: (1) big computer-chip and AI stocks like Nvidia are jumping, and (2) a huge cybersecurity company (CrowdStrike) announced it is buying a rival (Zscaler).

One important thing to understand: the U.S. Federal Reserve (the people who set interest rates) is expected to *raise* rates again at its December 9 meeting — traders see about a 96% chance of a small quarter-point hike. Higher rates are usually bad for risky stocks, so this bounce could be fragile. Today looks like a relief rally (a snap-back after a big drop), not necessarily the all-clear. Be careful chasing things that have already run up a lot this morning.

---

## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. Zscaler (ZS) — up about 13% to 17% this morning (around $255–$260)**
This is the clearest story of the day. CrowdStrike agreed to buy Zscaler in a deal worth about $43.9 billion, paying with its own stock. Each Zscaler share is set to be worth roughly $260 — that's a 34% jump over where it closed Friday. **What to do:** The easy, big money has mostly already happened this morning. Once a buyout is announced, the stock usually settles close to the agreed price and stops moving much, because the deal won't actually close until late 2027 and still needs approval from shareholders and government regulators. If you buy now near $255–$260, your upside is small and you're mostly betting the deal goes through. The real risk is if regulators block it — then the stock could fall back hard. This is more of a "watch and understand" name than a great new buy today.

**2. Nvidia (NVDA) — up about 6% to 8% this morning**
Nvidia is the biggest chip company in the world and the heart of the AI boom. It's bouncing today along with the rest of the chip group. There's also drama: the CEO, Jensen Huang, resigned on November 12 after a personal scandal, and President Trump publicly asked the board over the weekend to hire him back. The board has said no. **What to do:** If you believe the AI story is still intact, this bounce could continue short-term. But be careful — the stock just had its worst stretch since 2008, leadership is unsettled, and Washington is also investigating the company over chip export rules. This is a buy only if you can stomach big daily swings and watch it closely. Don't put in money you can't afford to see drop 10% in a day.

**3. Strategy (MSTR) — up about 9% this morning**
This company (formerly called MicroStrategy) is basically a giant pile of Bitcoin wrapped in a stock. Bitcoin fell hard last week to around $55,000, then bounced back to about $61,000, and this stock jumped with it. **What to do:** This is a high-risk way to bet that Bitcoin keeps recovering. Important warning: the company owns its Bitcoin at an average price of about $75,000 per coin — well above today's ~$61,000 — so it's underwater. One well-known analyst even warned Bitcoin could fall much further. Only consider buying if you specifically want to bet on a Bitcoin rebound over the next week or two, and keep the position small. If Bitcoin rolls over again, this stock falls faster than Bitcoin itself.

**4. CrowdStrike (CRWD) — down about 8% this morning**
This is the *buyer* in today's big cybersecurity deal. Its stock is falling because it's paying a rich price and giving up a chunk of ownership to do the deal. **What to do:** When a buyer's stock drops on a deal, it can sometimes be a chance to buy a strong company at a discount — *if* you believe the combined company (over $10 billion in yearly subscription revenue) will be a cybersecurity powerhouse. This is a "consider buying the dip" idea for someone who already likes CrowdStrike, but give it a few days — these deal-day drops can keep sliding before they settle.

**5. Oracle (ORCL) — up about 5% this morning**
Oracle is a big software and cloud-computing company that has become a key player in renting out computing power for AI. It rose today after telling investors it expects to generate more than $30 billion in spare cash per year by 2029. **What to do:** This is a steadier AI play than the wild chip names. Consider buying if it keeps climbing and you want exposure to the AI buildout without the extreme swings of Nvidia. Lower risk than the others on this list, but still tied to how the whole AI trade behaves.

## Big Movers (up or down about 40% or more today)

**Capricor Therapeutics (CAPR) — down about 69%**
This small biotech crashed after U.S. health regulators (the FDA) rejected its cell therapy for Duchenne muscular dystrophy — for the *second* time — citing concerns about the quality of its trial data. The company disagrees with the decision. **Risk warning:** This is a tiny, now badly damaged stock. It may look "cheap" after a 69% drop, but there is no quick fix here — the company has to go back and work with regulators, which can take a very long time or fail entirely. Do not try to catch this falling knife hoping for a quick bounce; small biotechs in this situation can keep grinding lower.

## Also Watching (5 more)

**AMD (AMD) — up about 4% to 6%.** The number-two chip company, bouncing with the whole group today. A simpler way to bet on the chip rebound than Nvidia. Worth watching if chips keep climbing this week.

**Broadcom (AVGO) — up about 5%.** Another major chip and networking company riding today's rebound. Same idea as AMD — it moves with the AI-chip crowd.

**Micron (MU) — up about 5% to 7%.** Makes memory chips that AI computers need lots of. One of the strongest bouncers today. Good to watch, but it's a volatile name that swings hard in both directions.

**Marathon Digital / MARA (MARA) — up about 7%.** A Bitcoin mining company, moving up with Bitcoin's bounce. Note: the company recently sold about $1.1 billion of its own Bitcoin. Like Strategy above, this is a risky, short-term Bitcoin bet — only for small positions.

**SELLAS Life Sciences (SLS).** This small biotech had a big win — its late-stage trial for a leukemia treatment met its main goal (patients lived meaningfully longer), and the stock had its best week in six years. Watch for follow-through, but remember it has already run up a lot this year, so it can be choppy as early investors take profits.
