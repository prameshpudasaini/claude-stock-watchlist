In plain terms: it's a hopeful morning after a nervous week, but the calm is fragile. Keep your bets small and don't chase anything that has already shot straight up.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Akamai Technologies (ticker: AKAM) — the deal everyone is talking about
**What's happening:** Akamai, a company that helps deliver websites and cloud computing quickly, signed a 7-year, $11.6 billion contract to supply computing power to Anthropic, one of the leading AI companies. The deal could grow to about $20 billion, and Anthropic even received the right to buy a slice of Akamai's stock. The shares jumped roughly 21%–23% before the market opened.
**Why watch it today:** This is the single biggest piece of news in the market this morning. A jump this big on a real, long-term contract often keeps a stock moving and active for several days.
**What to consider:** If it holds its big gain or keeps climbing after the open, some traders will want to ride that momentum. But be careful: the stock has already made a huge one-day leap, so a lot of the good news is now baked in. Buying after a 20%+ pop is risky — if early buyers take their profits, it can pull back fast. Smaller and later is safer here than big and early.

### 2. IonQ (ticker: IONQ) — the quantum computing story keeps running
**What's happening:** IonQ (a leader in quantum computing, a new and very powerful kind of computer) announced two exciting things this week: a technical breakthrough in fixing computing errors, and that one of its machines will be connected to Nvidia's chips at a new Nvidia research center. The stock rose about 10%–11% on the news and was up again today to roughly $45.
**Why watch it today:** Big, exciting news like this often keeps a stock and its whole group moving for several days. Other quantum names (D-Wave, ticker QBTS; Rigetti, ticker RGTI) are rising alongside it.
**What to consider:** Consider buying if it keeps going up, but keep the amount small. These companies make very little money today, so the stock runs on hope and excitement — which means it can drop just as sharply as it rises.

### 3. CrowdStrike (ticker: CRWD) — cybersecurity is the quiet hot streak
**What's happening:** Companies that protect against hackers have been on a strong run for weeks, helped by a growing worry that AI creates brand-new ways to be attacked. CrowdStrike, one of the biggest names in the field, has been climbing and just held its big customer conference where it launched several new AI-security products. Several Wall Street firms raised their price targets on it.
**Why watch it today:** This is a whole group moving together (also strong: Palo Alto Networks, ticker PANW; Fortinet, ticker FTNT), and steady, multi-week strength is often calmer and more reliable than a single big one-day pop.
**What to consider:** Consider buying if it keeps rising, as a way to play the AI boom that isn't a pricey chip stock. The risk: these stocks are not cheap, so on a bad market day they can give back gains quickly. (One name in the group, Zscaler, ticker ZS, is actually down about 3.5% today on a management shake-up — a reminder that even hot groups have weak spots.)

### 4. Advanced Micro Devices (ticker: AMD) — a big winner taking a breather
**What's happening:** AMD, a major computer-chip maker, has been on a tear — up roughly 185% this year — and recently became one of only four U.S. chip companies worth more than $1 trillion. This morning it's down about 2% as some investors cash in profits. There's no bad news; it's just a pause after a huge run.
**Why watch it today:** With the AI-plumbing excitement from the Akamai deal lifting chip stocks, a small dip in a strong leader can be a chance to get in at a slightly better price.
**What to consider:** Consider buying on this small dip *if* chip stocks stay strong through the day — a healthy pause is normal after a big climb. The risk works both ways: a stock up this much this fast can also keep sliding if profit-taking snowballs, so don't rush, and keep it small.

### 5. Mirum Pharmaceuticals (ticker: MIRM) — a coin-flip event lands tomorrow
**What's happening:** The FDA (the agency that approves medicines) is expected to decide by tomorrow, September 26, whether to approve Mirum's drug for a rare bone disease. This kind of yes-or-no ruling is a true "all or nothing" moment for a small drug company.
**Why watch it today:** The decision is imminent, so the stock could move sharply today as traders position ahead of it.
**What to consider:** This is a gamble, not a steady bet — be honest with yourself about that. If the FDA says yes, the stock can jump; if it says no, it can drop hard, and the fall usually hurts more than the rise helps. Only consider a small amount you're fully prepared to lose, and understand you're betting on a coin flip with the result out of your hands. Many careful traders simply watch this one from the sidelines.
