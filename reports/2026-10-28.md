## The Big Picture Today (read this first)

Two huge things land **today** and they will push the whole market around:

1. **The Federal Reserve announces its interest-rate decision at 2:00 PM Eastern.** The Fed is the group that sets the country's main interest rate. Almost everyone expects them to **leave rates where they are** (a range of 3.75%–4.00%) rather than cutting or raising. The thing that actually moves stocks will be what the Fed chair *says* afterward — specifically, whether they hint at raising rates again in December. If they sound worried about inflation (prices rising too fast), stocks could drop; if they sound relaxed, stocks could jump. **Expect a sudden, sharp swing right after 2:00 PM.**

2. **Microsoft (MSFT) and Alphabet/Google (GOOGL) report their quarterly results after the market closes today.** These are two of the biggest companies in the world, and they're at the center of the artificial-intelligence (AI) spending boom. Traders are betting on moves of roughly **5% for Microsoft and 6% for Google** once the numbers come out. Because they're so large, how they move tomorrow morning can drag the entire market — and especially other tech and chip stocks — up or down with them.

**What this means for you:** Today is a day where the *market itself* is the main event. If you don't want a wild ride, it's okay to sit on your hands until after the Fed speaks and after the big tech results are out. If you do want to trade, size your bets small and know that volatility (big up-and-down swings) will be unusually high.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Vertiv (VRT) — consider buying if it keeps climbing
- **Move:** Up about **15–16%** before the market opened (to roughly the mid-$160s area).
- **Why:** Vertiv makes the cooling systems and power equipment that keep AI data centers running. It just reported much stronger profits than expected ($2.21 per share vs. the ~$1.82 Wall Street looked for), beat on sales ($4.31 billion), and **raised its full-year forecast**. The CEO said the data-center buildout is *accelerating*. It also just completed a $1.45 billion purchase of another company (UtilityInnovation Group).
- **Plain-English take:** This is one of the clearest "AI is still booming" signals today. If the stock holds its big jump and the overall market is calm after the Fed, it could be a good one to **buy and ride** for a few days. **Risk:** it already jumped a lot, so a bad Fed surprise or a weak Microsoft/Google report tonight could knock it back.

### 2. Enphase Energy (ENPH) — a possible bet-against, or avoid
- **Move:** Down about **17–18%** before the open.
- **Why:** Enphase makes equipment for home solar panels. It warned that demand for home solar is weak because higher interest rates make solar loans expensive and a federal tax credit for homeowners has expired. Its forecast for this quarter was gloomy (sales could fall another 10%).
- **Plain-English take:** The problems here are real and not a one-day thing. If it keeps sliding, it **could be a one to bet against** (betting it falls further). But betting against a stock that already dropped 17% is dangerous — any small bounce can hurt you fast. Simplest move for most people: **stay away** rather than try to catch it.

### 3. Microsoft (MSFT) — wait for tonight, then decide
- **Move:** Roughly flat before the open; the real action is **after the close today** when it reports.
- **Why:** Microsoft is a core AI winner through its cloud business (Azure) and its partnership with OpenAI. Traders expect about a **5% swing** on the results.
- **Plain-English take:** Don't guess ahead of the number — that's a coin flip. The smarter play is to **watch the report tonight**: if the cloud business grew strongly and the stock jumps, buying into that strength tomorrow can work. If cloud growth disappoints, it could drag all tech down. **Patience beats gambling here.**

### 4. Alphabet / Google (GOOGL) — same plan as Microsoft
- **Move:** Roughly flat before the open; reports **after the close today** alongside Microsoft.
- **Why:** Google is riding the AI wave through its search ads, cloud business, and its Gemini AI. Traders expect an even bigger swing of about **6%**.
- **Plain-English take:** Like Microsoft — **don't bet before the number.** React to it. Strong ad and cloud growth tomorrow could make it a good short-term buy; a miss could mean trouble. Watching both MSFT and GOOGL together also tells you the mood of the whole AI trade for the next week or two.

### 5. Corning (GLW) — consider buying on steady strength
- **Move:** Up roughly **1.5% to 3%** before the open.
- **Why:** Corning makes specialty glass and, importantly, the fiber-optic cables that connect AI data centers. It beat expectations with about $5 billion in sales. It's a quieter way to play the same AI-infrastructure theme as Vertiv, without the huge one-day spike.
- **Plain-English take:** A **steadier buy candidate** than the big movers. The gain is modest, so there's less risk of an immediate reversal. Good for someone who wants exposure to the AI buildout without chasing a stock that already rocketed. **Risk:** it's still a tech-linked name, so it'll follow the market's reaction to the Fed and tonight's big reports.
