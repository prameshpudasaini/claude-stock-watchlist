## The big picture this morning (read this first)

Three things are pushing the market around today, and almost every pick below ties back to one of them:

1. **A big warning about AI.** Over the weekend, some of the most famous leaders in artificial intelligence — including the CEOs of Anthropic (Dario Amodei), OpenAI (Sam Altman), and Elon Musk — said companies are building powerful AI too fast and should slow down for safety reasons. Traders took that as a sign that spending on AI computer chips and data centers might cool off. So chip stocks are falling hard this morning, while companies that protect against AI going wrong — cybersecurity firms — are going up.

2. **Oil is jumping.** Oil prices shot up about 3% (Brent crude near $107 a barrel) after Saudi Arabia shut down a key pipeline and tensions with Iran in the Middle East got worse. When oil rises, oil and gas company stocks usually rise with it.

3. **The Federal Reserve meets this Wednesday (Sept. 16).** This is unusual: instead of cutting interest rates, there's a roughly coin-flip chance (around 50–56%) that the Fed will *raise* rates by a quarter-point because expensive oil is pushing prices up. Higher interest rates are generally bad for expensive tech and growth stocks. That's an extra reason the tech-heavy Nasdaq is weak today and why the next few days could stay choppy.

Bottom line: money is moving *out* of computer-chip and big-tech names and *into* oil, cybersecurity, and safer corners of the market. The overall market is down modestly (the S&P 500 down about half a percent, the Nasdaq down more than 1%).

## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. Rum Group (ticker: RUM) — up about 28% before the market opened. Consider buying, but carefully.**
This is the company that used to be called Rumble (a video and cloud-computing company). News came out that Anthropic, the AI company, signed a giant $13.7 billion deal to use Rum Group's computing power. Rum had hinted at a huge mystery contract back in August but hadn't said who the customer was — now we know. That's a real, company-changing amount of money. The idea: if you think the excitement has more room to run this week, this is a buy. The catch: the stock already jumped a lot before you can even buy it, so you'd be paying a much higher price than Friday. Big first-day pops like this often give back some of the gain in the days after, so keep any bet small.

**2. Diamondback Energy (ticker: FANG) — up about 2% early. Consider buying while oil is spiking.**
Oil is surging because of the Middle East pipeline shutdown, and oil producers make more money when oil is expensive. Diamondback is one of the strongest U.S. oil drillers and was leading the energy group higher this morning (alongside APA, EOG Resources, and Marathon Petroleum). If you'd rather spread your bet across many energy companies instead of picking one, you can buy the **Energy Select Sector SPDR Fund (ticker: XLE)**, a basket of big oil and gas stocks that was up more than 1%. The risk: if the Middle East situation calms down, oil can fall back just as fast, and these stocks would drop with it.

**3. CrowdStrike (ticker: CRWD) — up about 5.4% early. Consider buying.**
CrowdStrike is a cybersecurity company (it protects computer systems from hackers). With everyone suddenly worried that AI is moving too fast and could be dangerous, money is flowing into companies that keep systems safe. Palo Alto Networks (ticker: PANW), a similar cybersecurity firm, was also up more than 4%. This is a "the news happens to help us" trade — if AI-safety worries stay in the headlines this week, these names can keep climbing. The risk: if the whole market keeps sinking, even these winners can get pulled down.

**4. Scholar Rock (ticker: SRRK) — up about 9%, near $60.45. Consider buying on real, lasting good news.**
The U.S. Food and Drug Administration approved Scholar Rock's drug ISEMBYLD (also called apitegromab) — the first muscle-targeting treatment for spinal muscular atrophy, a serious muscle-wasting disease, in adults and kids age two and up. This is a big deal: it turns Scholar Rock from a company that only had experimental drugs into one that can actually sell a product. Several Wall Street analysts raised their target prices right after (Truist to $76, Citi to $74, Raymond James to $72), all above today's price. Unlike a rumor, a drug approval is a solid, permanent event, which makes this a steadier pick than most. The risk: the stock already moved up, and how fast the drug sells is still unknown.

**5. Betting against computer chips (ticker: SOXS) — a way to profit if chips keep falling.**
This one is for people who think the chip-stock slide has further to go this week. SOXS is a fund built to go *up* when semiconductor (computer-chip) stocks go *down* — and it moves three times as fast as the chip group, in the opposite direction. With Intel down about 6%, Marvell down over 7%, and AMD down about 5% this morning on the AI-slowdown fears, plus a possible Fed rate hike Wednesday, chips could stay under pressure. **Important warning:** these "3x" funds are turbo-charged and lose value quickly if you're wrong or if you hold them too long. This is a short-leash, few-days trade only — not something to buy and forget.
