## Market Backdrop (what happened into the weekend)

Wall Street closed a losing week. **S&P 500 fell 1.0% Friday to 7,457.69** (‑1.6% on the week), the **Nasdaq dropped 1.4% to 25,520** (‑2.9% on the week), and the **Dow fell 407 pts to 52,146**. Two forces drove it:

- **Semiconductors got smoked.** Chips led a global sell-off on fear that AI hyperscalers may throttle capex, amplified by cheaper/better Chinese AI models (Moonshot's new Kimi release). The AI-spend trade is being questioned for the first time in a while.
- **Oil is spiking on war.** U.S. Central Command struck Iran for a 7th straight night. **WTI +2.8% to ~$81**, Brent +4.6%. That helps energy, hurts everything rate-sensitive, and adds an inflation risk into the week.

The week ahead is light on economic data but **very heavy on earnings** — Tesla and Alphabet both report Wednesday after the close, Intel Thursday, plus GM, IBM, Verizon, AT&T, Lockheed, and Nokia. This is the setup that matters.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. Energy — Long (momentum)**
*XLE ~$96 area / oil names SLB, HAL, XOM; USO for direct crude exposure.*
Oil is the cleanest trend in the market right now. Seven straight nights of U.S. strikes on Iran and WTI back over $81 keep a bid under crude and energy equities while tech bleeds. This is the sector rotation of the moment. **Direction: long energy on any dip; trail stops — if Iran headlines cool, oil can give back the war premium fast.**

**2. Tesla (TSLA) — Watch (earnings Wed 7/22 after close)**
Street wants revenue ~$26.4B (+17% YoY) and EPS ~$0.54. TSLA is a binary event Wednesday night — huge implied move. **Direction: watch, don't chase into the print. Position after guidance (deliveries/margins/robotaxi commentary) rather than gambling on the number.**

**3. Alphabet (GOOGL) — Watch (earnings Wed 7/22 after close)**
The single most important AI read of the week. Street wants EPS ~$2.90 (+25%) on revenue ~$116.9B (+21%). With semis selling off on capex fears, GOOGL's cloud growth and 2027 capex guide will either calm or confirm the AI-spend worry — and set the tone for the whole complex. **Direction: watch; a strong capex/cloud guide is a potential relief rally for beaten-down semis (NVDA, AVGO, AMD).**

**4. Intel (INTC) — Watch (earnings Thu 7/23)**
Story stock into the print: Q2 is expected to be Intel's **first GAAP-profitable quarter after six straight losses** (~$0.22 EPS vs a year-ago loss, revenue ~$14.4B, +12%). But it's caught in the semi downdraft (‑4% Friday on heavy volume). **Direction: watch for a turnaround beat; high risk given sector tape.**

**5. Semis short / SOXS — Watch for short (hedge)**
If you lean bearish tech, semis are the weakest group: AI-capex doubt plus Chinese-model competition. **SOXS (‑3x semis)** is the vehicle. **Direction: watch for short/hedge, but size small — a single strong Alphabet capex line Wednesday can squeeze this hard. Not a hold-through-earnings position.**
