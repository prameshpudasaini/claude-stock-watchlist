# Short-Term Stock Watchlist — September 8, 2026

*For someone who wants to buy today and hold anywhere from 1 to 14 days. Written in plain English. This is research and education, not financial advice. Short-term trading is risky, and small, thinly-traded stocks are the riskiest of all — they can fall as fast as they rose.*

---

## The mood this morning (read this first)

Two big things are driving everything today:

1. **Oil is spiking.** Over the weekend and overnight, attacks by Houthi fighters hit Saudi Arabian energy sites (reportedly wounding dozens of people) and forced some oil operations to stop. That, plus rising tension between the U.S. and Iran around the Strait of Hormuz (the narrow sea lane much of the world's oil passes through), sent oil prices to their highest level in about three months. Brent crude (the main global oil price) is pushing toward $100 a barrel. This is good for oil and defense companies and bad for almost everyone else, because expensive oil means higher costs and more inflation worry.

2. **Interest rates are a headache.** Strong U.S. jobs data has investors worried the Federal Reserve (the U.S. central bank) may keep rates high or even raise them, instead of cutting. Government bond yields are high (the 10-year Treasury near 4.80%). High rates tend to hurt expensive, fast-growing tech stocks and speculative bets like crypto.

The result: Dow futures are down a few hundred points, the S&P 500 is slightly lower, and the Nasdaq is barely higher. Money is flowing **into** oil and defense and **out of** risky, rate-sensitive names. There is also fresh worry about a trade fight with Canada. Keep position sizes small today.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. IonQ (IONQ) — Quantum computing — *Worth watching; consider a small buy, but know the risk*
IonQ holds its big **Investor Day today at 12:30 p.m. Eastern**, live from the New York Stock Exchange. This is the day the company is expected to give, for the first time ever, a multi-year sales forecast that folds in its recently bought business, SkyWater Technology. The stock has been climbing for days into this event (it jumped about 9% in one recent session), and a Wall Street firm (Jefferies) flagged today as the next big catalyst.
- **Why it matters today:** A strong, specific forecast could push the stock up further. 
- **The catch (important):** When a stock runs up *before* good news, it often falls *after* the news comes out — traders call this "selling the news." If you buy, keep it small, and understand that even a good presentation can lead to a drop simply because everyone already expected it.

### 2. Lockheed Martin (LMT) — Defense — *Consider buying*
With war worries rising in the Middle East, defense (weapons and aerospace) companies are in favor. On top of that, the bank UBS just upgraded Lockheed to "Buy" this morning with a price target of $674. When oil and conflict headlines heat up, big defense names like this tend to attract steady buying.
- **Why it matters today:** Real news catalyst (the upgrade) plus a supportive backdrop (Middle East tension).
- **The catch:** If tensions suddenly calm down, these gains can fade. This is a calmer, lower-drama pick than most on this list.

### 3. ExxonMobil (XOM) — Oil & gas — *Consider buying*
Exxon is the most straightforward way to ride today's oil spike. When oil prices jump the way they have, the biggest oil producers usually make more money, and their stocks tend to rise with the price of crude. Analysts are pointing to Exxon as a top pick among energy names right now.
- **Why it matters today:** Oil at a three-month high directly helps Exxon's profits.
- **The catch:** If the oil scare eases (say a ceasefire or the Saudi facilities come back online), oil prices could drop fast and take the stock down with them. You are really betting on the oil price staying high for the next week or two.

### 4. Roivant Sciences (ROIV) — Biotech — *Consider buying if it keeps climbing, but be careful*
Roivant jumped about **24%** in early trading after a drug from one of its units (a treatment called mosliciguat, for a serious lung-and-blood-pressure condition) showed strong, clear results in a mid-stage patient study. Good trial news like this can keep lifting a biotech stock for several days as more investors react.
- **Why it matters today:** A real, positive medical result — not hype.
- **The catch:** Biotech stocks are jumpy. A large part of the good news may already be in the price after a 24% pop. If you buy, it is usually safer to wait for it to show it can hold its gains rather than chasing the first spike.

### 5. The Trade Desk (TTD) — Advertising technology — *Consider betting against it (expecting it to fall)*
The Trade Desk is being **removed from the S&P 500 index** (the list of 500 big U.S. companies that many huge funds must mirror). This change was announced late last week and takes effect around September 21. When a stock is kicked out of the index, the big funds that track it are forced to sell their shares, which can push the price down in the days around the change.
- **Why it matters today:** Forced selling is a mechanical, predictable pressure over the next couple of weeks.
- **The catch:** This is widely known, so some of the drop may already have happened. Betting against a stock (profiting if it falls) carries its own risks — if it unexpectedly rises, losses can grow. Keep it small.

## Big Movers

*Honest note: in my scan this morning I did not find a U.S.-listed stock that was clearly up or down about 40% or more on news. The biggest clean, news-driven moves were in the 12%–24% range. Here they are, with warnings.*

- **Roivant Sciences (ROIV), up ~24%:** Strong mid-stage trial results for a lung-disease drug (see Top 5 above). Risk: biotech stocks swing hard; much of the good news may already be priced in after a 24% jump.
- **Novartis (NVS), down ~12%:** The big drugmaker had **two** separate drug studies fail — one for a muscle-wasting disease and one related to cholesterol/heart risk. This dragged down other drug stocks too (Amgen, Eli Lilly, Ionis, Dyne, Sarepta). Risk: failed trials are a real, lasting negative, not a quick dip — be cautious about assuming it "bounces back" fast.
- **Boston Scientific (BSX), down ~2%:** The medical-device maker warned that a recent cyber attack will likely have a "material" (meaning significant) hit to its third-quarter and full-year 2026 results. Risk: the full financial damage may not be clear yet, so more bad news could follow.

*If any tiny stock rockets 40%+ intraday on a single headline today, treat it with extreme caution: those moves often reverse violently, and thin trading means you can get stuck.*

## Quick reminders
- High oil and high interest rates are the two forces to respect today. They favor energy and defense, and hurt expensive tech and crypto.
- These are short-term ideas (1–14 days). Decide in advance how much you're willing to lose on each one.
