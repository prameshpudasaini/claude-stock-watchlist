# Stock Watchlist — Monday, October 26, 2026

*Written before the market opens (around 9:00 AM New York time). All prices and percent moves are early "pre-market" numbers (trading that happens before the official 9:30 AM open) and can change fast once regular trading starts. Always check a live price before you buy or sell anything.*

## The big picture today

Two things hang over the whole market this week, so keep them in mind no matter what you trade:

1. **The Federal Reserve meets Tuesday and Wednesday, with its decision on interest rates coming Wednesday, October 28, at 2:00 PM New York time.** The Fed's main interest rate is currently 3.50%–3.75%. Investors are unsure whether the Fed will cut rates a little or leave them alone, and that uncertainty can make the whole market jumpy. A surprise either way can swing stocks hard on Wednesday afternoon.
2. **A giant week of company results (earnings).** Microsoft (MSFT) and Alphabet/Google (GOOGL) report Wednesday after the close; Amazon (AMZN) and Intel (INTC) report Thursday. These are huge companies, so their results can push the whole market — especially anything tied to artificial intelligence (AI) and computer chips — up or down.

Early signs this morning are mildly positive: stock index futures (bets on where the market opens) are a touch higher, with Dow futures up roughly 150 points. The loudest single story is a sharp drop in Brazil-linked stocks after Brazil's weekend election.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. Range Resources (RRC) — natural gas company being bought out. Up about 10%–15% pre-market.**
A bigger energy company, EQT, agreed to buy Range Resources in a deal valued at more than $50 billion, paid in EQT shares. When one company agrees to buy another, the one being bought usually jumps toward the agreed price — that is what is happening here. **Plain-English take: this is the calmer, lower-risk idea on the list — consider buying if you want to ride a confirmed takeover.** But because it is an all-stock deal, Range's price will now move with EQT's price, and EQT fell about 4% this morning, so your gain depends partly on how EQT does. This is a slow, modest-upside idea, not a lottery ticket.

**2. Microsoft (MSFT) or Alphabet/Google (GOOGL) — both report results Wednesday after the close.**
These are two of the most important companies in the market, and investors badly want to know how much money they are making from AI versus how much they are spending on it. **Plain-English take: if you believe their AI results will impress, you could buy a little before Wednesday — but understand you are gambling on a surprise.** Earnings reactions are unpredictable; a great report can still send a stock down if investors expected even more. Safer choice: wait until after the numbers come out and trade the reaction instead of guessing ahead of it.

**3. Verizon (VZ) — phone and internet company. Up about 2% pre-market after a good earnings report.**
Verizon reported stronger-than-expected results and raised its outlook for the rest of the year. It is a steady, boring, dividend-paying company, so the move is small but backed by real news. **Plain-English take: consider buying if you want a lower-drama stock going up on solid results** — do not expect fireworks, just a steadier ride than the names above.

**4. Moderna (MRNA) — vaccine maker. Down about 7%–8% pre-market, and still sliding.**
Moderna keeps falling after disappointing late-stage results for one of its experimental cancer vaccines. When bad news like this hits, the selling can continue for several days as investors lower their expectations. **Plain-English take: this is one to bet against (or simply avoid) if it keeps dropping** — the news was a genuine setback. The risk the other way: beaten-down stocks sometimes bounce sharply for no clear reason, so do not assume it only goes down.

**5. Xencor (XNCR) — small biotech company. Up about 31% pre-market on early cancer-drug data.**
Xencor shared encouraging early-stage results for an experimental kidney-cancer treatment at a big medical conference. Small biotech stocks can jump hard on news like this. **Plain-English take: consider buying only a small amount if you want to chase the excitement** — but this is high-risk. Early data is not approval, the company is small, and a stock that jumps 30% in a day can give a lot of it back just as quickly. Only risk money you can afford to lose here.

## Big Movers (about 40% or more, on news)

**XP Inc (XP) — down roughly 46%–50% pre-market.** XP is a Brazilian investing/brokerage company whose U.S.-listed shares are collapsing after Brazil's presidential runoff election on Sunday. Investors had been betting on a business-friendly, reform-minded winner and had pushed these stocks up sharply in recent weeks; the opposite result (a Lula victory) is now reversing all of that in a single morning. **Risk warning: this is a violent, emotional move. A stock that falls this much can keep falling, but it can also snap back hard if the panic fades. Do not treat a huge one-day drop as a guaranteed bargain — these election-driven swings are extremely hard to time.**

*(Note: pre-market "most active" and "biggest mover" screens often list tiny, thinly-traded companies with 40%+ swings that have no clear, verifiable news behind them. I did not find a second large mover today with a clear, confirmed reason. Any tiny stock up or down 40%+ on no real news is dangerous — those moves reverse and crash just as fast as they spike, so treat them as gambling, not investing.)*

## Also Watching (5 more)

**Nu Holdings (NU) — down about 25% pre-market.** Another Brazilian financial company caught in the same election-driven selloff as XP. Same warning: big, emotional move that is hard to time; could keep falling or bounce.

**MercadoLibre (MELI) — down about 14% pre-market.** Latin America's big online shopping and payments company, also hit by the Brazil election result. Watch for a possible bounce if the selling is overdone, but there is no rush — let it settle first.

**Fair Isaac (FICO) — down about 9% pre-market.** The company behind FICO credit scores is falling after analysts lowered their ratings on the stock. **Could be one to bet against if it keeps sliding**, but a single downgrade day is not always the start of a long drop, so be cautious.

**Ross Stores (ROST) — up about 3%–7% pre-market.** The discount clothing retailer got an upgrade (a more positive rating) from a Wall Street firm. A steadier, lower-drama idea; **consider buying if it keeps rising**, but the move is modest.

**CoreWeave (CRWV) and other "AI cloud" stocks — falling pre-market.** These companies rent out AI computing power and are very jumpy. They dropped on a social-media comment that spooked investors. With Microsoft, Google and Amazon all reporting this week, this whole group could swing hard in either direction on AI spending news. **High-risk and headline-driven — watch, but do not bet big before the big earnings land.**
