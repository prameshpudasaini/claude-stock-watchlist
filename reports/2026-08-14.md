## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. Riot Platforms (RIOT) — Long (earnings + $9.1B AI deal) — up ~19% pre-market**
Beat on Q2 and, bigger, struck a reported **$9.1 billion deal with Anthropic** to sell AI data-center capacity — the clearest confirmation yet of the miner-to-AI-infrastructure pivot. This is the rare Bitcoin-adjacent name with a hard, dollar-figure catalyst rather than just a BTC-price beta. *Direction: long — the Anthropic contract re-rates the story from "crypto miner" to "AI landlord." Don't chase the opening spike; buy the hold above the gap and use a stop, since a +19% pop can give back the first move.*

**2. Nu Holdings (NU) — Long (record earnings) — up ~12–13% pre-market**
First-ever **$1B+ quarterly net income** ($1.1B, +49% YoY), gross revenue **$5.9B (+39%)** topping the ~$5.48B consensus, and a record 12.4% risk-adjusted NIM while scaling Mexico to 16M customers. Clean top-and-bottom beat from a profitable, fast-growing fintech — the kind of un-crowded winner that holds a gap. *Direction: long on strength; let the open settle, then buy the hold. Fundamentals-driven, so less prone to a same-day round-trip than a headline spike.*

**3. Reddit (RDDT) — Long / watch (S&P 500 inclusion) — up ~12% pre-market**
S&P Dow Jones Indices will add Reddit to the **S&P 500 before the open on Aug 18** — only the second pure-play social name in the index (after Meta). Index inclusion forces mechanical buying from passive funds, which puts a bid under the stock through Monday. Comes on top of a strong Q2 (revenue +61%). *Direction: long/watch into the Aug 18 add — this is an index-flow trade with a defined event date. Risk is the "buy the rumor, sell the inclusion" fade once passive buying is done, so plan an exit around the add date.*

**4. NIQ Global Intelligence (NIQ) — Long (beat + raised guidance) — up ~15% pre-market**
Q2 adjusted EPS **$0.27 vs $0.21 est**, revenue $1.12B edged past, and management **raised FY adjusted-EPS guidance to $1.08–$1.12** — midpoint ~12% above prior consensus. Consumer-intelligence/data-analytics name that isn't a crowded AI ticker. *Direction: long — a beat-and-raise from a lower-profile name leaves room to run if the tape stays risk-on; wait for the gap to hold above the open before entering.*

**5. Applied Materials (AMAT) — Watch (overreaction dip-buy) — down ~5% pre-market**
Beat Q3 ($3.50 EPS / $9.12B rev vs $3.40 / $8.99B) and guided Q4 well **above** consensus ($10.25B vs $9.54B; "unprecedented" demand), yet the stock fell on softer China mix (28% of sales, down from ~35%) and an already-extended run. *Direction: watch, not a reflex buy — the sell-off is expectations-driven, not a broken story. If it stabilizes above the pre-market low with strong Q4 guidance intact, it's an oversold-bounce candidate on the AI-capex picks-and-shovels theme. Skip if it keeps bleeding.*
