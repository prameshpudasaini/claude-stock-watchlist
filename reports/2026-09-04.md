## The one big story driving everything today

Today is all about **jobs and interest rates** — and the news just made the "the Federal Reserve might raise rates" fear worse.

- **The August jobs report came in hot.** The economy added **162,000 jobs** last month — way more than the roughly 55,000 experts expected. The unemployment rate stayed at 4.1%. A strong jobs number is usually good, but right now traders read it as "the economy is too warm, so the Fed has no reason to cut interest rates — and might even raise them."
- **The Fed meets in less than two weeks (September 16–17).** One Fed official (Governor Chris Waller) said this week he's watching next week's inflation report to decide whether to hold rates steady or raise them. So **next week's inflation number is the next big event** — bigger than today's jobs report for where stocks go from here.
- **Money is nervous about expensive tech.** When rates might go up, the priciest fast-growing stocks (big tech and computer-chip names) tend to wobble. Chip stocks have been jumpy and selling off on and off for the last two weeks.
- **Two clear winners are standing out from the crowd:** a company-by-company earnings season (some stocks are popping or crashing hard on their own results), and a fresh **rare-earths supply scare** with China after China quietly stopped some shipments to the U.S.

**Bottom line:** this is a stock-picker's market today. The broad market is drifting sideways and jumpy, but individual companies are making huge moves on their own news. The picks below are mostly about *specific* company stories, not the whole market.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. MP Materials (MP) — consider buying (but keep it small and expect big swings)**
MP is the main American rare-earths company. Rare earths are special metals needed to build electric-car motors, wind turbines, weapons, and electronics — and China controls more than 90% of the world's supply. This morning, news broke that **some Chinese companies have stopped shipping rare earths to the U.S.** over political tension, weeks before China's leader visits Washington. That's exactly the kind of scare that sends money rushing into the few non-Chinese suppliers like MP. The stock rose about 3% early and this theme can run for several days. **The catch:** these stocks are emotional and can drop just as fast if the two countries make peace or the shipments quietly restart, so don't chase it too hard.

**2. Samsara (IOT) — consider buying**
Samsara makes software and sensors that help trucking fleets and factories track their vehicles and equipment. It reported quarterly results last night that beat expectations, and — more importantly — it raised its outlook for the year. The stock jumped about **16% after hours to roughly $45**. Its yearly recurring revenue (the steady, repeating money it collects) is growing 30% a year, which is very healthy. When a software company beats *and* raises its forecast like this, the stock often keeps drifting up for several days. **The catch:** it already made a big one-day jump, so it may pause or dip first before going higher — don't buy in a panic.

**3. Lululemon (LULU) — consider betting against it, or simply avoid it**
The yoga-clothing maker had a brutal night. It **cut its full-year sales and profit forecast for the second time this year**, its U.S. sales fell 8%, and its core women's leggings sales dropped about 20%. The stock crashed roughly **18% to an 8-year low**. A brand-new CEO starts Monday, September 8, inheriting a mess. When a former-favorite brand keeps cutting its own numbers, the stock often keeps sliding as more experts lower their targets. This could be a good one to bet against if it keeps falling. **The catch:** after an 18% crash, stocks sometimes bounce for a day or two on bargain-hunting, so betting against it right at the bottom is risky.

**4. Smith & Wesson (SWBI) — consider buying**
The gun maker reported much stronger results than expected — earnings of $0.36 per share versus the $0.21 experts predicted, and sales well above forecasts. The stock jumped about **17%** to around $16. Earnings surprises this big often carry a stock higher for several days as the good news sinks in. **The catch:** it's a smaller, thinly-traded company, so it can swing hard in both directions — keep the position small.

**5. USA Rare Earth (USAR) — consider buying (higher risk than MP)**
This is a smaller, more speculative version of the rare-earths bet in pick #1. It rose about **4%** this morning on the same China-shipment-halt news. Because it's smaller than MP Materials, it tends to move faster — bigger jumps up, but also bigger drops. It's a way to bet harder on the rare-earths scare. **The catch:** smaller stocks like this can reverse violently, so treat it as a small, high-risk piece of the same theme rather than a core holding.
