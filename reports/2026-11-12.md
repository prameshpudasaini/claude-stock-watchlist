## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. Nvidia (NVDA) — down about 7–8% before the open
This is the story of the day. Reports say Nvidia's board met to decide whether to fire CEO Jensen Huang over reports about an affair. Nothing is confirmed yet, and the company hasn't made an official announcement. One analyst warned the stock could drop another 20% if he is actually removed, but it could also bounce back quickly if the board keeps him.

**What to do:** This is too much of a coin-flip to buy right now — wait until the board actually decides. If you like to take risks and think the bad news keeps coming, this could be one to bet against (bet it keeps falling), but understand that a single positive announcement could send it jumping up fast and burn anyone betting the wrong way. Safest move: watch and wait for clear news.

### 2. CoreWeave (CRWV) — jumped about 25% yesterday, giving back around 7% today
CoreWeave, a company that rents out AI computing power, reported very strong results: sales grew 168% from a year ago, and it has $132.5 billion in future orders lined up. The stock shot up 25% on the news, then pulled back a bit this morning as the whole Nvidia scare hit AI stocks.

**What to do:** Consider buying on this dip if you believe the AI spending boom continues — the actual business results were excellent. But be aware this stock moves wildly day to day and is tied closely to how Nvidia goes, so keep your position small.

### 3. Fossil Group (FOSL) — up about 25% on earnings
The watchmaker, written off as a dying business, just reported its first sales growth in four years. The stock has been on a tear, roughly doubling over the past few weeks, and company insiders have been buying shares themselves — usually a good sign.

**What to do:** Consider buying if it keeps climbing, as the turnaround story is real and the momentum is strong. Big warning: this is a small, risky stock that has already run up a lot, so it can drop sharply just as fast. Only risk money you can afford to lose.

### 4. Tetra Tech (TTEK) — up about 8% on earnings
This engineering and environmental consulting company beat expectations with its quarterly results (earnings of $0.49 per share on about $1.34 billion in sales). This is a steadier, less dramatic company than the AI names above.

**What to do:** Consider buying — a solid earnings beat with a calmer stock makes this one of the lower-drama ideas on today's list, good if you want less wild price swings.

### 5. AEVEX Aerospace (AVEX) — up about 14% on earnings
AEVEX, a drone and defense-technology company, reported strong quarterly results and raised its forecast for the rest of 2026. The defense and drone area has been a hot corner of the market.

**What to do:** Consider buying on the strong results and raised outlook. It's a smaller company, so expect bigger swings than a big-name stock — keep the position modest.
