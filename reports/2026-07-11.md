## Top 5 Picks for Today (setting up Monday July 13)

**1. JPMorgan Chase — JPM — Long / watch into Tuesday earnings**
JPM kicks off bank earnings Tuesday July 14, the same morning as June CPI. Financials have been a relative safe-haven while chips bled. A clean CPI print + solid net-interest-income guide could send the whole bank complex higher. Play: build/watch Monday, size for the Tuesday-morning double catalyst. Risk: hot core CPI sours the tape before banks even report.

**2. Circle Internet — CRCL — Long (momentum)**
Circle jumped **+13.3%** on **OCC approval** — a landmark giving stablecoin issuers a path to banking legitimacy. This is a genuine regulatory catalyst, not a meme spike, and it re-rates the whole stablecoin/crypto-adjacent group. Play: long on any orderly pullback; momentum intact. Risk: crowded, high-beta, headline-sensitive to crypto and rate moves.

**3. Delta Air Lines — DAL — Watch (airline read-through)**
Delta **beat** ($1.56 vs $1.53 EPS, record $17.7B revenue), **reaffirmed** a punchy full-year $6.50–$7.50 EPS guide (well above the ~$5.97 street), and **hiked the dividend 15%** — yet shares dipped ~1% on profit-taking (up 28% YTD). Strong print, quiet reaction = coiled. **United (UAL) reports Wednesday July 15**; a good Delta guide sets a positive tone for the group. Play: watch DAL for a base, or use UAL as the fresh catalyst. Risk: highest quarterly fuel cost in Delta history — margins tight if oil pops.

**4. Celcuity — CELC — Watch (binary biotech, PDUFA July 17)**
Hard catalyst this week: FDA decision on **gedatolisib** for HR+/HER2-/PIK3CA-wild-type advanced breast cancer, **PDUFA July 17**. Phase 3 data was strong (9.3 vs 2.0 mo PFS, HR 0.24). Priority Review + Breakthrough + Fast Track lean toward approval. Play: speculative long into the decision or an options straddle for the move. Risk: **binary** — an approval is likely partly priced, and any CRL/label surprise can gap it 30–50% either way. Not a swing setup, a defined-risk event bet.

**5. Semiconductors (SOXL / SOXS) — Watch both sides**
The chip tape is the market's fault line. **ASML (Wed) and TSMC (Thu)** are the referees on whether the "AI bubble" call sticks. Oversold bounce or fresh leg down both live here. Play: **SOXL** for a capitulation bounce trade, **SOXS** if ASML/TSMC guide cautiously — but wait for the print, don't pre-position into a 3x fund. Risk: leveraged ETFs decay; these are day-to-few-day trades only, not holds.
