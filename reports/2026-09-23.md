Stocks are roughly flat to slightly lower to start the day. A few things are pulling in different directions:

- **Quantum computing is the hot story.** A company called IonQ said it cracked a hard technical problem, and the whole group of quantum-computing stocks jumped in response.

## Top 5 Picks for Today

### 1. GRAIL (ticker: GRAL) — a make-or-break day
**What's happening:** Today, a panel of outside advisers to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (the FDA, the agency that approves medical products) meets and votes on GRAIL's "Galleri" test — a single blood test the company says can spot signs of up to 50 kinds of cancer. The stock already jumped about 33% on Monday because documents the FDA released ahead of the meeting looked encouraging.
**Why watch it today:** This is a true yes-or-no event. A positive vote from the advisers could send the stock higher; a negative or doubtful vote could knock it down hard. The vote and comments come out during the day.
**What to consider:** This is a gamble on the outcome, not a calm investment. If you want to bet on a favorable vote, understand the stock could fall sharply if the panel is unimpressed. Many careful traders prefer to wait until the vote result is clear rather than guess ahead of it.

### 2. IonQ (ticker: IONQ) — the quantum computing story of the week
**What's happening:** IonQ announced what it calls an industry-first: technology that fixes quantum-computing errors in real time using an ordinary computer chip. In plain terms, it says it removed a big roadblock to making quantum computers actually useful. The stock rose about 7% after hours and was up as much as 15% before today's open.
**Why watch it today:** Good news like this often keeps a stock moving for several days as more investors pile in, and the excitement is spreading to the whole quantum group.
**What to consider:** This could be worth buying if it keeps climbing on the news. But be careful — these stocks are expensive relative to how little money the companies actually make, so they can drop just as fast as they rise. Keep any position small.

### 3. Viking Therapeutics (ticker: VKTX) — riding strong weight-loss drug data
**What's happening:** Viking is developing a weight-loss drug (called VK2735) that works like the popular shots from Eli Lilly and Novo Nordisk. On Monday the company reported that patients kept most of their weight loss even after switching to less frequent doses, and the stock jumped about 31% to roughly $40. Several Wall Street analysts then raised their price targets to $120–$125.
**Why watch it today:** Big, positive drug news often keeps a stock elevated for a while, and this one has fresh analyst support behind it.
**What to consider:** Consider buying if it holds its gains or keeps rising. The risk: the actual late-stage trial results won't come until 2027, so this is running on hope more than final proof — expect bumps along the way.

### 4. Coinbase (ticker: COIN) — the easiest way to ride the Bitcoin wave
**What's happening:** Bitcoin pushed up to about $85,000, and Coinbase (the largest U.S. crypto trading exchange) jumped more than 10% to trade above $193. New rules that make it easier to trade tokenized versions of stocks are also helping.
**Why watch it today:** Coinbase tends to move in the same direction as Bitcoin, only more dramatically. As long as Bitcoin keeps climbing, Coinbase usually follows.
**What to consider:** Consider buying if Bitcoin keeps going up. The flip side: if Bitcoin turns down, Coinbase can fall much harder than Bitcoin itself, so watch the coin's price closely.

### 5. Micron Technology (ticker: MU) — big earnings coming next week
**What's happening:** Micron makes memory chips, which are in short supply and in heavy demand because of the AI boom. The stock is up a stunning 266% this year. It reports its quarterly results after the market closes on **September 30** — within your 1-to-14-day window — and management has already hinted at record numbers.
**Why watch it today:** Stocks often drift and get more active in the days leading into a big earnings report as traders position themselves.
**What to consider:** This is a bet on a strong report. The danger is that expectations are sky-high after such a huge run, so even good-but-not-perfect results could disappoint. If you buy ahead of the report, know it's a coin-flip on the day and consider whether you'd rather wait for the numbers.
