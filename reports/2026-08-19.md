## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. Moderna (MRNA) — up ~83–86% pre-market — LONG / MOMENTUM (see risk note)**
The story of the day. Moderna and Merck's personalized mRNA cancer vaccine (Intismeran, paired with Keytruda) hit **both endpoints in the Phase 3 INTerpath-001 melanoma trial** (1,137 resected stage IIB–IV patients) — the first-ever positive late-stage readout for an mRNA cancer vaccine, cutting recurrence and spread vs. Keytruda alone. On top of that, the FDA cleared Moderna's new flu shot (mFLUSIVA), giving it a fourth approved product. This is a genuine platform-validation catalyst, not a low-float pop. *Risk: an 80%+ single-day gap invites violent profit-taking; a hold works best entered on a pullback, not chasing the open tick.*

**2. Merck (MRK) — up ~8.7% pre-market — LONG**
The lower-beta, cleaner way to play the same catalyst. Merck co-owns the melanoma win (Keytruda is the backbone), and an 8–9% move in a $200B+ mega-cap is far more durable than an 80% move in a former COVID name. For a 1–14 day hold, MRK carries a fraction of MRNA's reversal risk while still riding the vaccine headline. Long-biased.

**3. Target (TGT) — Q2 beat, guidance raised — LONG / WATCH**
Q2 net sales +5.3%, comps +3.8% on +3.6% traffic, and FY guidance raised (EPS now $9.90–$10.90). Stock came in near $154, up ~55% YTD. *Caveat: reported EPS of $4.11 includes ~$1.65 from a $994M tariff refund — strip that out and the beat is smaller, so watch the reaction rather than the headline.* Retail is a leadership pocket, but the bar is high; long on a clean hold above the pre-earnings level, otherwise watch.

**4. Lowe's (LOW) — down ~3%+ pre-market — WATCH FOR SHORT / FADE**

Clean "cut-guidance" fade setup and a sympathy read on the housing/DIY consumer. Watch for continuation lower; note pro/online strength (+15.7% online) caps the downside.

**5. TJX Companies (TJX) — down ~4.6% pre-market — WATCH FOR SHORT / FADE**
