# 2026-08-24 — Monday Trading Watchlist

> **Quick picture of the day:** The market is starting the week a little nervous. Futures (bets on where the market opens) point slightly down — S&P 500 about -0.1%, the tech-heavy Nasdaq about -0.4%, Dow about -0.1%. Two fresh worries are weighing on things: the U.S. put new sanctions on Iran over the weekend, and trade talks with Canada broke down with both sides threatening new tariffs (import taxes). But the real story is what's coming later this week — it's a huge one. **Nvidia reports its quarterly results Wednesday after the market closes**, the **Jackson Hole economic conference** (where the head of the Federal Reserve speaks) runs Thursday through Saturday with **new Fed Chair Kevin Warsh giving his first big speech Friday**, and **Friday morning brings the PCE inflation report** (the Fed's favorite measure of how fast prices are rising). Because so much lands later, today can be choppy and headline-driven. Bitcoin is still shaky after a sharp weekend drop to around $77,000, which keeps the crypto-related stocks jumpy.
>
> *Prices below are pre-market or last-known levels — always confirm the live price before you buy or sell anything.*

## Top 5 Picks for Today

**1. Nvidia (NVDA) — Watch and get ready; don't gamble on the earnings gamble yet**
- **Why it matters today:** Nvidia is the most important stock in the market right now, and it reports its results **Wednesday after the close**. Everyone is watching what it says about AI spending, its new "Rubin" chip, and its business in China. This one report can move the entire market and every chip stock with it.
- **What to do:** **Watch and prepare, but be careful.** The report is basically a coin flip in the short term — a great result can send it flying, a cautious outlook can sink the whole chip group. Many careful traders don't make a big bet right before the report; they wait to see the reaction Wednesday night/Thursday and trade that instead. If you do buy ahead of it, keep it small because the swing can be violent both ways.

**2. PDD Holdings (PDD) — Watch today's earnings reaction; wait for the result before betting**
- **Price/setup:** Reports its quarterly results **before the market opens this morning.** Wall Street expects about $2.77 in earnings per share on roughly $17.1 billion in sales.
- **Why it matters today:** PDD owns Temu, the cheap-shopping app. The big fear is shrinking profits — Temu is spending heavily on marketing and shipping to grow overseas, and that eats into earnings. Analysts have been slashing their price targets (Barclays cut to $89, Macquarie to $87). PDD's reaction can swing the whole group of Chinese company stocks.
- **What to do:** **Watch, then react.** This is a "let the news come out first" situation. If profits hold up better than feared, the stock (and other China names) can bounce; if margins look worse, it can drop hard. Don't guess ahead of the number.

**3. Alibaba (BABA) — Lean against it / avoid buying the dip today**
- **Price:** About **$116.60 in pre-market, down roughly 3–4%** (and down about 8.5% in Hong Kong trading).
- **Why it matters today:** Alibaba just announced it is selling about **$10.2 billion of new stock** to raise cash for its AI push. Selling lots of new shares waters down (dilutes) what existing shareholders own, which is why the stock is falling. One analyst bluntly called it a "white flag," meaning the heavy AI spending and price wars are catching up to them.
- **What to do:** **Be cautious — this could be one to bet against or simply avoid today** while the new-share news sinks in. The bigger picture on Alibaba is still positive with most analysts rating it a buy, so this may become a buying chance *later* once the selling settles — but chasing it lower right now is risky.

**4. Coinbase (COIN) / Strategy (MSTR) — Watch; let Bitcoin prove itself before buying**
- **Why it matters today:** These two stocks move up and down with Bitcoin, and Bitcoin is still unsettled after a sharp weekend drop to around $77,000. MSTR in particular swings more than twice as hard as Bitcoin in both directions.
- **What to do:** **Watch, don't chase.** If Bitcoin holds above roughly $76,000 and climbs, these can bounce and could be worth buying. If Bitcoin slips back under that, expect more selling. Because they move so fast, keep any position small and set a clear exit point.

**5. Tesla (TSLA) — Consider buying on dips while the robotaxi story runs**
- **Price/setup:** Closed around **$363 on Friday** after its best week since May.
- **Why it matters today:** Tesla has a steady drip of good news on its self-driving taxi rollout — a new Las Vegas-area permit for up to 5,000 robotaxis, an upcoming "Cybercab" launch event, and a paid robotaxi fleet now running in seven U.S. cities. This is a real multi-headline story, not a one-day pop.
- **What to do:** **Consider buying on pullbacks** while the robotaxi news and heavy trading volume keep pushing it up. Watch the $345 area as a floor; the main risk is a bad headline (like a recall in China), so set a stop-loss to protect yourself.

## Big Movers (about 40% or more today)

- **Springview Holdings (SPHL) — up 500%+.** A tiny company that jumped after announcing a partnership to put rooftop solar panels on housing projects in Singapore. **Risk warning:** This is a very small, thinly-traded stock that recently did a reverse stock split to stay listed. Moves this big on one press release almost always give back a huge chunk fast — treat it as a lottery ticket, not an investment.
- **Callan JMB (CJMB) — up ~360%.** Another micro-cap ripping higher, but with no clearly confirmed news behind the move. **Risk warning:** When a tiny stock triples with no obvious reason, it is usually a short-lived trading frenzy that can crash just as quickly. Extremely dangerous to chase.
- **authID (AUID) — up ~130%.** A small identity-verification software company spiking sharply. **Risk warning:** Same story — tiny, jumpy, and prone to giving it all back. Only for people who accept they could lose most of it fast.
- **Sadot Group (SDOT) — up ~50%.** Jumped after clearing about $1.08 million in debt and pitching itself as a new "AI-powered" commodity-trading platform (TradeOS). **Risk warning:** This is a micro-cap re-branding around the AI buzzword; the actual revenue is tiny (~$1 million). Exciting story, very high risk of a sharp reversal.
- **BTC Digital (BTCT) — up ~35–80%.** A small crypto-miner riding two waves at once: a Bitcoin bounce and its own pitch to shift into AI computing (a completed 10-megawatt site in Georgia). **Risk warning:** Small crypto-linked stocks swing wildly with Bitcoin and hype — quick to spike, quick to drop.

*Note on the down side: no major stock is down 40%+ on news this morning, but see REGENXBIO below in "Also Watching" — it's down about 21% on a real setback.*

## Also Watching (5 more)

- **XPeng (XPEV) — Watch.** The Chinese electric-car maker reports results **before the open today**, right alongside PDD. It's an early read on how well EVs are selling in China; a strong number could lift the whole group, a weak one could drag it.
- **REGENXBIO (RGNX) — Down ~21%, be cautious.** The gene-therapy company dropped hard on a regulatory setback tied to its treatment for MPS II (a rare genetic disease). **Watch, don't rush to buy the dip** — bad regulatory news can keep pressuring a biotech stock for days.
- **CrowdStrike (CRWD) & Salesforce (CRM) — Watch.** Both report **Wednesday, the same day as Nvidia.** CrowdStrike is the tell for cybersecurity stocks; Salesforce is the read on big-company software and AI "agents." Get ready now, act on the reactions.
- **Intuit (INTU) — Watch.** The TurboTax and QuickBooks maker reports **Tuesday.** A useful early check on how much small businesses are spending and how its AI products are selling, ahead of the bigger software reports Wednesday.
- **Leveraged chip funds SOXL / SOXS — Watch for the Nvidia reaction only.** These funds amplify the daily move of chip stocks (SOXL goes up 3x when chips rise, SOXS goes up 3x when they fall). They're a way to trade Nvidia's Wednesday result — but they lose value fast if you're wrong, so they're for a quick move, not for holding blindly through the report.
