# Daily Trading Brief — Saturday, June 6, 2026 (Weekend Edition / Week-Ahead Setup)

**Heads up:** It's Saturday — U.S. cash markets are closed. This brief reads off **Friday's
violent close** and sets up the trade for the week of **June 8–12**. The single most important
thing to understand: the regime flipped. As recently as Thursday the tape was "choppy rotation."
Friday it became a genuine **risk-OFF, Fed-rate-HIKE scare.**

**What happened Friday (June 5):** May payrolls printed **+172k vs. ~88k expected** — more than
double — with unemployment steady at 4.3%. A hot labor market plus sticky inflation **killed the
rate-cut story and put a rate HIKE back on the table** (CME FedWatch now ~**67% odds of a hike by
December**). Treasury yields spiked, and that lit the fuse on an already-wobbly AI/chip complex
still bleeding from Broadcom's weak guidance. The result:

- **Nasdaq −4.18%** to 25,709 — its worst day since April 2025.
- **S&P 500 −2.64%** (worst day since October); **Dow −1.35%** (−695 pts).
- **PHLX Semiconductor Index (SOX) −10%+** — worst session since **March 2020**. ~**$1.3T** in chip
  market cap erased in a day: **NVDA −6%, AVGO −20% (two-day), AMD −11%, MU −13%, MRVL −17%.**
- **Bitcoin crashed below $64,000**, triggering ~**$1.1B in liquidations**; crypto-equity proxies broke.
- Oddly, **VIX stayed contained near ~15–16** — telling you the damage was **concentrated in
  semis/AI**, not yet a broad systemic panic.

**The week's pivot is Wednesday's CPI (June 10, 8:30 ET).** April ran hot (3.95% headline / 2.99%
core). With rate-hike odds already elevated, a **hot CPI = more downside**; a **cool CPI = relief
rally** in beaten-down tech. Don't fight that print — position around it, don't predict through it.

## Top 5 Picks for the Week

**1. Apple — AAPL — Long / Watch (binary catalyst: WWDC Monday, June 8)**
The clearest single-name catalyst of the week, and it lands first. Apple is expected to unveil an
AI-overhauled Siri (reportedly powered by a custom ~1.2T-parameter Google Gemini model, ~$1B/yr)
with possible multi-model choice and a paid tier. Bulls see an AI re-rating: **Morgan Stanley $330
(bull $440), Wedbush $400, BofA $380.** But the stock has already run, expectations are high, and
**UBS is skeptical ($296, Neutral)** — many features may ship in beta. As a defensive mega-cap,
AAPL is also a relative safe-haven if money keeps fleeing semis. **Long into a constructive Monday,
but size small — this is a "sell-the-news" risk if Siri underwhelms.**

**2. Oracle — ORCL — Watch into earnings (Wed June 10, after the close)**
The week's biggest AI read-through. Street wants ~**$1.96 EPS / ~$19.1B rev**, with cloud growth
guided **+46–50%** and a jaw-dropping **$553B RPO backlog (+325% y/y).** ORCL is up ~40% in three
months. The bull case is the backlog converting; the bear case is **AI capex blowing out** (Oracle is
raising up to $50B in debt/equity to build capacity). Reports the **same day as CPI** — double
event risk. **Watch, don't pre-position; let the print clear, then trade the reaction.**

**3. Nvidia — NVDA — Watch for an oversold bounce (do NOT catch the knife)**
Down ~6% Friday and the epicenter of the chip rout, but the underlying Blackwell demand story is
intact and banks kept nudging targets up on expanded H2 shipments. After a SOX −10% day, a sharp
**oversold relief bounce is likely** — but only buy **once it stabilizes**, ideally **after CPI
clears Wednesday.** Buying before the print is gambling on a coin flip. Highest-quality name to own
when the group finds a floor.

**4. SOXS — Long (tactical hedge / CPI-hot trade only)**
The 3x-inverse semiconductor ETF is the cleanest way to play **continued chip weakness or a hot
CPI.** Use it as an event hedge into Wednesday, not a position to marry. **Leveraged decay makes
this a 1–2 day instrument — hard stops, and flip off it fast if chips base.**

**5. Taylor Morrison Home — TMHC — Long toward the deal (lowest-drama setup)**
Popped **~22%** after agreeing to be acquired by **Berkshire Hathaway for $6.8B all-cash** — one of
the first big moves under Buffett successor Greg Abel; expected to close 2H 2026. This is an
**event-driven, market-direction-agnostic** trade: shares should grind toward the cash deal price
regardless of CPI or the chip rout. **Long for the arb spread; the main risk is deal/regulatory
hiccup, which looks low here.**
